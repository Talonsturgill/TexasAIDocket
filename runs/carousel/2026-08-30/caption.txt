Pflugerville checked who was searching its own cameras.

Records showed 459 outside organizations ran nearly 1.6 million searches touching its network over the last six months. The council voted unanimously to end its Flock agreement. All 28 cameras will be turned off and covered.

In 2023 the Legislature unanimously added $1 to auto insurance to fight catalytic converter theft. The Motor Vehicle Crime Prevention Authority put at least $30 million of it into cameras from El Paso to the Louisiana border. Its own report counts 62,000 plate searches in 2025 and about 1,660 cleared catalytic converter theft cases.

The Governor has ordered state agencies to pause the funding. His news index carries no release on it.

The authority's board meets October 13th, 2026.
