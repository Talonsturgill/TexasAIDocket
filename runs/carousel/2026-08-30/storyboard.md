# Storyboard — 2026-08-30 — the fee, the network, and who was reading it

## The synthesis

Three directors pitched. Two of them independently landed on a sectioned catalytic converter as
the deck's object, which is a genuinely good conceit and is exactly why it is not the one chosen.
Two of three arriving at the same picture means it was the available picture, and the reader who
has seen a cutaway converter reads "engine part" before they read anything else.

The register taken is the third one, **galvanized hardware standing in a Blackland Prairie right
of way at last dark**, for one measured reason. The artwork ledger's eleven decks measure at deck
median L* 27.7, 22.2, 15.6 and one breach at 86.7. **The mid band is unoccupied**, galvanized
steel is honestly a mid material, and `brand.yaml` caps light decks at one per eight runs, which
four runs since the breach does not yet allow.

Grafted from the other two, each with the reason it was taken:

- **"Bounded is walled, hedged is open."** An exact figure is drawn between two finished walls. A
  figure its own source hedges (*at least*, *estimated*, *about*, *nearly*) gets one wall and a far
  edge running off the frame uncapped. Six of this story's figures are hedged by their own source,
  and this makes the hedge visible as geometry rather than as a footnote. It is the numeral law
  turned into a drawing.
- **The cover, drawn.** c20 says the cameras "will be turned off and covered", and a cover is the
  one object in this whole story whose appearance the record actually describes. Drawing the cover
  and refusing to draw what is under it is both the honest move and the better picture.
- **An absence is a sentence naming a document, never a dark rectangle.** Deck no. 9 shipped
  unstamped fields as black rectangles and they read as redactions, which asserts something was
  removed when the record says nothing was ever stated.

**The one hazard fixed in synthesis.** The third director's own risk list flagged that putting 459
and 28 on one frame invites the reader to form a part-and-whole ratio, which is the arithmetic this
project forbids. They count different things. **They are on different frames.** Slide 1 carries the
28 a town controls. Slide 3 carries the 459 it discovered. Nothing in the deck draws them together.

## Deck laws, all nine frames

1. **NO LENS ANYWHERE.** Nine frames about a camera network and not one circular aperture, pole,
   plate or mount. This is what stops the deck becoming surveillance iconography, and it is also
   honest, because the record describes none of it.
2. **A COUNT IS DRAWN ONLY WHERE THE RECORD COUNTED IT.** Any quantity a frame's own geometry
   happens to produce (posts, ranks, contour lines) is never printed and the frame says so.
3. **NO TWO SETS TOUCH.** No line, bracket, containment or shared axis joins two counts drawn from
   two different sentences.
4. **EVERY COUNT NAMES THE SET IT COUNTED**, in mono, on the frame, in words the deck actually read.
5. **BOUNDED IS WALLED, HEDGED IS OPEN**, declared per frame.
6. **ONE LIGHT PER FRAME**, azimuth declared. Eleven decks running have lit from the side, so this
   deck's key sits high and slightly behind the subject on every frame that carries drawn light.
7. **$81 million is deliberately on no frame.** It is in c16's quote and available, and printing it
   beside $50.8 million invites a remainder this project may not publish. The omission is a
   decision, recorded so a later pass does not add it back as a helpful detail.

Planned value arc, 34, 22, 40, 58, 44, 71, 30, 47, 26. **Planned, and replaced in the ledger by a
measurement off the rendered PNGs.**
