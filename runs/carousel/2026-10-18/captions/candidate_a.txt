Drones found no screwworm. Texas counts 43 premises.

USDA's drones flew over Texas more than 2,000 times since June. They found no case among the more than 36,000 animals observed, KRGV reported. The Texas Animal Health Commission counts 43 premises in 17 counties.

USDA's research service has AI models that already pick areas for surveillance, fly trapping and sterile fly releases.

Kim Lohmeyer directs its laboratory in Kerrville and says the models can't predict every scenario, especially people moving infested animals.

Never move an animal that shows signs. Report a suspected case within 24 hours to a commission region office or the veterinarian on call at 1-800-550-8242.

The deck runs from a fly on a wire to a shut gate.
