Drones found no screwworm. 43 Texas premises had it.

USDA's drones flew over Texas more than 2,000 times since June. They found no case among the more than 36,000 animals observed, KRGV reported. That finding covers only those animals. The Texas Animal Health Commission counts 43 premises in 17 counties with a confirmed detection.

USDA's research service has AI models that already pick areas for surveillance, fly trapping and sterile fly releases.

Kim Lohmeyer directs its laboratory in Kerrville. Lohmeyer says the models can't predict every scenario, especially people moving infested animals.

Never move an animal that shows signs of infestation. Report a suspected case to the commission within 24 hours at 1-800-550-8242. Who on the place has that number?
