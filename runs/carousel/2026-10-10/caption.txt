Austin Community College calls its coming AI policy its "first-ever."

Its October 9th post says a framework called TRUST is meant to help shape it. The third rule reads "AI can support your work, but it should never replace your expertise, decision-making, or responsibility." The college says more than 79,000 students rely on it each year.

The Faculty and Student AI Town Hall on October 12th at the Rio Grande Campus will explore in-progress AI policies. The October 22nd seminar at the Highland Campus is open to community members.

The college's AI Resource Hub already links a PDF titled Acceptable Use of Artificial Intelligence Policy, marked for faculty and staff only.

The post names no adoption date, no vote and no adopting body. Who decides whether the new policy replaces that document or sits beside it?
