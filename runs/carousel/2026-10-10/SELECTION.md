- **It is AI in use, written down by the people who use it.** A college that teaches tens of
  thousands of Texans is setting the rules for how its students and faculty use AI in their work,
  and the five rules are concrete acts (say where it was used, check what it made, keep your own
  judgment, keep the work honest, respect the author). That is the "who is now doing what
  differently" question the deck exists for, and it is a classroom rather than a clinic.
- **It has a next step a reader can take this week.** The town hall is Monday evening, and the
  seminar on the 22nd is open to community members. `texan_check` rewards a closing frame with a
