The town hall is Monday, October 12th.

Austin Community College's Faculty and Student AI Town Hall runs 6:30 to 9 p.m. at the Rio Grande Campus. The college is developing what it calls its "first-ever" AI policy. The town hall will explore in-progress AI policies, it says.

The deck follows one drawn desk through October.

The policy starts from TRUST, a framework from two collegewide AI committees. Its five rules ask people to say where AI was used and to check what it produces. None of them bans AI.

The post names no adoption date, no vote and no body that adopts the policy. A form on the college's generative AI page takes feedback too.

The college's AI site already links a document titled Acceptable Use of Artificial Intelligence Policy. Does the "first-ever" policy replace it or sit beside it?
