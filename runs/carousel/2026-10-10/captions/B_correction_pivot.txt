Austin Community College calls its coming AI policy its "first-ever."

The college posted the plan on October 9th. A framework it calls TRUST is meant to shape it. Its third rule reads "AI can support your work, but it should never replace your expertise, decision-making, or responsibility."

A town hall on October 12th at the Rio Grande Campus will explore in-progress AI policies. It runs 6:30 to 9 p.m. Community members are invited. More than 79,000 students rely on the college each year.

The college's own AI Resource Hub already links a faculty and staff document titled Acceptable Use of Artificial Intelligence Policy.

The deck sets that title on a drawn laptop beside a blank sheet.

The post names no adoption date, no vote and no adopting body. Who signs off, and what becomes of the document already on the hub?
