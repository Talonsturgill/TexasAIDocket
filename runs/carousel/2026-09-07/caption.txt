A successor is not a handover.

The Texas Advanced Computing Center is switching Frontera off. Its own guide says the queues close permanently on October 1st, 2026. The same page calls it the largest cluster dedicated to open science in the United States. The guide's one promise for afterwards is that the file systems stay reachable to move data off. It never names a machine the work goes to.

The same center leads the construction project behind Horizon. That machine's own guide begins "This user guide is in progress". A dated notice says the machine is still limited only to internal users. Its construction award runs to April 30th, 2028. One of the three ways in is the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource Pilot.

A manual is how a public machine is open. Nine frames read both guides side by side.

Where does the work go after October 1st, 2026?
