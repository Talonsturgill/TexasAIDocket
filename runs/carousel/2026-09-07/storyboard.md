# STORYBOARD — carousel no. 17, 2026-09-07

## THE SYNTHESIS, and what each director contributed

Three treatments came back and all three were buildable. The spine taken is **TWO MANUALS, ONE
FINISHED AND ONE UNFINISHED**, because it is the truest reading of the record. Everything in this
story is carried by two documents belonging to one organisation and sitting in two completely
different states of repair, and a manual is how a public machine is actually open.

Grafted onto it, from the other two rooms:

- **From the QUEUE room, the light.** A plane source UNDER the raised floor, up through 25 percent
  open perforated tile. Illuminance falls with height, so every vertical surface is brightest at
  its foot and black by knee height, and **every cast runs UP**. Nothing in sixteen decks lights
  this way. It is what gives the two-manuals deck a real value structure instead of nine frames of
  aqua on black, which is the risk that room named against itself.
- **From the QUEUE room, no vanishing point anywhere.** Nine orthographic, plan, elevation, section
  or axonometric constructions. This deck is about ORDER and STATE, and perspective converts
  position into remoteness, which is the wrong claim.
- **From the MACHINE room, the attribution discipline.** A colour means one thing and is counted
  by the frame's own script rather than asserted in prose.
- **From the MACHINE room, its warning**, which is that nine close-ups of grey hardware collapse to
  one picture at feed size. Answered here by nine different camera classes and by capping the
  cavity idiom at two frames.

**Why the light and the emission never confuse each other.** They are two species with two
behaviours. The plenum light falls off with height and never reaches white. Emission does not fall
off at all and its edges are hard. A reader does not need that explained to feel it.

## STRUCTURAL LAWS — every frame obeys all five

1. **NO VANISHING POINT ANYWHERE.** Nine constructions, every one orthographic, plan, elevation,
   section or axonometric. No horizon, no sky, no convergence.
2. **TWO LIGHT SPECIES AND ONLY TWO.** The plenum uplight is a plane source below the frame whose
   falloff with height is computed rather than painted, capped at `plenum` L\* 74 so it never
   reaches white. Emission does not fall off, and its edge falloff is capped at 2 CSS px. Every
   cast shadow runs UP. **No lamp, no key, no pool, no cone, no rake appears in any frame.**
3. **A DOCUMENT IS LIT EXACTLY AS FAR AS IT IS WRITTEN.** `om4` where the source states it,
   `om4_dim` where it does not. One hue at one intensity, no third step, no ramp, no fade. The two
   manuals are drawn from one construction and differ only in EXTENT, never in colour. The
   unfinished guide is never warned about, it is simply less lit. This is the grid watch's own bar
   law applied to a document, and it is what keeps a severity verdict out of the deck.
4. **A BLANK IS A BODY AND A HOLE IS A BODY. NOTHING IS A BLACK RECTANGLE.** Every cavity gets a
   lit lip, a drawn side wall and a floor. **The cavity idiom is allowed on frames 4 and 5 and
   nowhere else.** Frames 7 and 9 draw not-yet-ness as a positive body instead. If a repair round
   wants a cavity on a sixth frame, that is the tell, and the correct answer is to refuse the
   repair.
5. **NO NUMERAL except inside a quote, a claim id, a date the record carries, or an award number
   the record carries.** No count is drawn, no depth is implied, no total is printed. Every numeral
   is JetBrains Mono with tabular figures and never Fraunces, because FIELD_NOTES records Fraunces
   closing a 3 into a 5 at feed size and this deck is made of dates.

**Type moves off the standard skeleton: frames 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9. Six of nine, planned here

rest**. The cover is the simplest frame in the deck on purpose.
