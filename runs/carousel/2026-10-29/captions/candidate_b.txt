Briscoe Wind's substation sits directly across from 397 acres.

On October 21st Soluna and Applied Digital agreed to form a joint company. It would develop a 50 MW AI data center near Silverton in Briscoe County.

That 50 MW is Phase 1 of Dorothy 3. Soluna describes Dorothy 3 as up to 300 MW and the Briscoe Wind Farm as 150 MW. It expects Phase 1 to draw from the wind farm and the grid.

Soluna says it was built "to bring load to the generation". It bought the 397 acres in August and says the substation across from them positions Dorothy 3 for behind the meter access.

Closing depends in part on approval of a tax abatement in Briscoe County. Soluna expects to close by year end. The county posts its Tax Abatement Guidelines and Criteria beside its Commissioners Court agendas. Will the abatement appear on one before then?
