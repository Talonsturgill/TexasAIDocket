50 MW is the deal. It isn't the plan.

Soluna and Applied Digital agreed on October 21st to form a company for a planned 50 MW AI data center near Silverton. Soluna describes the Dorothy 3 campus as up to 300 MW.

It sits beside Briscoe Wind, a 150 MW wind farm Soluna bought in April. Soluna says the wind farm's substation sits directly across from the land it bought for Dorothy 3. "We built Soluna to bring load to the generation," chief executive John Belizaire said.

Soluna expects the first 50 MW phase to draw power from Briscoe Wind and the grid.

Closing depends on conditions that include Briscoe County approving a tax abatement. Applied's ChronoScale has signed only a non-binding letter of intent to lease the facility.

Soluna expects closing by the end of the calendar year. Will Briscoe County decide on the abatement before then?
