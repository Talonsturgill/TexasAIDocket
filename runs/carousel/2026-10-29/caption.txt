Briscoe Wind's substation sits directly across from 397 acres.

Soluna announced on October 21st a deal to form a joint company with Applied Digital. It would develop a 50 MW AI data center near Silverton in Briscoe County.

That 50 MW is Phase 1 of Dorothy 3. Soluna expects Phase 1 to draw from Briscoe Wind and the grid. It describes Dorothy 3 as up to 300 MW.

Soluna bought the 397 acres in August. It says the substation positions Dorothy 3 for behind the meter access. It puts Briscoe Wind at 150 MW. Soluna's chief executive says Soluna was built "to bring load to the generation".

Closing depends in part on approval of a tax abatement in Briscoe County. Soluna expects to close by year end. The county posts its Tax Abatement Guidelines and Criteria beside its Commissioners Court agendas. Which public body decides the abatement, and when?
