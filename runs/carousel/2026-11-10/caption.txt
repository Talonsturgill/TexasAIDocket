A Frontera node held two Xeons.

Jose Rizo-Rey's lab at UT Southwestern Medical Center typically ran a 5.3 million atom simulation at 43 nanoseconds per day on 64 of those nodes. Frontera was retired in October after more than eight million jobs.

On Vista at UT Austin, eight GPU nodes gave Rizo-Rey "almost the same speed." Most Vista nodes run NVIDIA's GH200, a Grace CPU paired with a Hopper GPU.

His account names software and people. He says the speed "required a lot of optimizations with GROMACS, with the help of people here at TACC." One lab's account, not a benchmark.

Frontera's successor Horizon is in its first phase of early operations. Its power infrastructure is complete and its AI hardware is going in. Who will tune the code for the labs that run on it?
