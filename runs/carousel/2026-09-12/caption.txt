Who decides whether a crack counts?

A rater used to measure distress by hand on a sampled portion of the pavement section. That put a person in the traffic and the weather. Texas State University now reads that damage at pixel level from paired photos and depth maps. TxDOT funds the work and donated the van.

A score of 70 or above counts as good or better. Tracking that quality is how the agency finds roads needing repair. The team's own 2025 paper says a pavement engineer still has to verify what the machine returns.

Nine frames run from a rater on the fog line to the empty farm road the money is aimed at.

The release puts the project in a second phase and stops at closer to statewide deployment. It names three next steps and dates none. What would have to change before the score on a road you drive moves?
