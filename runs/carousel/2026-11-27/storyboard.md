# Carousel, November 27th, 2026: one sheet, one million

## The story, and what the fact check did to it

On November 18th Texas Comptroller Don Huffines announced proposed amendments to Rule 3.308 (c2, c4). They ran in the Texas Register on November 20th (c45), filed November 9th (c42). A data processing service would be one where the provider processes data the customer supplied and returns it organized (c22). Storage or manipulation alone would not count (c23), nor would a customer using a provider's computing on its own data (c24). Hosted technology services include cloud computing, artificial intelligence and quantum computing and are not taxable data processing (c25, c3). The Legislature taxed data processing in 1987 (c7). The comptroller's fiscal note estimates General Revenue losses from $1,947,310,000 in fiscal year 2027 to $4,286,040,000 in 2032 (c26 to c31) and local losses from $507,450,000 to $1,116,890,000 (c32 to c37), and finds an insignificant effect on the economy (c38). Comments go to Robin Robinson within 30 days of publication (c40, c41). Earliest adoption December 20th (c43). The fact check carried 45 claims and rejected any claim that the tax has ended, any independent cost estimate, and two Tribune quotes it couldn't read in full.

Docket item tx-2026-0255, statewide, state-policy.
