# Slide 08, THIRTY DAYS, three concepts

1. **The mailroom sorter as a month (PICKED).** A violet enamel steel pigeonhole letter sorter, six cubbies across and five down, exactly FIG.window_days of them, stands on the coral sweep under a raked coral key, each cubby with a blank cream label slip, and only the last cubby, bottom right and nearest the lens, carries the dusk gold slip with a folded comment letter in it. Only this beat has it because the grid is a month of mail slots, one per day of the comment window, and the deadline is the one slot left with a letter in it.

2. **The index card drawer (the plan).** A steel 3 by 5 drawer holding exactly 30 upright cards in blocks of five with a gold tab on the last card, close on the studio sweep. It counts the days, but November 25th shipped a card tray of tabbed cards two days ago, so a reader of the feed sees a repeat.

3. **A tear off day pad.** A desk block of 30 blank leaves on a steel binding bar, the top leaf curling back, the bottom leaf edged in gold, macro at desk height. A calendar with no digits reads as a pad of paper, and frame 7 already holds paper at macro, so it would share a camera and a medium with its neighbour.

**Why 1.** The 6 by 5 grid is countable at 432 px, it reads as a month without printing a digit, its mail slots say comments, and it shares no world, camera or medium with frame 7 (a macro page) or frame 9 (an envelope on a home desk).

**Type.** The sorter is wide and stands low, so the violet wall above it is the quiet. The headline sits high across the wall in two lines, the dek under it, and the sorter's top edge stays below the dek.
