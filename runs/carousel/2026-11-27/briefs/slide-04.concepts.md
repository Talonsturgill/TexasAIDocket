# Slide 4, THE NEW TERM, three concepts

1. THE DEFINITION, READ CLOSE (relief). The Register page as a TXBENCH material relief in plan, cropped close on a violet leather desk blotter over rose oak, a dusk gold wax paragraph and under it exactly three raised gold bullets, each followed by greeked words whose blob lengths follow the letter counts of the three named terms, a chrome binder clip on the top edge throwing the frame's longest cast. Only this beat is a definition with a list of three named things, so only this beat is a highlighted paragraph with three bullets under it.

2. THREE TABS (txthree, raked three quarter). A thick bound copy of the proposal standing half open on an oak table under a coral key, three dusk gold index tabs standing out of its fore edge at one section, the rest of the edge plain. The three tabs mark three named terms, which no other beat has, though a closed book reads as "a long document" before it reads as "three names".

3. THE LOUPE (macro, txthree). A brass and glass reading loupe standing on the page at a 30 cm macro, the lens magnifying the gold paragraph and its three bullets while the rest of the page falls out of focus. It says "read the words closely" strongly, but a magnified greeked page invites a reader to try to read it, and the glass is the hardest thing in the kit to render well.

PICK: 1. The page itself is the claim, the three bullets are the figure, and it is the only plan view relief between a wide spatial hall (frame 3) and table height stacks (frame 5), so medium and camera differ from both neighbours.

Composition: hook top left over the violet leather, two lines, dek four lines under it at width 840; the page enters at about y 540, tilted, bleeding right and bottom, its gold paragraph and bullets in the middle of the frame.
