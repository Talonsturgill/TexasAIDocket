# Slide 09 concepts (WRITE IN)

1. THE LETTER ON THE ESTIMATE. A walnut home desk at dusk by a rose window, seen from a seated eye just above the work: the fiscal 2032 stack of eight kraft reams and a loose block stands at its true 0.43 m height, and on its top rests an open #10 envelope with the reader's letter half out, one dusk gold line on it, a pen beside and a laptop glowing soft behind. Only this close could have it, because the deck's whole estimate has become the thing a reader writes on, and the one gold line is their letter quoting the proposal.

2. THE SLOT. A worm's eye macro from inside a dim hallway as a sealed envelope, gold line showing through its window, is pushed through a brass mail slot in a violet door, the fiscal 2032 ream stack standing on the hall table beside it. It answers "send it", but the slot is a door detail any mail story could use and the stack becomes a prop beside the act.

3. THE CALENDAR AND THE STAMP. An orthographic plan of a blotter where the letter, a stamp and a pocket diary open to the last day lie beside the top of the stack seen from straight above. It carries the date and the action, but a top down tray reads as a chart (charged lately) and repeats frame 8's day card.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only picture where the reader's own letter physically rests on the estimate it answers, so it could only have been made for "It is still only a proposal" and the write in beat, and it keeps the CAMERA_MOVE (from above at the desk) after frame 8's studio drawer, in a lit interior rather than a studio.

Type: the headline sits low left over the walnut desk front and the shaded foreground, three lines, with the dek under it, the stack and envelope standing to the right and rising into the rose window light.
