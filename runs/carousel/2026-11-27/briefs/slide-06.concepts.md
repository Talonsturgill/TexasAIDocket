# Slide 06 concepts, CITIES, COUNTIES, TRANSIT

1. AT THE LEDGE. Eye height at a curved walnut council dais in an empty chamber washed rose and violet, the camera close enough that two kraft and rose stacks on the transaction ledge (507 and 1,116 sheets, one per $1,000,000) are the hero while the blank brass nameplates and empty high backed chairs curve away behind them. Only this beat puts the estimate on the bench where a city council or a commissioners court sits, so the loss lands on the governments closest to home.

2. LAST BUS. An empty transit shelter at dusk under a violet sky, a bench seat in brushed steel with the two piles set side by side on it and the curb's long coral light across them. It is specific to transit, but it drops cities and counties, and an outdoor dusk is a mood the deck's frame 9 and frame 7 light already lean toward.

3. THE SQUARE FROM ABOVE. A county courthouse on its square seen from a drone at dusk, the two piles standing on the lawn at giant scale like monuments, kerbs and a bus stop at the corner. Strong scale play, but giant paper reads as a gag and the miniature buildings fight the one sheet one million rule's true size.

PICK: 1. Swap in any other headline and the dais with blank plates stops making sense; it is the one room where cities, counties and transit boards decide, and it differs from frame 5's oak table at table height by world (a public chamber), camera (raked along a curve) and scale (the room behind the paper).
