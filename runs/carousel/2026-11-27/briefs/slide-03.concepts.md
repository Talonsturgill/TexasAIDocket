# Slide 03, HOSTED, three concepts

1. **Inside the cold aisle.** One point perspective down a contained cold aisle, two facing runs of violet powder coated racks behind perforated doors and status lights, a frosted roof overhead, the far end door glowing coral. Only this beat names "a provider's computing", and this is that computing as a place the customer rents and never owns, the room the proposal would take out of the tax.

2. **The customer's drive in someone else's slot.** A macro at rack height of one empty server sled pulled out on its rails, a single drive carrier half seated in it, the rest of the rack's lights out of focus behind. It says "on its own data", the customer's data on the provider's machine, but at feed size a drive carrier reads as hardware detail rather than the rule's idea, and it tells the storage half only.

3. **The storage wall.** An orthographic elevation of a long tape and disk library, hundreds of identical cartridges in lit bays under a coral rake, nothing processed, only kept. It carries the second sentence ("storing data alone would not count") but loses the first, and a flat elevation repeats the grid look judges have charged lately.

**Pick: 1.** The headline is about using a provider's computing, and the aisle is the one picture where the reader stands inside it. The neighbours are an office counter (2) and a page relief in plan (4), so a wide one point interior shares neither world, camera nor medium.

Type: the headline centred high over the frosted roof, where the aisle's ceiling is one quiet plane between the converging rack tops. The dek centred low over the near floor tiles, which are kept solid and quiet. The coral door sits between them on the axis.
