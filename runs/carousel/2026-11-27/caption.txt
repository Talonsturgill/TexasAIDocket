Does Texas tax AI as data processing?

Comptroller Don Huffines says the Legislature never taxed AI. His proposed Rule 3.308 amendments would class cloud, AI and quantum computing as hosted technology services, outside the tax.

The release says it saves Texans money. His fiscal note has the state losing $1,947,310,000 in fiscal 2027 and $4,286,040,000 in 2032. It has local governments losing $507,450,000 in 2027 and $1,116,890,000 in 2032. The note calls the economic effect "insignificant".

Nothing is adopted yet. Comments to Robin Robinson must arrive by December 20th, the earliest adoption date.

The deck stacks the estimate, one sheet per million dollars.

Comptroller-elect Sarah Eckhardt takes over January 1st. She asked him to halt rule changes begun since August 1st. Does this one survive the handover?
