AI for runtime. Compression got the credit.

Permian Resources named two AI tools in its November 4th release. An artificial lift monitor is there to increase runtime. Large language models get its subsurface data to evaluate wellbore placement.

The measured column reads differently. Output was 457,528 barrels of oil equivalent a day. Record runtimes are credited to power and compression projects. The tools may well work. The release prints no result for either.

The company's 2025 annual report puts about 67% of its net leasehold in Texas and names Reeves County. On May 21st the Texas Tribune reported the Railroad Commission said it knew of no AI use by oil and gas companies. The release came 167 days later.

The deck follows one pump jack through one stroke.

How much of those record runtimes is the monitor's?
