AI for runtime. The record runtimes went to power and compression.

Permian Resources named two AI tools in its November 4th release. An artificial lift monitor is there to increase runtime. A second feeds its subsurface data to large language models.

Third quarter output was 457,528 barrels of oil equivalent a day. The release prints no measured result for either AI tool.

Its 2025 annual report put about 67% of its net leasehold in Texas. Neither filing says which wells the monitor watches. On May 21st the Texas Tribune reported the Railroad Commission said it was not aware of any use of AI by oil and gas companies. The same article quoted experts and analysts saying AI is reshaping how Permian operators handle produced water.

The Commission meets next on November 17th. How much of those record runtimes is the monitor's?
