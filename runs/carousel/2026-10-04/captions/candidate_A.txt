Voted down as a charter. Piloted in Fort Davis, Aldine and Houston.

The State Board of Education has the final say on charters. It voted the Alpha leaders' bid down 10 to 3.

In September 2025 Commissioner Mike Morath answered Alpha School associates and asked for a follow-up discussion.

That November TEA's Andrew Hodge emailed Joe Liemandt about supplemental pilots of Alpha's model.

By mid-June TEA staff praised progress on pilots with Fort Davis and Aldine.

On October 1st The Texas Tribune and ProPublica reported the emails. At least 10 districts were approached. At least five said no. The three pilots are supplemental learning, not Alpha's full two hour day. Fort Davis calls it an extra layer of intervention. TEA says it connected interested districts and left the choice to them.
