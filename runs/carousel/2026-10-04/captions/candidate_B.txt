A school bus with its stop arm out.

In this deck each bus is a district. Arm out means no.

At the State Board of Education the arm went out. The board has the final say on charters. The Tribune reports it voted the Alpha leaders' AI charter bid down 10 to 3. Members cited AI teaching and student screen time.

District by district it went another way. Emails show TEA staff connected Alpha-linked companies with district leaders. State officials and Alpha affiliates approached at least 10 districts. Ector County, Fort Bend, Irving, Pecos and Pflugerville pursued no agreement. Houston, Aldine and Fort Davis folded the arm and launched pilots for supplemental learning. TEA says it left the choice to each district.

Governor Greg Abbott's press secretary says any tool a district deploys must prove results for students. Who in your district decides what counts as proof?
