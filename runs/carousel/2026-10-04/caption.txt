Emails obtained by The Texas Tribune and ProPublica show it was about Alpha's program and meant to be shared with school districts. Staff connected Alpha-linked companies with district leaders.

The State Board of Education has the final say on charters. The Tribune reports it voted the Alpha leaders' AI charter bid down 10 to 3.

State officials and Alpha affiliates approached at least 10 districts. Ector County, Fort Bend, Irving, Pecos and Pflugerville pursued no agreement. Houston, Aldine and Fort Davis launched pilots. All three say they will use the software for supplemental learning. TEA says it left the choice to each district.
