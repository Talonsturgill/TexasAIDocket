Disaster City was built to collapse.

TEEX's 52 acre mock community in College Station even has a derailed train. From November 5th to November 13th it hosted DARPA's Triage Challenge finals. Drones and ground robots worked beside first responders, relaying data on simulated casualties to human medics. RoboScout won the Systems competition and $1.5 million. Disruptors won the Data competition and $1 million.

DARPA Director Stephen Winchell said human machine teams outperform every approach to the problem. Then he said, "But that's not enough, right?" Some teams did well identifying victims. Others did well at triage or evacuation.

DARPA's SBIR topic TRIAGE-X seeks systems that recognize chemical toxidromes and physically deliver the antidote or a life saving intervention. It is open until November 25th. Who in Texas answers it by then?
