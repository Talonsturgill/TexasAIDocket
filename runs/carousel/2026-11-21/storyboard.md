# Carousel, November 21st, 2026: the handoff, robots that find and medics who treat at Disaster City

## The story, and what the fact check did to it

TEEX staged the finals of DARPA's Triage Challenge at Disaster City in College Station from November 5th to November 13th (c1), a 52 acre mock community with full scale structures that can collapse, rubble piles and a derailed train (c11). Autonomous drones and ground robots worked alongside first responders, triaging simulated mass casualties and relaying critical data to human medics (c2). Systems had to locate casualties, identify signs of injury and send the data (c10), reading vital signs from a distance (c18). The five competitions included a tornado through a market, smoke filled buildings, night searches and rescue under rubble (c14). On the night course a robot searched during a controlled explosion while role players took cover (c27). More than 100 participants competed for more than $3 million (c12). RoboScout won the Systems competition and $1.5 million and Disruptors the Data competition and $1 million (c3). DARPA's director said human machine teams outperform every approach, then that it is not enough (c5, c6). TRIAGE-X is open until November 25th (c19, c20). The fact check carried 31 claims, corrected six claim texts, and rejected any statement that the systems are AI or that no real patient was involved.

Docket item tx-2026-0254, Brazos County, defense-and-federal.

## Why this treatment, and what was grafted

Base: THE HANDOFF (the machine and medic lens). The story's own measure of success is the moment a machine hands a medic what it found, so one lime link carries that handoff through the deck, working, crossing smoke, split and then open toward the next job. Grafted from A TOWN BUILT TO FALL: Disaster City as a measured place (frame 2) and the rubble section where the machine reads someone it can't reach (frame 4). Grafted from BRASS AND THE CLOCK: money as machined brass plates at one scale (frame 7), the credential pegboard (frame 6), and the rule that every more than figure is drawn to its floor and runs off the frame. Declined: the moulage tray macro and the father and daughter team, because people at close range are this deck's named risk, and the exploded mast, folded into frame 4.
