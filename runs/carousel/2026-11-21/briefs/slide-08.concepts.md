# Slide 08, NOT ENOUGH, three concepts

1. **The year rail in the studio (the plan).** Two half assemblies, the rover's finding head and an orange triage cradle, stand on a brushed aluminium year rail on the win sweep, each sending a lime link that stops short of the other. Only this beat has a winning result drawn as two halves that do not yet meet, with the years since 2023 ticked under them.

2. **The test track in the night workshop.** A twin aluminium test track runs from the near left of a deep blue block walled workshop off the right edge, the teal finding rover parked on it at the first year tie and an orange evacuation cradle on its own bogie further down, each throwing a lime link toward the other that stops in mid air, the ties ending at the finals while the rails run on. Only this beat says better than every approach and still not enough at once: the two best halves are on one track, and the handoff between them and the track ahead are both unfinished.

3. **Blue hour on the pile.** A low camera on the rubble at Disaster City after the finals, the rover's lime link arcing out over the slabs toward a parked medic's litter and stopping a hand's width short, a deep teal and violet sky behind. Only this beat has the reach that falls short, though it is the same outdoor world as frames 4 and 9.

**Pick: 2.** It keeps the plan's idea (two halves whose links don't meet, years since 2023 counted, the rail running on), which is the only picture that carries c5, c6 and c7 together, and moves it off the studio sweep that frame 7 stands on and off the outdoor late light of frame 9, so it is unlike both neighbours in world and light.

**Type.** Headline top right, right aligned, three lines over the quiet blue wall, beside the rover's mast rising at the left. Dek low on the dark concrete floor under the track, full width, so the middle band belongs to the two machines and the break between their links.
