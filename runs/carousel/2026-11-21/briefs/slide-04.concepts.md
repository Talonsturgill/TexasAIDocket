# Slide 04, THE JOB, three concepts

A. THE SECTION. A long lens, near level elevation of a collapse cut clean on one plane, pancaked slabs, a CMU wall with its cores, a leaning slab over a shored lean-to void with a role player sitting clothed and unhurt against the wall, Brazos clay strata below grade, the teal rover on the top slab with its mast tipped down. A pale reading cone passes straight through the concrete to the person and one lime link climbs from the rover's radio out of the frame, so find, read and tell are three lines in one picture.
Why only this beat: the sentence is three verbs about a person the machine can't reach, and only a cut through solid rubble can show the machine, the hidden person and the line out to the medic at once.

B. THE READOUT BENCH. A studio macro of the rover's sensor head exploded on its guide rods (lidar, stereo pair, standoff lens, radio), each stage lit in turn, with the lime link leaving only the radio stage. Why only this beat: the head's four stages are literally locate, signs, vitals and send, the job broken into parts.

C. THE MEDIC'S END. Over the shoulder of a medic, from behind at a tent edge, the lime link arriving from the rubble field into a rugged tablet while the rover is a small teal shape on a distant pile. Why only this beat: "tell a medic" seen from the receiving end.

PICK: A. It is the only one where the reader sees why the machine is needed (the person is out of reach inside solid concrete) and all three verbs at once. B is a studio object like frames 7 and 8, C repeats frame 1's handoff and frame 5's link across a field.

Type: headline in two lines top left in the clear sky band, dek under it in a column at left, the rover and link kept to the right so the sky on the left is quiet.
