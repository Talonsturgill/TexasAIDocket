# Slide 03, FIVE COURSES, three concepts

**A. The briefing table of five maquettes.** Five model sets on rising plinths march away down a long walnut table in a blue plaster briefing room, low window sun raking in from the left: a market under a gauze tornado funnel with one canopy torn loose, a two storey block coughing cotton smoke, a black felt night box with one pinpoint lamp, a rubble mound, and a fifth set under a drape. Only this beat lays the five competitions side by side at one glance, as the set models an organiser would brief from, so the range is the picture.

**B. The hangar of five bays.** A long training shed seen down its length at eye level, five open bays each holding one condition (a wind fan and torn tarp, a bay filled with smoke, a blacked out bay, a rubble bay, a closed door), the teal rover small at the threshold. It says five conditions, but it borrows the rover and the life size scale the next frame needs, and five bays down a corridor read as a building before they read as courses.

**C. The course tiles.** Five raised material tiles on a plinth wall, each a relief of one condition (wind combed sand, smoke grey felt, a black tile with one lamp, broken concrete, a blank tile), lit from the chassis key. Plan view risks the top down tray that judges read as a chart, and the relief bench locks the camera.

**Pick: A.** The maquettes are the only picture where five whole disasters fit in one frame at once and each still reads as its own staged place, and it is unlike both neighbours (frame 2 an outdoor raked aerial of the real town, frame 4 a life size section under sky) in world, scale and light.

**Type.** A right column over the shaded blue back wall and the open walnut on the right half of the table, the hook in three lines, the dek under it. The plinths climb the left half from a cropped near plinth at the bottom left, so the right half is quiet wood and wall. Pills sit on the plinths, left of the column.
