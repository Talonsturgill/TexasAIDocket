# Slide 09 concepts, OPEN UNTIL NOVEMBER 25TH

1. THE EMPTY POCKET (outdoor, close, late light). An open rugged hard case on a dropped pickup tailgate in long warm light, its grey foam cut with one empty cylindrical pocket, a lime link arcing up from the parked triage rover's radio at the frame's edge and ending in the empty pocket, a ring of engraved day ticks on the open lid. Only this beat has it because the next ask is delivery: the data already reaches the medic, the dose does not, and the empty place where the dose would ride is the ask itself.

2. THE CARGO BAY (studio, worm's eye, medium). The triage rover seen from the floor of a butter sweep with its rear deck hatch swung open on an empty machined cradle, the lime link from its own radio folding back into the cradle. Only this beat could have it because TRIAGE-X asks the finder to become the deliverer, but it reuses the studio and the rover-hero framing of frames 7 and 8.

3. THE DOOR ON THE CALENDAR (relief, plan). A raised clay relief of days as tiles from the finals' last day to the close, the run of tiles stepping up to a single open slot at the end with the lime link dropping into it. It carries the date cleanly, but it is a chart in plan, the top down tray judges charged lately, and it says when without saying what the ask is.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only picture where the headline's one noun, delivery, is visible as an absence: a case packed for a dose with the dose missing and the machine's lime link pointing at the hole. Unlike frame 8 (cool studio, aluminium rail, close from the left), it is outdoors in late gold on a truck, so world, light and medium all turn.

Type: the headline and dek sit low, over the shadowed ground and bumper beneath the dropped tailgate, where the truck's own shadow makes a deep teal floor; the case, the lid and the oak own the upper two thirds.
