# Slide 01 concepts, Disaster City, "The robot finds. The medic treats."

A. THE HANDOFF ON THE SLABS (outdoor, v2win morning, low close camera). A teal tracked triage rover stands close and large on broken concrete slabs at knee height, its sensor head turned out of frame left toward someone it has found, while a medic ten metres beyond, seen from behind, holds a rugged tablet and one lime link arcs from the rover's radio down onto that tablet. Only this sentence has both halves in one picture, the machine looking one way to find and handing the other way to the person who treats.

B. OVER THE MEDIC'S SHOULDER (outdoor, high back three quarter, tablet scale). From just behind a medic's shoulder the rugged tablet fills the lower third, its lime received mark lit, while far up the rubble pile the rover is a small teal shape with the link coming down off it. It puts the reader in the treating role, but the person is close enough to read as a kit mannequin, the hands carry the frame and the machine shrinks to a dot.

C. FROM INSIDE THE RUBBLE (worm's eye through a crack in a collapsed slab, sky beyond). A slot of win sky framed by broken concrete and rebar, the rover's head leaning over the edge to look in, a lime thread rising out of the slot toward a medic standing on the rim. Strong for the "find" half and for frame 4's job, but it draws the casualty's viewpoint, which leans on a person under rubble and steals frame 4's section.
