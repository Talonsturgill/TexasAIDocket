# Slide 05, NIGHT COURSE, three concepts

A. THE LANE AT NIGHT (outdoor night world, low WIDE camera, 40 mm). The triage rover fills the lower right in rear three quarter silhouette on a rutted dirt lane, its mast cut out against a controlled burst blooming orange through teal and violet smoke beyond a line of earth filled barrier units, two helmets ducked at one end and a medic hunched behind the near unit at left with one lime link running to her from the mast. Only this beat has a machine upright and working while every person in the frame is folded down behind cover, which is the headline's tension in one look.

B. THE BARRIER IN SECTION (cutaway relief, raked orthographic). A single earth filled barrier unit cut open like a core sample, role players crouched in its lee on one side and the rover's lens rising past its rim on the other, the blast reduced to a warm wash on the far face. Only this beat is about cover, but a section repeats frame 4's sectioned pile, its neighbour, so it fails point 5.

C. THE ROVER'S OWN VIEW (camera at the lens, looking out, 24 mm). The scene through the rover's head, smoke and the burst filling the frame, a lime ring on a figure in cover at the edge. It puts the reader in the machine, but it loses the machine itself and the people ducking around it, and a sensor view slides toward a UI look the judges have charged.

PICK: A. It is the only one where a stranger sees in one second a machine still standing and searching while the people duck, which is the sentence "It searched through the blast", and its outdoor night world differs from frame 4's sectioned noon pile and frame 6's orthographic tent board.

Type: bottom left, the headline in three lines over the dark lane and the shadowed near barrier, the dek under it in a 520 px column. The rover owns the lower right, the burst and smoke the upper middle, so the left low quadrant is kept quiet for the type.
