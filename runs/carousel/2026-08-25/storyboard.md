# STORYBOARD — Carousel No. 7, August 25th, 2026

## The synthesis, and why

Three lenses were pitched. **BEHIND GLASS wins the spine**, the wall-mounted glazed public notice
case bolted beside the door of every Texas courthouse and city hall, with a laser-printed agenda
clipped inside. A document you can read and cannot reach.

It wins because it is the only treatment whose central object is also what this run experienced.
The scout sent after Fort Worth's primary record spent fourteen minutes and got 403 from every
path on the city's host, including its robots.txt, and found the city's own agenda system holds
nothing after June 16th. A reader cannot read the resolution their council passed. The deck is
built out of that.

**Grafted from THE INSTRUMENT**, which pitched a municipal valve vault. Its metaphor was the
tighter fit to the thesis, a valve being the difference between a line that carries and one that
does not, and two things it brought are taken wholesale:

- **Nine distinct cameras and nine distinct construction methods, none repeated.** That treatment
  named the convergence risk in numbers, `bespoke_median 0.52` on 2026-08-22, the worst in the
  ledger, from a deck built on one object. Behind Glass stands seven frames on one case, so this
  discipline is the defence and it is written into every dossier below.
- **Its honesty catch, which was real.** It checked the shape labels against `claims.json` rather
  than against item titles and found three that no claim stated. That is fixed at the source in
  `compute.py`, every shape now cites the claim id that proves it, and the count moved from seven
  to eight because two instruments that had been merged are genuinely different.

**Rejected from both:** cartography. The record is the only place these ten decisions sit side by
side, and a map would put them in Texas, which is the one thing the reader already knows.

## The physical law of this deck

**Nothing is carved, embossed, milled or engraved. Every document is dead flat.** All depth comes
from the AIR GAP between the glazing and the sheet. 2026-08-19 cut into limestone, 2026-08-22
milled recesses, 2026-08-20 pressed ink into fiber, every one of them under a raking key. This
deck takes its dimension from light passing THROUGH a plane in front of a flat sheet. There is no
raking key anywhere in it.

**It is a MID VALUE deck**, ground at L* 62 to 68. Five shipped decks are dark register, one is a
flat daylight sheet, one is paper. None is mid. Light and dark are EVENTS inside a frame here
rather than the frame's setting, so every frame must carry `case_dark` and `bond` as real areas.

## Structural laws for the ledger

Legible means quoted or computed. Everything below reading size is decorative and aria-hidden.
Toner and never ink, a hard edge with a faint scatter halo, never a wick. No invented item number,
party name or building. No frame prints a year on February 16th unless a claim carries one.
No cartography and no coordinate. The palette is craft and never evidence, so no frame names a soil.
