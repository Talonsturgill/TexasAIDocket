A data center abatement can die without ever being voted on.

Four died in Brazoria County on March 10th, when the commissioners court refused the reinvestment zone they rode on. The minute on each reads "No action taken due to I.3 failed".

That was the first of fifteen. Twelve Texas local governments acted on a data center this year and five of those actions were a resolution.

Five of the fifteen stop nothing. Archer County's unanimous denial "does not stop the project from moving forward". A Lubbock County commissioner was plainer. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding."

Six changed a legal state on the day. San Angelo wrote three of them. Zoning in May, the sewer in June, the water two weeks after that.

Fort Worth must hold two public hearings before its pause can take effect. Is a resolution that binds nothing worth passing?
