A data center abatement can die without ever being voted on.

Four died in Brazoria County on March 10th, when the commissioners court refused the reinvestment zone they were riding on. The minute on each reads "No action taken due to I.3 failed".

That was the first of seven. Between March 10th and August 11th seven Texas governmental bodies acted against a data center, and no two reached for the same instrument.

Five of the seven stop nothing, and their own records say so. Archer County's unanimous denial "does not stop the project from moving forward". A Lubbock County commissioner put it plainer. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding."

Two of the seven bind. Brazoria County refused the zone. San Angelo capped data center water, 7 to 0.

Fort Worth holds the first of two required public hearings no later than November 10th. Is a resolution that binds nothing worth passing?
