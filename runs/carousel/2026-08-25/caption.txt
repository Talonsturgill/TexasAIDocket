A data center abatement can die without ever being voted on.

Four died in Brazoria County on March 10th, when the commissioners court refused the reinvestment zone they rode on. The minute on each reads "No action taken due to I.3 failed".

That was the first of seven. By August 11th seven Texas governmental bodies had reached for an instrument, no two the same.

Five of the seven stop nothing. Archer County's unanimous denial "does not stop the project from moving forward". A Lubbock County commissioner was plainer. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding."

Two of the seven bind. Brazoria refused the zone. San Angelo put a number in its water code. Cooling fill "shall not exceed 8.0 gallons per square foot".

Fort Worth holds the first of two required hearings no later than November 10th. Is a resolution that binds nothing worth passing?
