A data center abatement can die without ever being voted on.

Brazoria County refused the reinvestment zone under four of them on March 10th. The minute on each of the four reads "No action taken due to I.3 failed". They died on a vote about none of them.

That was the first of eight. Between March 10th and August 11th eight Texas governmental bodies restricted a data center. No two reached for the same instrument.

Two of the eight say in their own sources that they bind nothing. Archer County's unanimous denial "does not stop the project from moving forward". A Lubbock County commissioner put it plainer. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding."

San Angelo's ordinance does bind. It capped data center water, 7 to 0. On the other four the record says neither.

Is a resolution that binds nothing worth passing? Fort Worth holds the first of two required public hearings no later than November 10th. The record is at texasaidocket.com.
