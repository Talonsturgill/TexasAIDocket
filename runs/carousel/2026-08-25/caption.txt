A data center abatement can die without ever being voted on.

Four died in Brazoria County on March 10th, when the commissioners court refused the zone they rode on. The minute on each reads "No action taken due to I.3 failed".

That was the first of fifteen. Twelve Texas local governments acted on a data center this year.

Six took effect that day. San Angelo wrote three of them. It allows a data center by conditional use and caps what one may draw.

Two of the fifteen were approvals. Wichita Falls cleared one and Williamson County approved an abatement.

Fort Worth holds the first of two required public hearings not later than November 10th.

Five of the fifteen stop nothing. A Lubbock County commissioner put it plainly. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding." Is a resolution that binds nothing worth passing?
