A data center abatement can die without ever being voted on.

Four abatement applications died in Brazoria County on March 10th, when the commissioners court refused the zone. The minute on each reads "No action taken due to I.3 failed".

That was the first of fifteen, by twelve Texas local governments. The record splits them evenly.

Five took effect. San Angelo wrote three, allowing a data center only by conditional use and capping the water that fills its cooling loop.

On five no source says either way. Two of the five were approvals, in Wichita Falls and Williamson County.

Fort Worth holds the first of two required public hearings not later than November 10th.

Five stop nothing. A Lubbock County commissioner was plain. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding." Is a resolution that binds nothing worth passing?
