Four abatements never got a vote. The item they all stood on failed first.

That was March 10th in Brazoria County, first of fifteen actions by twelve Texas governments. Nine frames, all fifteen in order, sorted by whether the instrument binds.

Five took effect. San Angelo wrote three, allowing a data center only by conditional use and capping its cooling fill.

Five reached for a resolution. Not one is among the five that took effect.

On five more the record carries no date in force. Two were approvals, Wichita Falls and Williamson County.

Of eight items with a step still ahead, the two on a data center both sit with a state body. The nearer closes September 4th.

A Lubbock County commissioner was plain. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding." Is one worth passing?
