A data center abatement can die without ever being voted on.

Four died in Brazoria County on March 10th, when the commissioners court refused the reinvestment zone they rode on. The minute on each reads "No action taken due to I.3 failed".

That was the first of fourteen. Eleven Texas local governments acted on a data center this year and no two reached for the same instrument.

Five of the fourteen stop nothing. Archer County's unanimous denial "does not stop the project from moving forward". A Lubbock County commissioner was plainer. "Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding."

Six changed a legal state on the day. San Angelo wrote three of them. Zoning in May, the sewer in June, the water a fortnight later.

Fort Worth holds the first of two required hearings no later than November 10th. Is a resolution that binds nothing worth passing?
