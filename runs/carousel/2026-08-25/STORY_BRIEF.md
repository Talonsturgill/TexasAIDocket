# STORY BRIEF — Carousel No. 7, August 25th, 2026

## The story

In 2026 Texas local governments started refusing data centers, and **they found seven different
ways to do it**. Most of those ways do not stop anything.

This is a story only this record can tell, because it is not one meeting. It is the shape of ten
separate decisions read together, and the record is the only place they sit side by side.

## The turn, and it is the whole point

**Most of these refusals do not bind.** The record says so in the sources' own words.

- Archer County denied the abatement unanimously with more than 30 residents in the room, and
  the coverage states plainly that it **"does not stop the project from moving forward"**.
- Lubbock County passed a disclosure resolution four to one, and the same report says
  **"Resolutions do nothing, they are not binding."**
- Killeen's commission refused the permit against a staff recommendation to approve, and
  **the city council makes the final decision**, using that vote as a recommendation.
- Fort Worth's pause **is not in force**. Under the city timeline it takes effect February 16th,
  after hearings and further votes.

**Two of the seven have teeth.**

- **Brazoria County** refused to create the reinvestment zone. Four abatement applications
  depended on it and the minute for each of them reads `No action taken due to I.3 failed`.
  Four applications died on one vote and none of them was voted on.
- **San Angelo** capped data center cooling water in the city ordinance, approved 7 to 0.

## The counter-image, on its own frame

Two bodies considered a restriction and declined. Tom Green County's commissioner
**referenced a $100 million lawsuit filed against Hill County** after Hill County approved a
similar one year moratorium. That is a reason to say no to saying no, and it is reported speech
about a lawsuit rather than a finding about one, so the frame must attribute it that way.

Laredo declined a moratorium and its direction to write permanent rules has reached three
agendas whose action history reads **`no action taken`**.

## What was refused entry

The **Killeen vote count**. The record carries two journalism sources for it, one saying 4-1 and
one saying 4-0, and neither is a minute. No vote count from Killeen ships. The frame uses only
what both agree on.
