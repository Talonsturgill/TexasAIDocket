Texas's large feedlots held more cattle than Nebraska's or Kansas's

On September 1st USDA estimated 2.5 million cattle on feed in Texas's large feedlots, 2.47 million in Nebraska and 2.32 million in Kansas. Texas held 22 percent of the 11.16 million nationwide. Each steer is drawn to stand for 500 thousand head.
