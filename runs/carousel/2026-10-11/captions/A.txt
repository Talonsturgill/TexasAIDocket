West Texas A&M's $9.75 million USDA grant is half the money.

The A&M System's October 7th release calls it WT's largest federal award ever. A required match adds $4.875 million from the System and $4.875 million from other sources. The total is $19.5 million.

The feedlot goes up next to WT's Nance Ranch east of Canyon. It pairs 90 small research pens with 45 commercial-scale ones. On September 1st USDA counted 2.5 million cattle on feed in Texas's large feedlots. Nebraska and Kansas had fewer.

Everything above comes counted except the AI. The release gives it one sentence, which says new techniques "can be evaluated with traditional and artificial intelligence-powered approaches". The release names no AI tool, vendor, camera, model or result.

The record holds the counts and one sentence. Which AI approach would these pens test?
