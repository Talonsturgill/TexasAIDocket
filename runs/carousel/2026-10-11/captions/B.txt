USDA is paying for half of a research feedlot near Canyon.

The A&M System calls the $9.75 million grant to West Texas A&M the largest federal award in WT's history. A dollar-for-dollar match from the System, the Texas Cattle Feeders Association, donors and other sources brings the project to $19.5 million.

The feedlot will pair 90 small research pens with 45 commercial-scale pens. Ground was broken in November 2025 next to WT's Nance Ranch.

The release mentions AI once. New techniques "can be evaluated" with traditional and AI-powered approaches. No tool or vendor is named. Neither is a camera, a model or a result.

On September 1st USDA counted 2.5 million cattle on feed in Texas's large feedlots. Nebraska and Kansas each had fewer.

If AI is tested in these pens, will WT's Feedlot Research Group name the tool and report the result?
