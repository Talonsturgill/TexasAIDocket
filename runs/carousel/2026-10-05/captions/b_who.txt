The UT System's health care AI lab funded 12 pilots. One says what it has saved.

The lab is UT REAL Health AI. It launched in 2025 with legislative approval and dedicated funding. It chose them from 120 submissions.

A UT San Antonio pilot puts an AI assistant called Leah across seven UT hospital sites for emergency triage and transfer decisions. A Dell Medical School pilot will read clinical notes across more than 7 million patient records for signs of suicide risk.

The lab's page credits the no-show project with more than 6,500 appointments and $900,000 in revenue saved at UTHealth Houston. That is the project's own count. The page projects at least 15,000 a year at UT Health San Antonio. None of the other eleven gives a saved count.

The lab's symposium is in Austin on October 7th and 8th. Will any of the other eleven bring a count of its own?
