At UTHealth Houston, a no-show project's entry states what it saved.

The UT System's page credits it with saving more than 6,500 appointments and $900,000 in revenue. The page cites no audit behind it. The page projects at least 15,000 a year at UT Health San Antonio, where it is set to expand.

The project is one of 12 pilots UT REAL Health AI funded out of 120 systemwide submissions. A UT San Antonio pilot puts an AI assistant called Leah into emergency triage at seven UT hospital sites. UTMB Galveston is deploying referral triage. A Dell Medical School pilot will read clinical notes across more than 7 million patient records for signs of suicide risk.

Of the 12 entries, only the no-show entry states what it already saved.
