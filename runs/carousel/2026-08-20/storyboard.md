# Storyboard, August 20th, 2026. Deck No. 4.

Docket item `tx-2026-0048`. Claims file `out/2026-08-20/claims.json`, 19 verified, 6 rejected.

## THE SYNTHESIS, and why this one

Three treatments came back. The winner is **the press proof**, the ballot as a physical object at
working distance, and two things are grafted onto it from the instrument-durability treatment.

**What the press proof won on.** The central fact of this story is not a place, a quantity or a
building. It is a sentence printed on a card that disarms the card it is printed on. Printing has
its own vocabulary for the gap between what was intended and what the sheet actually says, so the
register argues rather than illustrates. It is also the furthest from all three shipped decks,
which put the camera in a place. This one puts it on an object.

**Grafted in, first.** The two grounds law. One city's side of the deck is the ballot stock and
the other's is a warm neutral black, and the two never mix on any frame. **The palette is craft
and never evidence.** The first panel stopped this deck for publishing a geological claim that
came out of this very paragraph, so the paragraph now says what the colours ARE rather than what
they are supposed to prove.

**Grafted in, second, and it is the sharpest thing any director wrote.** The turn at slide 5. The
durable instrument is the vaguer one. League City names automated license plate reader cameras at
fixed locations on public roadways. Pflugerville's recommended section names a category with an
open list. Durability trades against specificity, and neither treatment that lacked this had a
real reversal in it.

**Grafted in, third, and then CUT.** One cartographic frame was planned and drawn. Not one claim
this run fetched names a county, the committed gazetteer holds counties and metros but no city
records, and which county a city reaches into is a question this record did not ask anybody. The
frame was cut and **no county is named anywhere in this deck.** The cities are named, because
every claim names them.

**Dropped from the press proof.** Its slide 6 stood a sheet on edge against a charter stack in
`TX3D`. The same claim is carried by the duration-as-extent inversion at less render risk, and its
own director named the TX3D camera as this deck's likeliest way to lose a frame.

**Dropped from the drafting table.** Its use of `capitol_granite` for every mark in the deck. Two
warm reds on one contact strip means neither is a reservation. Every hand-added mark here is
graphite, which is what makes the one red on the closing frame mean something.

**A CORRECTION THIS RUN OWES ITS OWN DIRECTORS, recorded here and printed on no frame.** The showrunner's brief for the third lens said
the two cities are eleven miles of freeway apart. They are not. They sit in different metros at
opposite ends of the state's populated middle. No distance and no county is published anywhere in
this deck, because nothing this run fetched states either. The figure was invented in the brief, is in no claim, and would have failed
`numeral_lint` if a frame had carried it. A director caught it and refused it. **No distance
between the two cities appears anywhere in this deck**, and the argument runs on same day,
different ground, which is stronger.

## STRUCTURAL LAWS

1. **Every mark is either printed or added, never both.** Printed is `ink` or `dropout` and was
   laid by a press. Added is `graphite` and was laid by a hand. The closing frame's red is the one
   exception and that is why it carries weight.
   **A press artifact is a third thing and it is not type.** Slide 2's second impression of the
   opening clause is letterforms, and it is not a line anybody is asked to read. It is what a
   plate that missed register leaves behind. It is declared `aria-hidden` and `data-decorative`,
   it measures 1.47 to 1 against the stock, and the fourth panel was right that calling it
   dropout muddled two different things. Dropout is type the press left unprinted so the paper
   shows through. This is type the press printed twice.
2. **The two GROUNDS never mix, and lit paper is not a ground.** A frame's ground is `stock` or
   it is `houston_black`, never both. League City's frames stand on stock, Pflugerville's on
   houston_black. **AMENDED after the fourth panel**, which read the law as written against the
   frames that had already rendered and found it false on three of them: the charter's lit course
   on 3, the ballot card on 4 and the bounded figure on 5 are all at stock value on the dark
   ground. That is correct and the law was wrong. Lit paper reads at stock value wherever paper
   appears, because a sheet under a lamp is a sheet. What a ground says about whose frame it is,
   an object lying on it does not.
   **THE PALETTE IS CRAFT AND NEVER EVIDENCE.** Colours drawn from a region are a design
   decision. The moment a frame states the geology as fact it is publishing a claim, and this
   run shipped exactly that to the panel before catching it. No frame names a soil.
3. **An oval is never furniture.** No oval is a bullet, a divider or a watermark. It appears when
   a choice is being drawn, and it is empty or filled, never partly.
4. **The perforation never tears.** The stub stays attached on every frame it appears on, because
   the election has not happened.
5. **The stock is new.** No stains, no yellowing, no deckle, no wear. Distressed texture says
   vintage and says nothing about Texas, and the doctrine bans it by name.
6. **No numeral appears that is not in `claims.json`.** No distance between the cities, no count
   of future councils, no camera count beyond c4, no claim of a first except as Mayor Long's.
7. **Nothing is drawn from the Lago Vista report.** It is journalism with no primary source and no
   established date, so it appears once, named as unconfirmed, on slide 6 and nowhere else.
8. **Furniture does not get redesigned.** Kicker at 25px, counter and source line at 24px mono, on
   every frame, numbered `01 / 07` through `07 / 07`.
9. **One light.** 315 degrees on every frame that carries a relief or a contact shadow.
