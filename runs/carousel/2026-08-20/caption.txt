Does the November vote decide it?

No. The League City ballot question says so before it asks anything. It opens "As a nonbinding statement of voter preference", then asks whether the police department should keep its license plate reader cameras.

Council called the referendum six to one on August 11th for the November 3rd, 2026 ballot. League City police have installed 55 of them since December 2022. A preference is not an instruction.

Pflugerville ordered its own November 3rd election the same day. Its charter review commission recommends a new Section 2.03 on the city's use of artificial intelligence.

League City's council meets August 25th, 2026 in Council Chambers at 200 West Walker Street. Its agenda for that meeting names no camera item, and public comment there may be general in nature. Pflugerville has a Flock camera lease at $87,500 a year set for August 25th too, unvoted.
