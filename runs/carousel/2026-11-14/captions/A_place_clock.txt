A computing cluster for Lubbock, on paper.

In January the Texas Semiconductor Innovation Fund gave Texas Tech $12 million toward a clean room. The university matched it for a $24 million project. In May a team at the same college received roughly $4.5 million for semiconductors meant for harsh environments. On November 13th Texas Tech announced another award of over $19 million, this one for a computing cluster in Lubbock.

The project will combine AI with simulation data and lab results to shorten development cycles for new semiconductor devices. Dean Roland Faller says Texas Tech will be one of the first universities with the newest generation of Nvidia chips to power it.

The cluster is not built yet. The announcement gives no install or completion date.

The deck draws each award as wafers, one for every $1 million. Sources are in the record at texasaidocket.com.
