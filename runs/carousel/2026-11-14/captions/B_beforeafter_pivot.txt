January's award went to a clean room. November's went to an AI cluster.

In January the Texas Semiconductor Innovation Fund gave Texas Tech $12 million toward a clean room. The university matched it for a $24 million project. In May the same engineering college got roughly $4.5 million for bandgap research.

On November 13th Texas Tech announced over $19 million more to help set up a computing cluster in Lubbock. It would pair AI with simulation and lab data. The dean says Texas Tech will be among the first universities with access to the newest Nvidia chips.

The cluster isn't built yet. The announcement names no site, no size and no install date.

The deck counts each award in wafers, drawn to illustrate. The record is at texasaidocket.com.
