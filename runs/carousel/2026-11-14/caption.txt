Lubbock has a computing cluster on paper.

In January the Texas Semiconductor Innovation Fund gave Texas Tech $12 million toward a clean room. The university will match it for a $24 million project. In May a Texas Tech engineering team received roughly $4.5 million for wide and ultrawide bandgap semiconductor research. On November 13th Texas Tech announced another award of over $19 million to help set up a computing cluster in Lubbock.

The project will combine AI with simulation data and lab results. Engineering dean Roland Faller says Texas Tech will be one of the first universities with access to the newest generation of Nvidia chips to power the cluster.

The cluster is not built yet. The announcement gives no install or completion date.

The deck draws each award as wafers, one for every $1 million.
