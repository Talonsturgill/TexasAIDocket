Floor hands helped move the pipe. On three ExxonMobil rigs in the Permian, an arm does.

Reuters reported that robotic arms now position columns of drill pipe over two stories tall. It put the steel at roughly 2,000 pounds.

Reuters found a Red Zone gate around the drilling floor of one rig in Midland. About a third of significant injuries in well drilling happen on the rig floor, ExxonMobil's Bart Cahir told Reuters.

The company expects automated rigs on about 25% of its Permian fleet next year and approximately half by 2028. It expects similar staffing levels on automated rigs.

Chief executive Darren Woods told the earnings call the rigs are "significantly improving safety". He gave no figure. Will a measured result be public by 2028?
