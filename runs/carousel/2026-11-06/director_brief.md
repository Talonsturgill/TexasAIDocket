## Craft refresh (Phase 1, this run)

Raking light reads relief through shadow, best from one narrow source at a grazing angle of about twenty degrees or
less, and a ridge running parallel to the light disappears, so the key should cross the relief's main features.
A digital bas-relief can compress depth heavily and keep its shape from one vantage. Darkening at depth edges raises
contrast the way occlusion does, so a relief's cavities are what make it legible at feed size.
