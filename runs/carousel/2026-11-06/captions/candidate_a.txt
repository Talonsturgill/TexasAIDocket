Roughly 2,000 pounds of steel pipe, moved by a robotic arm.

On ExxonMobil's automated Permian rigs the arms connect it to the drill string. Workers used to help move that pipe. The rig floor is the most common place for accidents on a rig, Reuters reported. On one rig in Midland a gate with a Red Zone sign now surrounds it. A contractor runs the arms from a screen in a small office on the rig.

That is one floor. ExxonMobil has three automated rigs in the Permian today. It expects them on about 25% of its Permian fleet next year and approximately half by 2028. The company says staffing should stay similar and calls the technology "a force multiplier, not a replacement." The safety gain is a potential, not a measured result.
