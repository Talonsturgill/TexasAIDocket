Floor hands helped move the pipe. Now an arm does.

ExxonMobil has three automated rigs in the Permian. Robotic arms on them position columns of drill pipe over two stories tall. Reuters put the steel at roughly 2,000 pounds.

On one rig in Midland a Red Zone gate surrounds the drilling floor. About a third of significant injuries in well drilling happen on the rig floor, ExxonMobil's Bart Cahir told Reuters. A contractor runs the machinery from a screen in a small office on the rig.

The company expects automated rigs on about 25% of its Permian fleet next year and approximately half by 2028. It expects similar staffing levels.

The deck walks from the gripper out to the pipe rack.

Chief executive Darren Woods says the rigs are improving safety. He gave no figure. Will one be public before half the fleet is automated?
