A car with no driver now goes to San Antonio International.

From October 14th anyone with the Waymo app can hail one in San Antonio, the airport included. Airports director Jesus Saenz said travelers now have "a new option to get there."

It is the same service that paused after the April flooding. Texas Public Radio reported an empty Waymo washed into Salado Creek in heavy rain on April 20th. Nobody was hurt.

The federal recall record says the software "may allow the vehicle to slow and then drive into standing water on higher speed roadways." It lists 3,791 potentially affected units.

Read on October 16th, the record still lists the remedy as under development. Waymo's interim step was tighter weather limits and updated maps. NHTSA's recall lookup carries the remedy field under Waymo LLC. What will the finished remedy change at the water's edge?
