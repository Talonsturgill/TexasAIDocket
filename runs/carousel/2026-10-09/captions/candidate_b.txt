UTMB put an AI inside its health record.

The University of Texas Medical Branch in Galveston announced the OpenEvidence collaboration on September 22nd. Clinicians ask it in plain language and get cited answers. UTMB says more than half of them actively use it. Read as an adoption story, it is a strong one.

Texas law has a section called Artificial Intelligence in Electronic Health Record. It says a practitioner who uses AI for diagnostic purposes must disclose that use to patients. UTMB calls its use clinical decision support. No source read says which this is.

A patient can ask in the exam room. The UT System Regents meet November 18th to 19th in Austin. Testimony on agenda topics needs a name and topic sent to the General Counsel at least 24 hours ahead.
