The Board holds regular meetings in February, May, August, and November. Combined committee meetings and Board meetings take place over a two-day period in February, May, August, and November, with committee meetings usually held on Day 1 and the general Board meeting on Day 2. The Board also holds special called meetings as necessary.
Open meetings are usually webcast live. Persons interested in the committee and/or Board meetings and desiring communication or other accommodations should contact the Board Office at least two days prior to the meeting. For more information on accessibility to Board meetings, please read the Accessibility Guidelines .
