Standard tests can miss the struggle.

Many veterans Rocio Norman treated reported trouble finding words, even when standard tests showed no clear impairment.

Researchers at the San Antonio VA already recorded veterans describing how they were injured and an important life event. A $200,000 San Antonio Medical Foundation grant funds a one year UT San Antonio pilot to analyze them with natural language processing. It looks for speech features linked to mild brain injury. The measuring is still a plan.

Today detailed language analysis takes slow transcription and coding, often by several reviewers. The tool is meant to support clinicians, not replace them.

If software can find communication differences, will they track anxiety, post traumatic stress, depression and sleep difficulties?
