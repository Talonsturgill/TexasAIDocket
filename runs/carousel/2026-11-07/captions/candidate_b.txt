More than 20,000 code concerns. Not violations.

Cameras on Dallas sanitation brush trucks photographed that many in four months, the Dallas Morning News reported. Software flags. A person reviews. A citation needs an officer to confirm a violation in person. The paper counted 57 citations at 34 properties.

About 13,800 of the 20,000 images were in southern Dallas. Oak Cliff council member Chad West asked whether the cameras could unintentionally target residents there who can't afford repairs. Code director Chris Christian said the cameras ride existing routes and aren't meant to target communities by income.
