3,700 courtesy notices. 57 citations. Not the same thing.

Cameras on Dallas sanitation trucks scanned 10,000 properties, the city's September 8th memo says, as FOX 4 reported. The memo says code officers reviewed and confirmed the detections behind the 57 citations, and that a citation comes only after an officer inspects the property and notifies the owner, Dallas Express and FOX 4 reported.

39 of the 57 citations were in Council Districts 4, 8 and 7 in southern Dallas, by the city manager's memo. Council member Chad West withdrew a move to cut the funding once the city manager's office agreed to brief the council.

The city's December 2025 briefing describes a three-year contract at $852,000 a year, subject to annual City Council appropriations. What should the council see before it funds another year?
