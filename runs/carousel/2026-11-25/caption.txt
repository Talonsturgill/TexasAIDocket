A scan ordered for something else.

At UTMB Health an AI system now reads radiology reports and flags lung nodules. About two thirds of the nodules its program found came from patients who were not having lung cancer screening.

Every flag goes to patient navigator Amanda Rodriguez. A validated risk score helps her decide which follow up comes first.

Medical director Dr. Shawn Nishi calls the AI an assistant, not a decision maker.

Dr. Nishi recalls a patient in her 20s whose scan after a car accident found a nodule. Dr. Nishi says a biopsy showed lung cancer and the patient had surgery intended to cure it. According to Dr. Nishi the disease was hereditary, and the patient named seven or eight relatives diagnosed with cancer.

Where you get scanned, who reads the report past the reason it was ordered?
