A scan ordered for something else.

At UTMB Health an AI system reviews lung cancer screening reports and also helps identify nodules found by chance. UTMB says about two thirds of its program's nodules came from patients not having lung cancer screening.

Every flag goes to patient navigator Amanda Rodriguez. A validated risk score helps her decide which follow up comes first.

Medical director Dr. Shawn Nishi calls the AI an assistant, not a decision maker.

Dr. Nishi recalls a patient in her 20s whose scan after a car accident found a nodule. Dr. Nishi says a biopsy showed lung cancer and the patient had surgery intended to cure it. According to Dr. Nishi she had a hereditary disease, and she named seven or eight relatives diagnosed with cancer.
