# Frame 7 concepts, "She was in her 20s"

1. **The rule on the ledge, raked.** A long brushed steel age rule lies across a weathered limestone ledge in low apricot sun, seen from a low camera at its young end, so one clear lilac glass bead sits big and sharp in the groove at the 20s tick while the frosted white enamel block of the screening ages rides far off down the rule toward the bright end. Only this beat turns her age into a distance you can see along one scale, with the screening years sitting decades away from where her nodule actually was.

2. **The stair of decades.** A flight of worn limestone treads climbs from the lower left toward a periwinkle sky, one tread per decade, the lilac bead resting on the second tread near the ground while the top treads are clad in frosted white enamel high above it. Only this beat needs her age to sit near the bottom of a climb whose screening landing is still far overhead.

3. **The oak round.** A sawn live oak round lies on caliche under a raking sun, its rings counting years with every tenth ring cut deeper, a lilac glass bead lodged in the ring at the patient's decade near the heart and the outer band of rings from the screening ages glazed frosted white. Only this beat reads a life as rings, with her nodule sitting deep inside the years no screening program reaches.

**Pick: 1.** It makes the distance between her decade and the screening span one physical length on one real object, and the low raking camera gives the bead the foreground it needs, where the stair reads as a bar chart and the oak round reads as a top down tray (both charged by recent judges). Neighbours are an interior oak counter (6) and people at distance on a lawn (8), so a macro on stone is a different scale and world from both.

**Type.** The sky over the rule's far, bright half is the quiet. The headline sits high, two lines across the upper left of the sky, large, with the dek directly under it and still in the sky, so the rule and bead own the lower 55 percent.
