# Slide 06 concepts, WHO DECIDES

1. ONE COUNTER, THREE STATIONS (studio sweep, high orthographic). One long oak counter crosses the whole frame on an apricot and periwinkle sweep: at the far left a palm sized steel reading bridge with a cyan strip lifts one gold flagged card out of a drawer, in the middle a navigator in a plum cardigan sets nine flagged cards along a steel ticket rail, and at the right a doctor in a white coat holds a pen over the front card. Only this beat has it because the sentence is a division of labour, and the picture makes the machine physically the smallest thing at the counter with two whole people doing everything after the flag.

2. THE HAND OFF IN MACRO (studio close, 85 mm). A gold flagged card crosses the frame from a brushed steel slot on the left into a plum sleeved hand, and beyond it, out of focus, a white cuff and a pen wait. Only this beat has it because the card leaves the machine at the frame edge and the people own the rest of the picture, but it is the same macro scale as frame 5's card in a hand.

3. THE MACHINE IN THE BACK ROOM (interior, low wide). A bright workroom where two people stand three quarters away at a big oak table in the window light, and a small steel box with a cyan strip sits on a shelf behind them in the background, a gold card on its tray. Only this beat has it because "works in the background" is drawn literally, but the people would be at room scale with the machine nearly invisible, and it shares frame 5's office world.

PICK: 1. It's the only one where a stranger can read "it flags, people decide" with the type covered, because the three stations are three different sizes and kinds of thing in one left to right line, and its high orthographic studio view differs from frame 5's over the shoulder office and frame 7's raked outdoor rule.

COMPOSITION: the counter and the two people fill the upper and middle of the frame, the sweep's apricot floor runs clear in front of the counter, and the headline sits bottom left over that floor in two lines ("It flags." / "People decide.") at about 96 px, with the dek in three lines under it. Plum pills ride above each station.
