# Slide 05 concepts, A PERSON CHECKS

1. THE NAVIGATOR'S DESK. Over her right shoulder in a plum walled office with a big apricot window on the left, a seated navigator seen three quarters away lifts the front card, gold flagged, from an oak card file of nine cards whose tabs are six periwinkle and three frosted, a monitor beside her turned edge on. Only this beat puts the flag in a person's hand at her own desk, the moment between the machine's flag and the doctor's decision.

2. THE QUEUE FROM ABOVE. A straight down plan of the navigator's desk blotter at 1 to 1 scale where nine cards lie in a ranked column, the gold flagged one slid out to the top by a cropped sleeve, the order itself the picture of a risk score sorting a queue. It is the beat about ordering, but a plan of cards repeats frame 3's plan page and frame 6's rack of nine, so it would sit between two neighbours that already own paper and plan.

3. THE DOOR AND THE CART. A wide low shot down a clinic corridor at golden hour where a single gold card rides a small mail tray toward an open office door, the navigator's silhouette at her desk inside the warm doorway, far and small. It says every flag travels to one person, but the person becomes a speck and the queue's nine cards can't be counted at feed size.

PICK: 1. It is the only one that shows a named role touching the flag, so swapping in any other headline breaks it, and its over the shoulder medium camera differs from frame 4's orthographic row and frame 6's high axonometric counter.

Type: top right column over the plum back wall, the hook in three lines and the dek beneath, with the window and her shoulder holding the left and the card file filling the lower right under the dek.
