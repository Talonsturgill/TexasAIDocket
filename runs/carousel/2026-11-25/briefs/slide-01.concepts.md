# Slide 01 concepts (cover, THE SCAN WAS FOR SOMETHING ELSE)

1. **The glass lung cut by a stray scan field.** A lilac glass teaching lung seen three quarters on a plum studio sweep, a thin periwinkle acrylic slab on steel posts passing through its lower lobes only, as if the field had been set for the abdomen, with one gold bead caught in the overlap. Only this beat has a scan that covers part of a body by accident and still holds a second thing.

2. **The gantry seen from the couch's foot.** An unmarked CT ring in a periwinkle lit room with its couch empty and a frosted pillow, a periwinkle laser plane hanging low through the bore and one gold bead floating at the top edge of that plane. Says "a scan was taken" but needs a person to say "for something else", and the next frames already go to rooms.

3. **A tray of slab sections like a bread slicer.** A row of periwinkle acrylic slices stood upright on an oak bench, stacked like a sliced loaf of a torso, the top few cutting into lilac glass, one slice with a gold bead. Reads as a scan, but the overlap and the "ordered for something else" turn are weaker and it nears a top down tray.

**Pick: 1.** The slab crossing only the lower lobes is the sentence itself: a field ordered for something else that still caught the lung, and the bead is what nobody asked it to find.

Composition: headline across the top in two lines over the quiet sweep wall, the lung apex rising just under it. The dek sits in a left column beside the lung, over the empty wall left of the glass, the slab passing under the dek and bleeding out of the left edge. Lung and slab cast onto the plum floor in the bottom band.
