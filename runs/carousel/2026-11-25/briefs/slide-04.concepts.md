# Slide 04, THE TURN, three concepts

A. THE THREE JIGS. Three identical lilac glass teaching lungs, each with a frosted bronchial tree inside, stand on violet enamel plinths in a row on the apricot sweep, seen through a parallel camera from a raking three quarter. Each wears a scan field as a thick acrylic band held on steel posts: the first a frosted white field over the whole lung (screening), the second a periwinkle band across the bases, the third a periwinkle band across the apices, and a gold bead sits inside every field; only this beat can carry it, because the picture is the share itself (two periwinkle of three) and the second fact (a scan covering part of the lungs still finds one).

B. THE SORTING TRAY. A long oak tray seen low and raking holds three acrylic scan blocks, one frosted and two periwinkle, each cut through a glass lung like a specimen slice, with the gold bead caught in the slice. It would only belong to this beat for the same share, but a tray of blocks reads as a chart from above and frame 3 is already a plan page.

C. THE CORRIDOR OF COUCHES. Three CT couches recede down a lit imaging corridor, the first under a white screening gantry, the other two under periwinkle trauma and abdomen gantries, a gold glint on each couch. It speaks to "the scan was for something else", but it borrows frame 9's gantry and its perspective shrinks the units, which breaks an honest share.

PICK: A. It is the only one where a stranger counts two periwinkle of three at feed size, with equal units under a parallel camera, and it also draws c10 (a field covering only part of a lung still holds a bead), which neither neighbour (a plan page before, a lit office after) shares in world, camera or medium.

Type: the row owns the upper middle against the blue wall, so the headline sits low, left, two lines over the apricot floor, with the dek under it at a smaller size and the floor kept plain for it.
