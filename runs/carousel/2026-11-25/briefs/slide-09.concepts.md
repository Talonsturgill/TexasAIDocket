# Slide 09 concepts, WHAT TO ASK

1. **The empty scanner, waiting.** An empty CT room seen low from the couch foot: a satin white gantry with its deep bore, panel seams and a periwinkle trim band, the periwinkle couch pad (the deck's code for a scan ordered for something else) running toward the reader under a folded pillow, the gantry's laser line lying across the empty pad, apricot window light falling across a violet floor. Only the close can have it, because the headline hands the reader their own next scan, and the room is set for whoever lies down next, whatever the scan was ordered for.

2. **The changing cubicle.** A 40 cm macro of a folded periwinkle gown on a bench in a small changing cubicle, a blank question card tucked under it and a curtain half drawn on apricot light. It is the moment before any scan, the one time a patient can ask, though the ask is carried by a prop rather than by the scan itself.

3. **The imaging floor in cutaway.** A raked aerial cutaway of one hospital imaging wing, five rooms in a row (an emergency bay, an orthopedic room, a cardiac room, an abdominal room, a screening room), each holding a scanner, in a periwinkle and frosted white code. It says any of these scans can count, but at feed size it is the distant isometric dollhouse the judges charged, and it explains with labels.

**Pick: 1.** The empty couch is the reader's own place, and the periwinkle pad under a laser line says "a scan for something else still crosses the chest" without one label, so the picture fails under any other headline.

Composition: the gantry fills the upper right and bleeds the right edge, the couch angles toward the lower right, and the type sits bottom left (headline in two lines, dek under it) over the violet floor in the shade beyond the window's light. The neighbour (frame 8) is outdoors at a live oak with a crowd, so this one is an empty interior with no people.
