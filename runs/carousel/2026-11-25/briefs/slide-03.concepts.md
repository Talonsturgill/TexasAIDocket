# Slide 03, EVERY REPORT, three concepts

1. **One report, one line in gold (material relief, plan view).** A single radiology report sheet lies on a violet stained oak desk under the chassis key, greeked plum ink standing proud of cream paper with tooth and a tri-fold crease, one findings line stamped in raised gold foil that runs to the page edge, where a polished steel binder clip holds a gold flag tab out over the desk, the top corner curling up. Only this beat is about the system reading the report itself, so the frame is the report, and the one thing the machine pulls out of it is the one gold thing on the page.

2. **The fanfold river (material relief, aerial scale).** A continuous fanfold of report paper snakes down the whole frame like a river seen from the air, hundreds of uncountable greeked pages folding back and forth, one gold line glinting far down one fold. It says every report rather than one, but at feed size the river is texture, it invites a count the record does not have, and the gold line shrinks to a speck.

3. **The sorting wall (txthree interior, medium wide).** A tall wall of oak pigeonholes in a reading room at dusk, each slot holding a rolled report, and one slot lit with a gold tab, seen raked from the floor. It is a different world from the neighbours' studio and records room, but frame 2 already draws a cart of folders, so a wall of slots repeats its idea at another size.

**Pick: concept 1.** The headline is about the chart review itself being automated, and only a single readable-as-a-report page with one line lifted out of it says "the system read this page and found this line" without a count, a screen or a machine deciding anything.

Composition: the desk owns the top 43 percent and the hook and dek sit there, left, standing on the page's top edge like a caption over a document. The page bleeds left and bottom, its right edge and curled top corner in frame, the clip and flag cropped by the right edge. Frame 2 is a medium interior and frame 4 an axonometric studio row, so a flat plan relief differs in medium and camera from both.
