# Storyboard — carousel No. 29, September 19th, 2026

**Deck title.** The crime was already in the water code.

**The world.** `firstlight`, a Texas water line at first light, from the lot under a water tower
to the buildings that ask about it. Chassis `assets/js/deck/2026-09-19-firstlight.js`, declared
once, loaded by all nine frames.

**The one light.** The key is low in the east off the camera's LEFT shoulder, at 14 degrees above
the plane. Every lit face in this deck is a left face, every right face is the deck's dark side,
and every cast runs RIGHT and away at about four times the object's own height. A 1.7 m person
throws about 6.8 m. Nothing is lit from overhead and nothing is lit from the right.

**The one screen.** Halftone, cell 7, angle 22, on all nine frames. It is the deck's stock, and it
was chosen by arithmetic. A screen's white-field ceiling bounds a frame's median, measured on
2026-09-15 at L* 26.7 for stipple c5 against 82.7 for halftone c6. This deck's argument is that
the light arrives across nine frames, so a stipple could not have printed it.

**The one accent.** `#2A7A9E`, brand token `comal`, whose own comment in `config/brand.yaml` reads
"the water itself". **COMAL ONLY EVER LANDS ON WATER SOMEBODY HOLDS A NUMBER FOR.** A gauged stock
tank gets it and frame 5's route gets it, because the route IS that number moving between the
three parties. A building, a pad, a sky and a person never do. Carried on frames 1, 2, 5, 6 and 9,
and absent from 3, 4, 7 and 8, which is five frames inside the three-to-six band and never over
about half a percent of any one.

**THIS PARAGRAPH SAID FRAME 2 CARRIED NONE UNTIL ROUND 2, and `measurements.json` records frame 2
with the HIGHEST accent share in the deck.** Frame 2 was redrawn in round 1 as the property line
and its stock tank took the accent deliberately, which is written into that frame's own dossier
and was never carried up here. The deck's strictest declared law was wrong about the deck two
pages later, which is the one place it cannot afford to be.

CONTINUITY: MOTIF_EVOLUTION, CAMERA_MOVE, VALUE_ARC

**Continuity devices, three, named here because `layout_check --require` refuses a storyboard that
declares fewer than two.**

1. **MOTIF EVOLUTION, the vessel.** Something that holds water is in every frame and it gets
   further from anybody's hand as the argument goes on. A stock tank on the lot (1), the hall that
   draws from it (2), the page that now demands the figure (3), the yard the hall energises
   through (4), the route the figure travels (5), the tower and the hall on one horizon (6), the
   building the answer is owed to (7), the building that owes it (8), and the tower again, full,
   with nobody reading anything (9).
2. **CAMERA MOVE, and frames 7 and 8 are the identical CAMERA.** Same eye, same horizon, same
   focal, same light, same type seat, one object swapped. It is the deck's only pair and it is
   the argument said twice: the building that is owed the answer, and the building that owes it.

   **THE RECT IS NOT IDENTICAL AND THIS ENTRY CLAIMED IT WAS.** A courthouse is 30 m wide and a
   data hall is 130 m, so at one distance the second fills the frame and reads as a wall. Round 1
   moved the hall from 34 m back to 48 m for exactly that reason, which means the pair rhymes on
   the camera and not on the rect, and a judge checked the declared rect against the drawing and
   found the claim false. What a pair holds constant is what the READER can see is constant.
3. **VALUE AND PALETTE ARC, AND IT IS THE DEVICE THIS DECK DID NOT PRINT.** Planned frame medians
   16, 22, 32, 26, 38, 34, 44, 40, 26. Measured at 432px, before round 3's repairs, 12.4, 35.7,
   32.5, 16.7, 8.3, 10.7, 19.1, 21.3, 18.5.

   **Six frames came in dark by seven to thirty points and the plan is NOT rewritten to match
   them.** Editing nine planned numbers to equal nine measured ones is the inversion `measure.py`
   exists to prevent, and it would turn a failed device into a satisfied one by typing. Both
   tracks live in `measurements.json`, `panel_ready` passes on the deck's MEDIAN rather than per
   frame, and the honest statement is the one a judge made: the light does not arrive, it spikes
   at frame 2 and collapses at frame 5. The cost is not cosmetic. This deck chose halftone over
   stipple BY ARITHMETIC on the grounds that its closing frames had to reach the forties, and no
   frame reaches thirty six, so the stock decision and the file size were both paid for an arc
   that was not printed.

   **THE LEVEL WAS RE-PLANNED ONCE, AFTER THE PROBE FRAME, AND THIS RECORD SAYS SO.** The first
   plan ran 22 to 66 and it was written against a chassis nobody had rendered. The probe frame
   came back at 38 with its type struck by the screen's own dots, and after the recompose it
   measured 24.5. The SHAPE was right and the LEVEL was written against a press that cannot reach
   it. Re-planning the level once against a measurement is honest. Re-planning it per frame after
   each render would be writing a description and calling it a plan.

**The four structural laws, from the directors room, binding on every frame.**

- **EVERY DRAWN BODY HAS A LIT EDGE, A DARK SIDE AND A CONTACT WITH WHAT IT STANDS ON.** This is
  yesterday's unrepaired finding, which all three judges charged across three rounds on frames 3,
  5 and 7 of carousel no. 28. A shape with one fill is not a subject. Every object in this deck
  comes off `txobjects.js` or `txfig.js` at true scale, which is where its lit face, its shaded
  face and its cast come from.
- **NO FRAME PRINTS A READING THE RECORD DOES NOT CONTAIN.** Every instrument is drawn with its
  graduations and without a value, every gauge band is blank, and the tower carries no town name.
  The only numerals in nine frames are the ones the two fetched documents print.
- **NOTHING IS DRAWN AS HAVING HAPPENED.** No stop-work tag, no chained gate, no dry tank, no
  gavel, no red tag. Nothing has been prosecuted and no permit has been denied on the record's own
  dates, and a frame that drew one would publish a verdict this record cannot carry.
- **ONE CREAM SHEET IN THE DECK.** Frame 3 only, and it lies on a LIT tailgate rather than a dark
  board. Yesterday's value track strobed on canvas mean from 37.6 to 91.4 and the single measured
  cause was four frames solving their problem with a cream sheet on a dark board.
