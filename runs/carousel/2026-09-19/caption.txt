The survey was never optional.

On September 14th the Governor's office told the Texas Water Development Board to penalize past failures to file.

A data center that fails to complete and return the survey "commits an offense punishable as a crime". One that returns it late is "ineligible for TCEQ permits, amendments, or renewals". Neither consequence is new. The Texas Water Code already requires the survey and the permit bar sits in its Chapter 11.

The same day ERCOT issued a market notice carrying the board's own water questions. Answers are due October 12th.

The release states the board can't write a state water plan while it is denied detail on water consumption. The board's report on its enforcement progress is due to the Governor's office by October 14th. Does it name a single enforcement action?
