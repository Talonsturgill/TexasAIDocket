# Selection — carousel no. 29, 2026-09-19

## The story

**Texas made a blank form a crime, and it did not have to pass anything to do it.**

The Texas Water Development Board writes the state water plan. The Governor's directive says the
board can't do that job while it is denied detailed information on existing and anticipated water
consumption, and that major water users including data centers appear to have committed civil and
criminal violations by not providing it.

**Two things the fact checker killed, and the deck may not carry either.** The water plan sentence
is the Governor's office describing the board's position rather than the board speaking, so no
frame puts it in the board's mouth. And nothing on either page says how many surveys went
unreturned, so no frame says "largely", "most" or any figure standing for them.

On September 14th, 2026 two documents landed on the same day.

- The Office of the Governor directed the board to enforce the survey against data centers, past
  failures included, and named the two consequences the Texas Water Code already carried. Not
  returning it is an offense punishable as a crime. Not returning it on time bars the filer from
  TCEQ permits, amendments or renewals under Chapter 11.
- ERCOT issued Market Notice M-B091426-01 and put the board's own water questions into Section C
  of a request aimed at developers of data centers 25 MW and larger. The body narrows the actual
  recipients to two defined groups, so no frame says every developer of that size receives one.
  The board's Executive Administrator authorized the operator to ask on its behalf. Answers are
  due at 5 p.m. Central Prevailing Time on October 12th, 2026, notarized, and an incomplete
  answer to Section C **may** subject the recipient to criminal penalties under Texas Water Code
  16.012. The modal is load bearing and no frame states penalties as automatic.

Docket items **tx-2026-0171** and **tx-2026-0172**, both admitted this run on documents fetched
and quote-checked this run.

## The angle

**The law did not change. The enforcement did.**

Nothing was legislated on September 14th. The Water Code already REQUIRES the survey, in its own
present tense, and the permit bar it names sits in Chapter 11. HOW LONG EITHER HAS BEEN THERE IS
NOT IN ANYTHING THIS RUN FETCHED, and an earlier cut of this paragraph said "had been for years",
which is the kind of sentence that sounds like context and is an invention. What happened is that the state pointed them at data centers and said so out
loud. The deck is about a mandatory thing becoming an enforced thing, which is a different event
and a much commoner one than a new rule.

The second half of the angle is the chain. The board needs a number. The board can't reach the
people who have it, because they are in an interconnection queue the grid operator runs. So the
grid operator asks the board's question, on the board's behalf, and forwards the answer back.
Three bodies to move one figure.

## The two adjacency risks, named rather than hoped away

**This is the third data center item decked in six days.** 2026-09-14 was the grid operator's
Batch Zero eligibility verification. 2026-09-15 was the three operational risks it put to its own
Board. The separations are real and the deck has to earn them.

- **Subject.** Those two were electrical. This is water, a different resource under a different
  code enforced by a different agency.
- **Actor.** Those two were the grid operator acting on its own system. The deciding party here
  is the Governor, and the grid operator is the postman.
- **The notice settles it in its own words.** *"This RFI is separate from the Batch Zero
  Eligibility RFI issued on September 9, 2026."* That sentence is in the claims and it is why
  this is not the September 14th deck told again.

**The absence hook is banned today.** The 2026-08-29 ledger entry records that four of eleven
decks were built on what a document does not say, that judges named it in three separate rounds,
and that the next run should spend an opening move that is not an absence. This deck is built on
a PRESENCE. Two consequences that exist, a deadline that exists, and a report date that exists.
Nowhere does it turn on a silence.

**September 14th already used the form.** Its angle reads *"A statewide audit ordered from the
Governor's office turned out to be a form."* So this deck is NOT about the mechanics of a request
for information. No frame is about notarization, portals or who files on whose behalf as its
subject. The form appears once, as the object the consequences attach to.
