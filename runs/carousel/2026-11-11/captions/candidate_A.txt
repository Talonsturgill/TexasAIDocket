Starbase hears the public December 9th.

Its City Commission takes up a tax abatement for a SpaceX gas-fired power plant and super-compute facility on 444 acres east of Brownsville.

Brownsville de-annexed that land in August at SpaceX's request, the Texas Tribune reports. SpaceX told commissioners the offer was good that day only. The company pledged the city up to $220 million for water and wastewater projects.

Starbase later annexed it. Brownsville's 90-day pause on data center applications began last week and doesn't reach it. The Tribune says the gas plant could keep the facility off the state grid.

The commission sits at 7 p.m. at 48491 State Highway 4. The notice puts initial phases at about $24 billion. Nothing is approved.

The deck walks the line to the hearing room.

Will the commission vote the night it hears the room?
