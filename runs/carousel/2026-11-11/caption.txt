Brownsville commissioners met for six hours and voted unanimously to de-annex land near the port at SpaceX's request. The offer was good that day only. SpaceX agreed to give the city up to $220 million for water and wastewater, the Texas Tribune reported.

Starbase later annexed it. Brownsville's 90-day data center moratorium took effect last week and doesn't reach it, the Tribune reports.

Starbase's City Commission will consider a tax abatement for a SpaceX gas-fired power plant and super-compute facility on about 444 acres. The notice estimates the initial phases at about $24 billion. The Tribune reports the plant could keep the facility off the state's power grid. The public is invited to be heard at 48491 State Highway 4.
