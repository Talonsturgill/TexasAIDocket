Two routes. One veto.

A new charter needs the education commissioner's recommendation first. The State Board of Education can refuse it. That board has refused. Education Freedom Accounts publishes four requirements instead. A child carries $10,474 to an approved private school.

Alpha School's front page says an AI tutor gives students their coursework. ProPublica and The Texas Tribune report the board rejected Alpha's charter pitch last summer. The program accepted most of its campuses anyway.

A spokesperson for the voucher program told them "That wasn't relevant." Read the four requirements and that answer holds up. Not one of them asks who or what does the teaching.

Alpha's own front page says its classes learn twice as fast and names no study. Which of the four would ever check whether it works?
