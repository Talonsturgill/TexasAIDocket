## Panel round 1 and its repairs

Round 1 was [6.19, 6.49, 6.79], median 6.54, with two hard fails.

1. Integrity. The cover and title said "Tesla's Texas rows read Production". The source's page 7 lists Cathode Materials and Lithium Refining under the Texas label at Early Ramp (c16, c17). The REPAIR scopes the hook and the document title to the set the source supports: "Tesla's Texas vehicle and Megapack rows read Production. The robot row reads Construction." The five doors draw those five rows.
2. Craft. Frame 8 drew a four-door crossover with mirror stems, side glass and a plate recess, against c42, and carried no "drawn to illustrate". The REPAIR replaces the draped kit crossover with one continuous lofted fabric shell over a two-seat form, with a folded hem above the tyres and no glass, mirror, seam or plate in the geometry. The dek now ends "The car is drawn to illustrate." The note in flow_notes.md that called frame 8 repaired was wrong when the judges read it. It is now true of the frame.

Also from the judges:
- Slide 6's dek attached c22's "vehicle and humanoid robot autonomy software" to the Cortex rows. It now attaches to the 400 MW plan, as the source does, on the slide and in the web edition.
- Slide 8's "No count is given." became "Tesla gives no count." so the set is named.
- The caption's first line went from "is made in California" to "production began in California" (c27).
- The first comment no longer dates the update October 21st. The PDF prints no date, so the 33 claims taken from it carry none.

Art:
- Slide 6's plate runs off both edges as a ground, brushed and scratched.
- Slide 9's work lamps cast real shadows.
