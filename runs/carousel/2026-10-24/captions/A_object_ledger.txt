A car with no steering wheel or pedals.

That is the Cybercab as NHTSA describes it. Tesla started Robotaxi rides with it in Austin on September 3rd. NHTSA opened an audit of how Tesla certified it that same day. It's still open.

Tesla's quarterly table lists more than 125,000 a year of installed Cybercab capacity under its Texas label. Tesla says that is not the current production rate.

The update gives no count of Cybercabs built or carrying riders. NHTSA calls the Austin start "a small number" of vehicles. The record holds no count between more than 125,000 a year and "a small number."

The deck lights one bay of an illustrated plant for each Texas row marked Production. The Optimus robot row reads Construction, so its bay is dark.

Tesla told NHTSA it plans more vehicles and locations. How many Cybercabs carry riders in Austin today?
