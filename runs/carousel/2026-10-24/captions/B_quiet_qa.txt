The audit opened the same day.

Tesla says Cybercabs started giving Robotaxi rides in Austin on September 3rd. NHTSA opened an audit query into the car's federal safety certification that day.

How many Cybercabs are carrying riders? Tesla's update gives no count. It gives installed capacity, which it says is not the current production rate. Its Texas rows list Model Y at more than 375,000 a year. Cybertruck and Cybercab sit at more than 125,000 each and Megapack at 50 GWh. All four read Production. The Texas row for Optimus reads Construction with no figure. The humanoid robot's production began in Fremont, California.

The deck draws each Production row as a lit bay and the robot's as a dark one.

The audit is still open. Which federal safety standards did Tesla determine don't apply to a car with no steering wheel?
