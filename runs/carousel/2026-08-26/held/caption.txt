The billion buys no building.

On August 12th the UT System Regents approved adding Phase 1 of the UT Dell Medical Center in Austin to the capital improvement program, at "a total project cost for Phase I of $1,000,000,000". The first line of the board's own cost table reads "Building Cost $0". The money is dirt and drawings.

Nine frames on what the board approved, what its own item says, and the one word that item never uses.

The board's background says that debt is "expected to be repaid from clinical revenues", which is what a hospital earns from treating patients. What would you want on the public record before a billion is repaid that way?
