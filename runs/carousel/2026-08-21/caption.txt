Texas authorized what was already driving.

Aurora began driverless freight between Dallas and Houston on May 1st, 2025. The TxDMV authorization under SB 2807 became a live requirement for commercial driverless operation on May 28th, 2026. That is 392 days later. Kodiak reported a fleet of 35 driverless trucks in the Permian Basin on June 30th, 2026. Waymo opened Houston robotaxi rides to anyone with the app on August 20th, 2026. Raul Gallegos of the National Federation for the Blind of Texas welcomed it for blind riders.

The state Senate's transportation committee holds a public hearing August 25th, 2026 in room E1.016. One of its three charges asks the committee to quantify the impact on traffic-related collisions.

That charge names three agencies whose rule making is in scope. The one that publishes the state's crash records is not among them. This record checked it anyway, and TxDOT's crash data page names no automated driving category. The per incident record this docket reached is federal.

The Legislature posts the meeting on capitol.texas.gov under Committees, Meetings Upcoming.
