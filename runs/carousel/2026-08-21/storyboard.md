# Storyboard — 2026-08-21 — Returned light

## The synthesis, and what was taken from where

Three treatments came back. The winner is the **returned light** treatment, because its central
idea is a physical law rather than a metaphor: a thing is bright because it threw the light back,
and a thing is dark because it did not. That is literally how a driverless vehicle perceives a
Texas road, it is literally what a Texas highway is engineered around, and it is the only one of
the three that can draw the honest absence as a **property of a real surface** rather than as an
empty box. The empty neatlined field shipped yesterday and is off the table, so a treatment that
needed a new way to draw nothing was the one that earned the deck.

**Grafted from the road surface treatment.** Two things, and both are load bearing.
The register stamp on every slide, which turns nine different cameras into one legible idea by
naming how each frame is drawn. And slide 2 entire, seven marks on one time lane with the gaps
proportional to elapsed days and **the seventh mount empty**, which is the best single drawing any
of the three produced. It puts the whole spine in one frame and draws the future as a physical
absence rather than as a caption.

The stamp was `FRAME WIDTH 8 FT` and so on for the first render, and it came out. A frame width in
feet is a numeral this run never computed, and two independent reviews found it contradicting its
own drawing. This project's law is that a numeral is computed from data or it is not published, and
camera furniture is not an exemption from it.

**Grafted from the empty seat treatment.** One catch, and it changes what the deck may say.
That director noticed that **no claim in the file supports a right to testify.** The two minute
limit and the fifteen written copies live only in the posted Notice of Public Hearing, which sits
under `capitol.texas.gov/TLODOCS/` and is disallowed. The claims file now carries that as an
explicit rejection, and no frame and no caption asserts it. What the record holds is that the
Legislature's own listing types the meeting a public hearing and gives the room, which is c36.
Also taken: the standing flag that the Waymo post writes the organisation two ways and neither
may be harmonised into the other.

**Discarded.** The four-appearances-of-one-seat motif, because four frames of one object is what
`bespoke_check` exists to catch. The corridor band struck across a map, because the claims give
endpoint cities and one distance and nothing that fixes a route, so no route is drawn anywhere.

## The register

**The road at night in the vehicle's own returned light, one noon inversion at slide 5, closing
under a public sodium lamp.**

Made of hot mix asphalt at night with no sky in it, glass bead thermoplastic, retroreflective
sheeting, amber trailer marker lamps, caliche haze in a headlamp beam, milled shoulder rumble
grooves, a high pressure sodium lamp, and one square metre of sun struck Houston sidewalk.

It touches none of the four spent registers. No Big Bend horizon and no fBm sky, because the deck
has no sky. No Gulf Coast overcast, because there is exactly one daylight frame and it is nothing
but hard shadow. No lit interior. No paper and no print.

**Three laws, held on every frame.**

1. **One light source per frame, from three.** The vehicle's emitter, the sun, or a public sodium
   lamp. No frame mixes two. The emitter runs 1 to 4, the sun takes 5 alone, the sodium lamp
   takes 6, 8 and 9, and the emitter returns once at 7, which is the frame where the instrument
   is pointed at the state's record and gets nothing back.
2. **Retroreflective means the record holds it. Matte means the record does not.** No mark is
   partly one.
3. **No red on any vehicle.** Trailer conspicuity returns white and markers are amber, so the
   reserved red is spent once and nothing else on nine frames wears it.

## The value arc, and the one inversion

| slide | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | **5** | 6 | **7** | 8 | 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean value | 2.5 | 2.0 | 4.0 | 2.5 | **8.5** | 5.0 | **1.5** | 5.0 | 6.0 |

**The inversion is slide 5 and it is doubled.** It is the only frame with the sun in it and the
only frame that argues the other way, so the value inversion and the argument inversion are one
event, at the position the library says the turn belongs. The 5 to 7 fall, the deck's ceiling to
the deck's floor with one mid frame between, is the strip's whole rhythm.

## Furniture, identical on all nine

The counter `NN / 09` in JetBrains Mono at 26px, the register stamp in the same face and size,
the source line, `texasaidocket.com` read from `config/brand.yaml`, and a two line location stamp
naming only what a claim on that frame names. Every one of them is the same type size on every
slide, which is the coherence spine.

The location stamp used to carry a coordinate pair. It came out with the frame width stamp and for
the same reason, and the render that shipped them had two of its three coordinates wrong against
the committed gazetteer's own computed centroids. The stamp is `data-decorative`, which is what
kept both the coordinates and a typed county count out of every gate's reach, so what it may say
is now a rule rather than a habit: **the location stamp names only what a claim cited on that same
frame names, OR a value this run computed.** Stated loosely it was unenforceable and three panels each caught one more stamp breaking it, one frame at a time. Checked all nine at once, three more were breaking it: STATEWIDE on two frames and THIS RECORD CHECKED on a third are the deck describing its own scope in words no claim uses. Not fabrications about the world the way PERMIAN BASIN on an Aurora only frame was, and exactly the kind of exception that turns a rule into a habit.**
