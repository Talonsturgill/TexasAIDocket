# Slide 09 concepts, NEXT SPRING

1. OVER THE PRODUCER'S SHOULDER. A rancher in a straw cowboy hat and chambray shirt seen three quarters from behind, holding a phone up to the pasture in golden light, its screen showing the very stock tank and live oak beyond, with one dusk gold report pill under the photo against the cobalt sky. Only this beat names the part a Texan would play, reporting from a phone, so only this beat can put the reader at a producer's shoulder with the report in hand.

2. THE EMPTY PLINTH. The caliche stone plinth from frames 1 to 3 stands empty in the cobalt to peach studio sweep, a soft pool of light on it and the pale ring where the glass would stand, waiting for what comes next spring. It answers "watch for the prototype" with the absence of the thing, but it can't carry the producer's phone and would repeat the studio of frames 1 to 3.

3. A PHONE ON A FENCE POST. A phone lying face up on the sawn top of a cedar corner post at a pipe ranch gate, close tabletop scale at dawn, its screen a pasture photo and a gold report pill, the pasture blurred beyond. It names the phone report but drops the person, so the "part a Texan can play" reads as an object rather than a person, and its fence post echoes frame 8's road of posts.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one that could not run under another headline in this deck: the next step in the claims is a producer reporting from a phone, and the phone's photo of the scene ahead ties the report to a real pasture. It is unlike frame 8 in camera and scale (a person at arm's length against a wide road of posts), and the late sun sits behind the camera so the sky ahead stays cobalt for the type.

Composition: the hat, shoulder and arm fill the right half and bleed right and bottom, the phone sits centre right below the middle, the tank and oak sit low on the left under the horizon. The headline sits top left in three lines in the clear cobalt sky, the dek under it in a 560 px column ending above the horizon.
