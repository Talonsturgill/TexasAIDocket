# Slide 08 concepts, THE ROAD TO 2028

1. THE LONG ROAD. A pale caliche ranch road bends away to a low sunset horizon with an oilfield pipe fence of exactly FIG.months_to_end domed posts beside it, the last post standing alone where the fence stops and the road runs on, every post laying a long violet shadow across the road. Only this beat measures a duration, and a road a reader can see to the end of is how a Texan reads a long wait.

2. THE CROSSING. Seen from a crouch on a gravel bar, a line of FIG.months_to_end cedar stakes steps across a shallow limestone creek and up the far bank toward hill country, each stake one month of the walk. It speaks to fieldwork across ground and to patience, though the water reads as a border and invites a Mexico reading the copy does not draw.

3. THE GATE AT DAWN. A pipe ranch gate stands open at the near edge of a pasture with FIG.months_to_end T-posts in the grass beyond it, shot from the hinge post at knee height, the gate swung toward the camera. It says a journey has started, but an open gate also says the work is done, which this beat must not claim.

**Pick: 1.** The road is the only one that is wholly about length and time with no false reading, and the last post where the fence ends and the road goes on is the date the headline names. Neighbours are a pasture of bales seen raking and orthographic (07) and a person at arm's length (09), so an eye level perspective road with a vanishing point is unlike both.

Type: the headline sits big and centred high in the cobalt sky over the road's vanishing point, two lines, the dek centred under it. The sun glow sits low on the left, the fence runs in from the lower left, and the sky above the horizon is kept clear of tree crowns so the type sits on clean cobalt.
