# Slide 7 concepts, ONE OF 40

1. **The bale yard.** Forty round bales in five rows of eight on golden stubble, seen high and raked through a long shifted lens, with one bale rolled out of the near row toward the reader into its own low light and its empty slot left behind in the row. A field a rancher counts at a glance is the only picture where "one in 40" is a thing you can stand in front of, and the empty slot says this one was picked out of the many.

2. **The sale barn board.** A studio sweep holding a pegboard of forty identical brass cattle ear tags on hooks, one tag lifted off its hook and turned to the key. Forty equal tags make the count without weighing dollars, but a tag board reads as a wall of swatches and a UI grid, the charge judges keep naming.

3. **The seed drill.** A macro on a planter's seed box, forty seed cups in a ring on a cast iron plate, one seed riding up out of its cup into the light. One bet among many as a planted seed is apt, but the ring reads as a dial and the macro scale collides with frames 2 to 4's macro glass.

**Pick: 1.** Only this beat asks a reader to count forty of something equal and find the one, and a bale yard is a count a Texan already reads at highway speed. Its camera (high raked oblique, horizon at the top fifth) and world (a stubble yard at midday gold, no road, no posts in a line) stay off frame 6's carved relief and frame 8's eye level road.

**Composition with the type.** The yard rakes from the far upper left to the near right, so the lower left triangle is open stubble crossed by the bales' long violet casts. The headline sits there in two lines over the casts, the dek under it in a 620 px column, clear of the claim ids at the bottom. The forward bale sits low right, opposite the type, so the eye runs type to bale.
