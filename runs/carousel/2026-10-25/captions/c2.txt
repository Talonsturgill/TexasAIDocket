The water board asked by mail. Now the grid operator asks too.

Records the Texas Tribune obtained for 2020 through 2025 count 334 data center facilities. 233 never submitted a requested water use survey. The fine is up to $500 and the board says it has no authority to prosecute.

ERCOT's September 14th notice carries the water board's questions in its Section C. Each answer came notarized, filed by the developer's utility.

Does that reach the 233? No. ERCOT asked data centers from 25 MW that are still proposed or not yet energized. Asking before the power is on is how the buildout gets planned around water. Plants already running still answer to a mailed form.

The deck gives each of the 334 a bore and plugs the 101 that answered at least once.

Will the report ERCOT expects by December 10th say what those answers show?
