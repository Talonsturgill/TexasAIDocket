The water board's survey arrives by mail.

It goes to operating facilities. State law requires a reply. The Texas Tribune's records count 334 data center facilities from 2020 through 2025. 233 never sent one back. Skipping it is a Class C misdemeanor. The Tribune puts the fine at up to $500. The board says it can't prosecute. State officials don't know whether any penalty has been issued.

Data centers not yet switched on now answer to the grid operator.

ERCOT's September 14th notice carries the water board's questions. The developer's own utility files each notarized answer. Silence is reported to the PUC and the Office of the Governor. The request reaches projects not yet energized.

The deck drills a bore for each facility and plugs the ones that answered.

ERCOT expects a report with the PUC by December 10th. Will it say who answered?
