The water board asked by mail. Now ERCOT asks before the power flows.

Records the Texas Tribune obtained for 2020 through 2025 count 334 data center facilities. 233 never submitted a requested water use survey. The Tribune reports a fine of up to $500 for skipping it. The board says it has no authority of its own to prosecute.

Does ERCOT's request reach those 233? Its list is drawn differently. The survey goes only to operating data centers. The request reaches proposed ones of 25 to under 75 MW classed as Substantiated and Batch Zero facilities or additions not yet energized.

ERCOT's September 14th notice puts the water board's questions in Section C. A complete response carries a notarized attestation. A failure to respond is reported to the PUC and the Office of the Governor.

Will the report ERCOT expects to publish by December 10th say how many responded?
