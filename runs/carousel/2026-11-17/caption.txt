One written answer on a STAAR reading test.

Since December 2023 a Texas Education Agency scoring engine has read the written answers on English STAAR tests first. Its design then sent at least 25 percent to people too. Their score counted. TEA says hand scoring would cost $15 million to $20 million more a year.

Then districts asked for a person. Reading rescore requests rose from 2,758 in spring 2024 to 64,489 this spring, the Texas Tribune reported October 7th. People did every rescore. More than 13,300 scores went up. In Dallas ISD 34% of more than 5,100 tests came back higher.

Fewer than half of one percent of all open-ended reading answers changed.

A rescore costs a district $50 a test unless the score changes.

TEA's design dates to December 2023 and March 2024. Does it still describe this spring's scoring?
