How a stray looks is spelled out.

Southwest Research Institute's release says strays "appear to be malnourished and skittish". Dogs that seem healthier and more approachable "are likely owned by local families and are classified as roaming."

In San Antonio a phone on a vehicle window will record video. Object detection software will find the dogs. Automated and human review will check that each dog was unrestrained. The same review will assess whether it was owned or a stray.

The project will repeat a 2019 manual survey in the same 32 study areas. It is a sample, not a count of every dog.

Animal Care Services Director Jon Gary calls the study another tool for seeing where loose dogs turn up. Field research is expected to run through December. The department will get a final report.
