Texas A&M, UT Austin and Purdue trained models on junk.

Four models trained on mixes of junk and control Twitter/X posts. Junk meant under 30 tokens with over 500 likes, retweets, replies and quotes combined. Control meant over 100 tokens with none.

Llama3 8B Instruct was the most sensitive. Its ARC Challenge score with chain of thought fell from 72.1 to 57.2 as junk went from 0% to 100%.

Thought skipping was the main failure. The paper's "No Thinking" showed up in 84% of failures with junk defined by engagement.

Instruction data 4.8 times the junk tokens didn't fully undo it. The best repaired models still trailed baseline by 17.3 points on ARC Challenge.

The junk shares were doses in a training mix and no measure of the internet. The authors limit the findings to Twitter/X junk and the models studied.
