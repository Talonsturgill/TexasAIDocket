In Dallas, researchers added one line.

UT Southwestern's team gave three open source AI models matched pairs of emergency patients. With no stigmatizing detail, all three picked out higher-risk patients at rates well above chance.

Once stigmatizing detail was added, nearly 10% of patients were incorrectly downgraded in some instances. One pair set "established patient with multiple chronic conditions" against "17 visits in the past 12 months."

The records came from Stanford Medicine in California and Beth Israel Deaconess in Massachusetts. Hospitals increasingly explore AI to help prioritize care, the release says.

First author Philip Jarrett says bias varies by model and each tool needs testing against real patient outcomes before use. The release names none of the three models. Which were they?
