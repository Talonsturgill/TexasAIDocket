The county decided before Saronic did.

The Texas Tribune reported on June 18th that the Cameron County Commissioners Court approved a $211 million tax break for Saronic. The court hoped the company would pick the Port of Brownsville. Opponents told the court it would take money from public schools.

That month Saronic told the Tribune it had not yet chosen a site.

On September 30th Saronic broke ground in Brownsville. It calls Port Alpha a software-defined yard for autonomous and manned ships. It puts private capital at more than $3 billion and direct jobs at up to 10,000.

The Tribune reported a 95% abatement over 20 years. It requires 35% of the full-time workforce to be local residents. The break shrinks if Saronic falls short of job projections.

Up to 10,000 is Saronic's own figure. Which projection does the break shrink against?
