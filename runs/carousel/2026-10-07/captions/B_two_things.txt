Ships built to sail with nobody aboard. A tax break counted in people.

Saronic broke ground on Port Alpha at the Port of Brownsville on September 30th. It puts more than $3 billion of private capital into 835 acres. It expects up to 10,000 direct jobs, from welding to naval architecture. The yard is for manned ships too.

Cameron County's say came in June, before Saronic had picked the site.

The commissioners court approved a $211 million tax break, the Texas Tribune reported. It is a 95% abatement over 20 years. Local residents must make up 35% of the full-time workforce. The break shrinks if Saronic falls short of its job projections. Opponents told the court it would take money from public schools.

The ship in the frames is drawn to illustrate.

Up to 10,000 is Saronic's number. Is it the one the break gets measured against?
