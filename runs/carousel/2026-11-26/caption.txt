37 percent reads like a failing grade.

Texas Tech worked with AVX Defense Technologies of Fort Worth on the Army's FleetSpace program. Together they developed an AI enabled planning tool for Army aviation. Texas Tech says a second tool tracks the remaining useful life of aircraft engines on its own. It recommends maintenance tied to the aviation technical manual. Texas Tech says both have been provided to the Army.

The 37 percent was never a grade. It is the share of missing and corrupted flight data the team's neural networks restored in testing. In testing they also predicted unplanned and 480 hour phase maintenance actions and cut data files by up to 73 percent.

Texas Tech's release names no result from service. If the Army runs them outside testing, how much of the result will be public?
