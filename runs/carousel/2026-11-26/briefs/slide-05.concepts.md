# Slide 05, FOUR EIGHTY, three concepts

1. **The hour rule (TXBENCH relief, plan view).** A brushed stainless hour rule crosses an indigo bench mat on the diagonal, a brass hook stop at its zero end, one etched tick every 2 hours and a long tick every 10, no numerals, and a single dusk gold enamel tab clipped at the 480 hour tick, its cast falling across the steel. Only this beat has a scheduled interval measured in hours, so only this beat can turn the phase into a length a reader watches coming, with the software's output as the one gold mark at its end.

2. **The planning rail (txthree studio, raked low camera).** A long machined aluminium planning rail recedes from the lens into a cerulean studio sweep, 48 machined stops along it, one gold magnet seated on the far stop. It reads as a schedule at distance, but the frame before is also machined aluminium in a raked camera (the vise bars), so it would repeat the neighbour's world and medium.

3. **The meter drum (txthree macro).** An extreme macro of a mechanical hour meter's drums rolling toward 480, the last drum enamelled gold. It is vivid, but a meter counts hours already flown, which implies service use and would break the deck's testing only rule.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one that makes "480 hour phase" a measured length read straight from FIG.phase_hours, it differs in medium (plan relief) from the raked interior before it and the macro render after it, and it implies nothing about service.

Composition. The rule runs from the lower left (zero, the brass hook) to the upper right (480, the gold tab), lit from the upper left so its cast lies along its lower right edge. The headline sits right aligned in the lower right triangle of open mat, its ragged left edge stepping with the diagonal, the dek under it. The "480 hour phase" pill sits beside the tab in the upper left triangle, which is otherwise quiet mat.
