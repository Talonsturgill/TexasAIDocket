# Slide 07 concepts, PEOPLE DECIDE

1. THE BENCH IN THE DOOR LIGHT. A low camera at a maintenance bench inside a hangar bay, backlit through a wide open door: a closed manual binder, a coiled borescope, a pair of blue nitrile gloves and a steel parts tray holding one blade flagged in dusk gold, the engine on its stand soft behind in the doorway and a long shaft of door light on the floor. Only this beat has it because the software's one output (the gold flag) has been handed to the tools of a person, and the person's kit, not the machine, owns the frame.

2. THE HAND AT THE PORT. A close macro at an engine's borescope port where a gloved hand, cropped at the wrist, feeds the probe in, with the gold flag tag hanging off the flange a hand's width away. It is the literal sentence (the AI flagged it, a person looks), but it shares frame 6's inside-the-engine macro scale and puts a hand at the centre where the kit's hands read as mannequins.

3. THE PLAN ON THE SHADOW BOARD. A frontal wall of a tool shadow board in the hangar, each tool in its painted outline, with the borescope's outline empty and the gold flagged card clipped where the borescope hangs. Clever but it reads as an inventory, it is flat face on (a charge judges keep making) and the person is only implied by an absence.

PICK: 1. It is the only one where the reader sees at once that the software's output has left the machine and is waiting on a person's bench, with a different world (a lit interior), camera (eye at bench height) and scale from frame 6's macro and frame 8's map.

Type: centred at the top over the shaded hangar wall above the open door, the hook in two lines and the dek under it, so the door light is the axis the type hangs over and the bench owns the bottom half.
