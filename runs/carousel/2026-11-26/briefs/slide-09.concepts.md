# Slide 9 concepts, "Every result so far is from testing"

1. **From inside the shop, looking out the open door.** The camera stands deep in an indigo hangar bay and looks out through a tall open door at a cerulean first light sky, with the engine on its stand at the threshold seen from its exhaust end and two gold hard cases on the sunlit floor beside it, long shadows raking back into the bay. Only the close has this picture, because the beat is a thing still inside, handed over and on test, facing the world where the first result from service would come from.

2. **The test cell window.** A macro through a thick test cell window, the engine a soft shape behind glass and a record tray on the sill in front of it with its restored cells glowing gold. It belongs to "testing", though it repeats the record tray of the middle frames and reads as a lab, not as the open question of service.

3. **The empty apron at dawn.** An aerial three quarter of a wide concrete apron in first light, painted bays empty except for one engine stand and two gold cases at a hangar door, the rest of the bays waiting. It says "nothing in service yet", though the subject shrinks under a tenth of the frame and the empty bays risk implying a fleet and a scale the copy never claims.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where the picture itself is the sentence (inside, at the door, not yet out), it is the opposite side, world and light of the cover's front studio view, and it gives the type a bright quiet sky through the door to sit in.

Type: the headline in two lines and the dek under it, in the sky framed by the door, high in the frame; the engine sits on the horizon line below it and the cases sit low on the lit floor.
