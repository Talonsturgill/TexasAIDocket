# Slide 6 concepts, THE ONE BLADE (c7 c8)

1. **The disc at macro, inside the case.** A heat tinted turbine disc fills the frame from a low raking camera, its near blades cropped by three edges and the ring receding up into the indigo case, with one blade carrying a thin dusk gold line along its leading edge. Only this beat has it because the Propulsion System Validator tracks engine life part by part, and the picture is one part found among many before it fails.

2. **The blade on the bench, borescope view.** A circular borescope eye view down the annulus, the blades seen edge on through a ring of lens vignette, one leading edge outlined in gold. It sells "on its own" as a tool looking inside, but the next frame is a maintenance bench with a borescope, so it would collide with frame 7's world.

3. **The life ruler as a blade.** A single twisted blade laid horizontally on an indigo granite plate like a ruler, a gold line running from root toward tip as far as its remaining life. It reads as a gauge, but it would draw a length that stands for a figure the brief does not give, which the honesty rule forbids, and frame 5 is already a ruler on a plate.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one whose subject is the beat's own sentence (one engine's life, tracked down to one part), it is a macro unlike frame 5's plan view plate and frame 7's medium hangar bench, and it draws no length or count.

Composition: the disc's far rim recedes up into the indigo case wall, which leaves the top third quiet. The headline sits top left in two lines over that indigo, the dek directly under it. The near blades and the gold leading edge own the middle and bottom.
