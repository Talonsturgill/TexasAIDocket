# Slide 01 concepts, BUILT IN LUBBOCK

1. THE ENGINE ON ITS STAND. An illustrative turboshaft sits yawed on its indigo stand in a cerulean sweep, seen three quarter from the intake at just above shaft height, its open access panel and one dusk gold tag hanging under the turbine flange the only warm mark on the machine. Only this beat introduces the machine the whole deck reads, and the tag says that something now reads it before it fails.

2. THE TAG, MACRO. A 30 cm macro on the heat tinted turbine flange, its bolt ring receding, with the gold tag on its wire filling the foreground and the engine dissolving into the indigo case behind. It names what the software puts out, but at feed size it reads as a luggage tag and leaves "provided to the Army" with no machine to stand for.

3. THE HANDOVER BENCH. Two closed hard cases on a shop floor at the foot of the engine stand, a tag on each, the engine cropped above. It speaks to "provided", but frame 9 already plans the two accent cases at a threshold, so it would spend the close's picture on the cover.

PICK: 1. It is the only one whose picture explains the whole headline at a glance, a real Army aviation machine in a clean shop, and it hands frame 2 (a close on the record) a clear change of scale.

TYPE: headline high in the cerulean wall, two lines, left; dek low on the peach sweep floor in front of the stand, so the engine owns the middle band and bleeds right.
