# Slide 04 concepts, UP TO (c6)

1. **Two bars in clear stock, by the shop window.** On a blue granite surface plate under a shop window, two milled 6061 bars lie side by side, butted against one steel stop block, each sealed inside a clear glass block the length of the full stock it came from, one bar 70 and one 27 of that length, helical chips curled at each cut face and a machinist's rule along the front. Only this beat compares two reductions as two lengths taken off one whole at one scale, and the clear remainder past each cut face is the "up to" made visible.

2. **The cut offs on the saw table.** A bandsaw table seen low and side on, two aluminium billets each cut through, the kept piece clamped and the cut off lying where it fell, the falling pieces 30 and 73 long. It makes the removed part the subject, which is exactly the sentence "smaller by", though the kept and fallen pieces read as four objects and the figure splits in two.

3. **Two columns of record plates in glass tubes.** Two clear glass cylinders standing on a lab bench, each filled with a stack of thin aluminium record plates to 70 and 27 of the tube's height, the empty glass above as what was cut. Vertical and countable, but it reads as a bar chart dressed in glass, and the neighbour after uses a rule.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where the whole and the part are one object each, so a stranger reads "the short one lost most of itself" in one look, and its world (window lit shop, perspective, medium) differs from frame 3's orthographic studio tray and frame 5's flat relief rule.

Type: the bars and glass own the middle of the frame, the shop wall and window the top. The headline sits low in two lines over the plate's deep blue front edge, the dek under it, so the window light stays clear above.
