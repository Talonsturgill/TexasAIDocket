# Slide 08 concepts, LUBBOCK TO FORT WORTH

1. **The riveted map plate.** A machined relief of Texas in warm caliche stone on a cerulean anodised field, every county a hairline groove, Lubbock County standing proud in South Plains red and Tarrant County in pale limestone, joined by one milled channel across the Rolling Plains that carries seven domed brass rivets, one per year of the partnership. Only this beat has two named Texas places and a span of years, so a route between two counties that is literally fastened, year by year, is the sentence drawn.

2. **Two county blanks, lap joined.** Lubbock and Tarrant counties cut out of plate as two loose parts, red and limestone, overlapping on a cerulean bench with a bare aluminium splice strap riveted across the seam in the way an airframe repair joins two skins. It says partnership through the deck's own trade, but without the state around them the two shapes are anonymous polygons a stranger can't place.

3. **The I-20 corridor at first light.** A raked txthree aerial of the High Plains falling to the Cross Timbers, one road ribbon running from a small grid of Lubbock lights to a Fort Worth skyline on the horizon. It is the most literal "to", but it is a photograph of a place, it needs a fake skyline, and frame 9 already owns first light outdoors.

**Pick: 1.** The map plate is the only concept a stranger can read as "Lubbock to Fort Worth" with the type covered, and the seven rivets put "since 2019" into the metal instead of the caption. It also sits apart from both neighbours: frame 7 is a hangar interior with a person's hand and frame 9 an outdoor dawn wide, while this is a plan view relief with no engine and no gold.

**Composition.** Texas fills the frame and bleeds off the left and the bottom (El Paso and the Valley cropped). The headline and dek sit in the quiet top right, in the notch over the Red River east of the Panhandle, where the field is open cerulean plate, left aligned in a column beside the Panhandle. Lubbock's red boss sits at the centre left as the focal point, and the riveted channel leads the eye down and right to Tarrant.
