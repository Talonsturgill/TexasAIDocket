## Phase 8, the story, and why this one

**The decision.** The Department of Homeland Security Science and Technology Directorate ran
an operational assessment of facial biometric image capture on the pedestrian exit lane at the
Progreso International Bridge in Hidalgo County, for U.S. Customs and Border Protection. It
published the account on September 1st, 2026. It is item tx-2026-0120 in the record as of this
run, so the deck stands on a decision the record holds.

**The angle.** The account is written in adjectives, and the only hard figure in it belongs to
the crossing rather than to the machine. Capture "exceeded expectations". Images were "high
quality". The technology "had to work with limited infrastructure, in a challenging
environment, and do it autonomously" and "it did just that". Against all of that the page
states no accuracy rate, no count of images captured, no retention period and no deployment
decision. The one number it publishes, 1.1 million pedestrians exiting to Mexico a year, is a
fact about the bridge.

**Why not the others.** Executive Order 14421 names no Texas anywhere, and a deck about it
would be the "change three nouns and this is Ohio" failure the field notes already paid for.
Dell's record AI server orders are a quarterly result rather than a decision. The National
Science Foundation's operations center is now in the record and would have made a decent deck,
except that the Texas Advanced Computing Center's own host is off limits to this project under
its robots policy, so the deck could not go and look at the thing it is about. The Alpha School
voucher story rests on one newsroom this run.

**Dedupe.** `dedupe_check` returns one faint entry at 0.21, the August 29th deck on the National
Science Foundation robotics center, sharing only generic words plus "opt-out". That deck's angle
WAS consent and opt-out procedures, so the opt-out lane here is one frame in this deck and never
its thesis. Surveillance was the beat on August 20th and August 30th and both were local
automated plate readers, one a nonbinding ballot instrument and one a funding pause. A federal
face capture test at a Rio Grande Valley footbridge is a different instrument, a different
actor and a different place.

**`texan_check` at selection** returns place yes, body yes, deadline yes, next step NO. There is
no comment window and no hearing on this test, confirmed against the Federal Register with the
comment window filter open on every Homeland Security document. So the closing frame takes the
route deck no. 10 already established, naming the nearest dated public room the record actually
holds and saying plainly that it belongs to a different program.

## Phase 1, craft refresh

One rotating focus, chosen because it is this deck's whole subject. **Expectation based
visualization**, which embeds an explicit reference model that externalizes what should be
present, and the finding that absence detection rises sharply when a reader is prompted to look
for what is missing. The instruction to the directors follows from it. Do not draw an empty
frame. Draw the SLOT the figure would sit in, ruled and labelled and empty, beside the slots
that are full.
