Kodiak AI scored itself at 93 percent at the end of August.

The figure is the share of claims and evidence in its driverless safety case the company calls materially complete. It isn't a safety rating.

Kodiak says it closes that case before trucks run unsupervised on the 219 mile leg from Dallas to Houston. It expects both by year end. Most of the leg is Interstate 45. It counts more than 1,300 loads and 750,000 autonomous miles for IKEA over four years, with a safety observer aboard.

Its chief executive says the work left is identified and scheduled, concentrated on final engineering verification and validation.

Kodiak counts more than 3.5 million autonomous miles since 2019. How many ran between Dallas and Houston?
