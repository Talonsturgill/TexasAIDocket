Kodiak AI scores itself at 93 percent.

The figure is the share of claims and evidence in its driverless safety case that the company calls materially complete. It isn't a safety rating.

That case must close before trucks run unsupervised on 219 miles of Interstate 45 from Dallas-Fort Worth to Houston. Kodiak expects 100 percent and a launch by year end. It says the safety observer never touched the wheel on its Lancaster to Houston runs. Every figure is the company's own.

TxDMV's authorization is free. The agency says the law doesn't let it require crash history. Its Enforcement Division takes concerns about automated vehicles operating in ways that could result in serious bodily injury or death.

Kodiak's release leaves one figure out. How many of its more than 3.5 million autonomous miles since 2019 ran between Dallas and Houston?
