An expert panel with radiologists at Baylor College of Medicine and Texas Children's Hospital in Houston and at UT Southwestern in Dallas names off-label use of adult-trained AI models among its concerns for children. "Unlike adults, children go through continuous physiologic and anatomic changes that require age-specific models trained on representative developmental data."

A second paper, with MD Anderson in Houston and the UT Medical Branch in Galveston among its departments, put a number on the same move. Built on 198 brain metastasis patients, it met an independent 69 unchanged. The C-index read 0.615 in training and 0.574 outside it. Its authors call that "limited standalone discrimination". Both items are dated September 9th.
