An expert panel published the same day names the practice this sits inside. Radiologists at Baylor College of Medicine and Texas Children's Hospital in Houston and at UT Southwestern in Dallas put "off-label use of adult-trained AI models" among their concerns for children. "Unlike adults, children go through continuous physiologic and anatomic changes that require age-specific models trained on representative developmental data."
