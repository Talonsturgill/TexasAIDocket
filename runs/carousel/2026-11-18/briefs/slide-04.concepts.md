# Slide 04 concepts, WHAT IT READ

1. **A carved column of air (relief, picked).** A bas-relief cut into a cerulean plaster wall, raked from the upper left: a rounded column of air in eight glassy bands, each with its own surface (smooth flow lines for the gases, fine grain for the smaller particulate, coarse grain for the larger, droplets for aerosols), standing on a cut plug of Texas ground in three weathered strata, the whole column outlined in an inlaid dusk gold bead, and the sulfur dioxide band projecting proud and wider in satin gold so it throws the longest shadow. Only this beat has it because it is a geologist's column applied to the air, the one picture where "the air has parts" and "one part led" are the same object.

2. **Eight gas bottles at a sampling bench (studio spatial).** A row of eight rounded glass sample flasks on a cerulean sweep, each tinted, one pulled forward in gold. Rejected because frame 3 is an ice studio, it would read as a lab product shot, and a row of eight bottles is the "rows of one object" template the doctrine names.

3. **Eight ribbons of air over a mesquite flat at dusk (outdoor world).** A low camera under a sky crossed by eight translucent stratified ribbons, one lit gold. Rejected because frame 5 is outdoor land from above, and drawn ribbons in a real sky would imply altitudes the dek says the bands are not.

**Pick, in one line.** Concept 1, because the column only makes sense as "eight parts, one strongest" and is the deck's planned measured relief, unlike the studio before it and the pasture after it.

**Composition with the type.** The column stands in the right half (x 590 to 990, y 150 to 960) on its plug, so the left column of wall stays plain deep cerulean. The headline sits in that column, left 72, around y 250, four lines, the dek under it, both seated on a soft indigo tint, with the proud gold band level with the headline so the eye runs from the words to the band.
