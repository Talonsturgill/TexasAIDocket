# Slide 01 concepts, READ FROM ORBIT

1. **The cut slab under a sheet of air.** Texas cut from its real outline as a thick slab of land, its top coloured by region from caliche to pine, every one of the 254 county lines engraved in it, with one thin dusk gold glass sheet of air floating over the whole state and its soft shadow falling across the counties. Only this beat has it, because the sentence is "all 254 counties, read from above", and the picture is exactly that, every county visible at once under one measured layer of air.

2. **The swath over the Gulf at dawn.** A wide aerial from very high over the Gulf of Mexico looking north at a curved Texas coast with the land under a thin band of haze, a single dusk gold swath line sweeping the state north to south. It says "from above" as altitude, but the counties are invisible at that scale, so the 254 drops out of the picture and it shares the satellite world with frame 03.

3. **A glass map table seen from the ceiling.** A lit table of 254 frosted glass county tiles fitted together like a mosaic, a gold light panel lowered over it on cables, seen from straight above. It carries the count, but a top down tray reads as a chart (charged lately) and fitted tiles repeat frame 07's 254 flagstones.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one that shows all 254 counties at once and the air over them as one object, so swapping in any other headline breaks it, and it shares neither the outdoor ground of frame 02 nor the studio satellite of frame 03.

Composition: the slab fills the lower 58 percent, seen from high over the Gulf so east sits frame right; the headline sits high in the open cerulean sky above the Panhandle in three lines, left aligned, with the short dek under it, so the sky is the quiet the art leaves for it.
