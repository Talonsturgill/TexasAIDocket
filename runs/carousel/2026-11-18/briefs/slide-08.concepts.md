# Slide 08 concepts, A place is not a person

1. THE SHOULDER AND THE KERB. A resident seen large from just behind the shoulder looks out over a fall pasture, and a low dusk gold kerb set in the grass runs from beside their boots far out past a limestone ranch house, live oaks and a pickup on a caliche road before it turns a corner near the horizon. Only this beat has it because one gold edge holds a house, trees, a road and a person at once, so the satellite's reading is plainly of the place and the one person in it is not what it measured.

2. THE SPECIMEN PLOT. On an ice cerulean studio sweep a cut square of pasture turf stands like a museum specimen, its four edges trimmed in dusk gold, with a small house, an oak and a single standing figure rooted in it. Only this beat has it because the reading is shown as a slab of ground with everything on it averaged together, and the one figure is a speck inside it rather than its subject.

3. THE PORCH AT EYE LEVEL. A low camera on a porch step looks out through a screen door at a yard where the air above the lawn is tinted faintly gold up to a soft ceiling, and a resident on the step sits under the porch roof outside the tint. Only this beat has it because the gold air and the person occupy different spaces in one frame, showing that the measure of the air over a place says nothing about the person on the porch.

## Pick

Concept 1. The headline is about scale between a place and a person, and only concept 1 puts a person and a whole reading's edge in one picture at true human eye height, so a stranger feels the person is one small part of what the gold line encloses. Its neighbours are a raking stone relief (7) and a raking terrain relief (9), so a spatial outdoor world at a shoulder shares neither medium nor camera. Concept 2 sits too near frame 9's outlined block, and concept 3 builds a porch interior nobody asked for.

## Composition with the type

The person fills the left third from the frame bottom up past the horizon. The kerb rises from near their boots to a corner right of centre at the horizon, and the far leg runs left behind the house and oaks. The headline sits top right in the open cerulean sky over the corner, in two or three lines. The dek sits low right over the near grass, clear of the kerb, so the middle band stays open for the house, the oaks and the corner.
