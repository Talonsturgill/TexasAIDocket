# Slide 06, EAST TO WEST: three concepts

1. **Three vitrines of Texas air.** Three slabs of real ground stand in a row on a cerulean studio sweep, west caliche with a mesquite, central limestone with a live oak and a juniper, east red clay with loblolly pines, each sealed under a tall dusk gold glass block whose haze is thin at frame left and thick at frame right. Only this beat has three clusters with a direction, and the haze stepping up from west to east over three grounds a Texan can tell apart is the sentence itself.

2. **One long core across the state.** A single 14 m museum core of Texas ground laid west to east in an orthographic side elevation, its strata changing from caliche to limestone to red clay, under one continuous gold glass air band that steps darker in three places. It draws direction as a line, but it is an orthographic section and a relief, too near frame 4's column and frame 7's raking stone floor.

3. **The horizon from a highway shoulder.** An eye level wide view along an empty farm road at morning in the v2explainer world, the sky clear and cerulean over mesquite at frame left and thickening to a gold haze over a pine line at frame right. It feels like weather, but it is an outdoor world like frames 5 and 8, and real haze would read as a forecast of a place rather than an order between clusters.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one that makes three separate clusters, rather than a gradient, visible at a glance, it keeps the haze an illustrative ordering in a studio rather than a claim about a real sky, and its studio medium differs from frame 5's outdoor stake field and frame 7's stone relief.
