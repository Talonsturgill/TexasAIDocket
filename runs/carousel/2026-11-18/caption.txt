Does dirtier air go with more cancer?

County by county across Texas, it tended to. A UT Dallas team paired satellite readings of eight air pollutants from 2022 with CDC cancer data for all 254 counties. Machine learning did the weighing. Sulfur dioxide was the strongest environmental predictor. The paper ran March 5th in Environmental Monitoring and Assessment.

The east has worse air and the west better. High cancer counties sit near other high cancer counties. Not every more polluted place had more cancer.

Ground monitors are costly. The paper says satellites can help where none stand. Satellite readings may be less precise.

Stand in any one of those counties. The researchers say the results don't establish that pollution causes cancer in the person standing there.
