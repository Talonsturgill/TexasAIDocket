65 decibels with the agreement. 85 without.

The City of Taylor lists both for Taylor Technology Campus, a proposed 664-acre data center and power project east of town near FM 112. The 65 rises if the site was already louder.

The council's October 8th vote reads like a yes or no on a data center. KEYE reports about 1,400 signatures for a ban the city found can't reach the ballot.

The city's case is that without the agreement the project stays in unincorporated Williamson County, with no zoning and no say for Taylor at all.

With it, the land would be annexed under terms the deck stakes out in feet. Data centers would sit at least 700 feet from the south line.

One exception lets drinking water into cooling if the city misses its deadline for the reclaimed water improvements. Who holds the city to that date?
