Left in the county, no zoning. Annexed into Taylor, terms in feet.

The city published both sides before the October 8th council vote. The land is 664 acres east of the city limits, for data centers and on-site power.

The proposed terms put data centers, generation and batteries at least 700 feet from the south line near FM 112. The city says landscaped buffers would separate the campus from nearby homes. The developers are committing $28.2 million and ask for no tax abatement.

The slides stake those distances on the ground.

The noise term waits on one measurement. The cap is the greater of 65 dBA or the land's level before construction, against the state's 85 dBA without the agreement. An acoustic study at the property lines would set that level.

How loud is that ground along FM 112 before anything is built?
