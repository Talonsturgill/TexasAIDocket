Left in Williamson County, no zoning. Annexed into Taylor, terms in feet.

The city published both sides before the October 8th council vote. The land is 664 acres east of the city limits, for data centers and on-site power.

The proposed terms put data centers, generation and batteries at least 700 feet from the south line near FM 112. The developers would commit $28.2 million to the city, Taylor ISD and nonprofits. They ask for no tax abatement.

The noise term waits on one measurement. Without the agreement the city lists the state's 85 dBA. With it the cap is 65 dBA or the level before construction, whichever is greater. An acoustic study at the property lines would set that level.

The council's regular meetings are in the City Hall Council Chambers.
