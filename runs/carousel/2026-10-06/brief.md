# Directors' brief, 2026-10-06

## The story (verified facts live ONLY in out/2026-10-06/claims.json; cite claim ids)
The City of Taylor (Williamson County, Blackland Prairie farm country northeast of Austin) has a
proposed development agreement with Big Watt Digital and PowerHouse Data Centers for Taylor
Technology Campus, formerly Project Mustang: a 664-acre project with data center facilities and
on-site power generation. The City Council votes on it at its October 8th, 2026 meeting, two days
after this deck posts. The developers had withdrawn their annexation and zoning application. The
city says without the agreement the land stays in unincorporated Williamson County, which has no
zoning authority, and the city would have no authority over any aspect of the project.

The city published its own WITH and WITHOUT table. With the agreement: noise capped at the greater
of 65 dBA or the pre-development level (without: state limit of 85 dBA, no county rules). Dark Skies
lighting (without: none). A closed loop cooled with reclaimed water, initial fill 1.575 million
gallons, potable water barred from cooling except in three named circumstances (without: none).
Wastewater to city pretreatment rules (without: a TCEQ permit, treating on site and discharging to
a creek with no city oversight), and the developers withdraw their TCEQ discharge permit
application and improve the plant feeding Mustang Creek. Buildings 300 feet from the north, east and
west lines; data centers, generation and batteries 700 feet from the south line, accessory 500; a
400 foot landscaped buffer along the south line near FM 112 with earthen berms at least 6 feet high
(without: none). Backup generators tested monthly on weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. $28.2 million in
community commitments, and no tax abatement asked. The site generates its own power with the
ability to sell to the grid. Air stays with TCEQ either way.

The counter-image: residents petitioned to ban new data centers, and the agreement is the city
choosing written terms over having no say at all. Nothing is approved before October 8th. Every
term is the city's description of a PROPOSED agreement whose full text was not found posted.
