TxDMV told Dallas it can't require crash history from driverless truck operators.

Dallas asked for it in rulemaking comments. The adopted rule answers that the statute does not allow it.

Since May 28th a driverless freight company needs a TxDMV authorization. It rests on one acknowledged statement with six conditions and a responder plan given to DPS, with no fee and no expiry.

Kodiak plans unsupervised driverless service from Dallas to Houston by year end.

The department's line is serious bodily injury. Below it the department says administrative action is unlikely. Roadside enforcement stays with DPS and local law enforcement. Concerns about one that could cause serious bodily injury or death go to its Enforcement Division.

TxDMV says law enforcement can check whether a truck is authorized. Would you know where to report one?
