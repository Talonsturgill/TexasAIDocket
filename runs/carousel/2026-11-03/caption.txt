Aurora told its shareholders about weigh stations.

Autonomous trucks have relied on voluntary inspections to bypass them, the October 28th letter says. It says the Aurora Driver can now tell whether a station is open, steer to an available scale lane and adapt to different scale configurations.

The letter puts the production ramp about six weeks behind its prior plan. Third quarter revenue was $3 million against a $257 million operating loss, stock-based compensation included.

Aurora names Dallas to Houston as its own route. Charger Logistics plans to start between Dallas and Laredo under a non-binding MOU, the letter says.

The letter is on the SEC's EDGAR site. Has a Texas scale house weighed a driverless Aurora truck yet?
