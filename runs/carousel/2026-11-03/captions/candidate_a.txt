Aurora's trucks used to bypass the scale. Now they pick the lane.

Voluntary inspections waved them past. Aurora's October 28th shareholder letter says its Driver can now tell if a station is open, find an available scale lane and adapt to the scale it meets.

Aurora counts 20+ trucks built and names Dallas to Houston as its own route. The letter puts production about six weeks behind its plan. Third quarter revenue was $3 million against a $257 million operating loss.

The rest is expected. Direct runs to customer sites through security checkpoints in November. More than 1,000 trucks at the end of 2027. Charger Logistics plans a start between Dallas and Laredo under a non-binding MOU.

Not one Texas weigh station or customer site is named.

The deck runs from the scale lane to the books.

When a driverless truck reaches a Texas scale, who besides Aurora will say so?
