Aurora Innovation wrote its shareholders on October 28th.

What does a truck with no driver do at a weigh station?

Autonomous trucks have relied on voluntary inspections to bypass them, the letter says. Now the Aurora Driver decides if a station is open. It finds an available scale lane and adapts to different scales.

Still open is the build. The production ramp is about six weeks behind plan. Third quarter revenue was $3 million against a $257 million operating loss, stock-based compensation included. Charger Logistics plans to start the Aurora Driver between Dallas and Laredo under a non-binding MOU. AUMOVIO is finalizing a line for Aurora's third-generation hardware in New Braunfels.

The deck rides one tractor onto the scale, then weighs the letter's numbers.

The letter names no Texas weigh station. Has a Texas scale house weighed one yet?
