One says agency. One says provider.

SEC. 552.051, IN FORCE SINCE JANUARY 1ST, 2026
(b)
A governmental agency
that makes available an artificial intelligence system intended to interact with consumers shall disclose to each consumer, before or at the time of interaction
(c)
A person
is required to make the disclosure under Subsection (b) regardless of whether it would be obvious to a reasonable consumer that the consumer is interacting with an artificial intelligence system
(f)
the provider of the
service or treatment
shall provide the disclosure under Subsection (b) to the recipient
The word governmental does not appear in (f).
Read the other way, (f) sets only the timing for a duty (b) gives to an agency.
