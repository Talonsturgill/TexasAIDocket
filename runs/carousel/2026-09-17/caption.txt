At Memorial Hermann in Houston, software writes the note before the clinician does.

The assistant is built into Epic and drafts the note a patient gets with a lab result. The clinician can review and edit it.

The system reports 32% fewer follow-up messages when clinicians started from the draft, and nine seconds saved per result comment. healthsystemCIO wrote on September 10th that the figures come from Epic and its customer, and that the 32% counts clinicians who chose it.

Section 552.051 took effect January 1st. Subsection (b) puts its disclosure duty on a governmental agency. Subsection (f) puts it on the provider of the service or treatment. Chapter 552 has no section headed Applicability.

Nine slides go from the announcement to the statute. Ask who wrote the note, before a first visit.

Does subsection (f) reach a hospital that is not an agency?
