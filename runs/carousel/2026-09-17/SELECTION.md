# Phase 8 — selection, and why this story and not the others

**Chosen: tx-2026-0162.** Memorial Hermann Health System's clinicians using Epic's Art assistant
to draft the plain language note a Houston patient receives beside a lab or imaging result.

## Why this one

1. **It is AI IN USE, with a person on the other end of it.** `APPLICATIONS.md` is explicit that
   the deck must not narrate back the filings the docket already publishes every day. This is a
   machine writing the sentence that tells a Texan what their own body's test result means, at a
   volume the health system itself puts at over 55,000 messages. Nothing else on today's board
   reaches a member of the public that directly.
2. **The record holds it.** Admitted this run at Phase 5, with a primary corporate source and a
   county. A deck about a decision the docket does not carry is a deck undermining the site it
   links to.
3. **The counter image is published, dated and specific.** A trade publication read the same
   announcement two days later and named exactly what its three figures do not establish. This
   deck does not have to manufacture its own scepticism, which is the failure mode of an honest
   deck built on a company release.
4. **The turn is a statute and it is genuinely open.** Texas has told a governmental agency since
   January 1st, 2026 that it must say when a machine is talking to you. Subsection (f) extends
   that disclosure to "the provider of the service or treatment" and never says whether that
   provider must be governmental. The fact-checker refused, correctly, to resolve it. A deck that
   ends on a real unsettled question is stronger than one that ends on a verdict it invented.

## Why not the others

- **The National Science Foundation's award to a UT Austin robotics centre (tx-2026-0163).**
  Admitted to the record this run and a fine item. It is the WRONG DECK TODAY because of the
  instrument rather than the topic. Yesterday's deck, carousel no. 26, read a university release
  against the federal award record that funds it, and its own ledger entry records that two
  judges named that same instrument as a repeat of carousel no. 11 eighteen days earlier.
  `dedupe_check` compares topic, entities and keywords, so it is structurally blind to an
  instrument repeat. Running it a third time in two days would be doing knowingly what the
  ledger flagged as a blind spot.
- **Kodiak's 35 driverless trucks in the Permian.** The best story on the field beat and it is
  held in the seed, not in the record, because the release names no Texas county. A deck about
  an item the record is holding is a deck about something the site does not carry.
- **The license plate reader split across North Texas.** Dallas police switching off 321 cameras
  on September 15th while Carrollton renewed a contract for 82 the same day, a week after Bastrop
  banned them outright. This is the most Texan story of the day and it is not shippable this run.
  Every account of it reached this morning is journalism. The Carrollton agenda answered 403 to
  every path tried, the Bastrop resolution is a PDF nobody could read, and the Dallas briefing
  sits behind a city hall host that did not answer. Held as a lead for a run that reaches the
  council records.
- **Samsung's Taylor fab running Tesla's chip.** Korean trade press citing unnamed industry
  sources, with no Samsung or Tesla statement. Below the primary source bar.
- **The University of North Texas gift (tx-2026-0165).** Admitted to the record. It is money and
  a name rather than a machine doing anything, and its own summary says so.

## What texan_check said at selection, and what follows from it

    places Harris County, Houston / body NO / deadline NO / next step NO

The story has a place and no dated door. The rule is that a placeless story is not capped and a
story with no next step is, and that the closing frame is the cheapest frame in the deck to
rewrite. So frame 9 carries the next step from the start rather than discovering it from a judge
in round four, and the directors were briefed with that. The material for it is verified and in
the claims file: the attorney general is required to maintain an online complaint mechanism under
the chapter, and the chapter gives a patient no suit of their own.
