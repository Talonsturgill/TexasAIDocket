Waymo lists 1,259. International Transport Eng lists 7.

Both sit on one report from the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles, data as of October 29th. It counts 3,164 automated vehicles from 17 companies. Waymo and Tesla Robotaxi hold 74 percent of them.

Since May 28th a company running automated vehicles commercially in Texas needs a state authorization. It carries no fee and never expires.

The City of Dallas asked the department to require crash history. The department answered that the Transportation Code doesn't allow it.

A concern that an automated vehicle could cause serious bodily injury or death can go to the department's Enforcement Division. Below that standard it calls administrative enforcement unlikely.

Each car in the lot on the slides stands for ten listed vehicles.
