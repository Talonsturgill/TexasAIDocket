The Texas DMV answered Dallas and Austin.

Dallas asked the department to require crash history and make approved operators public. Austin asked for a sticker or placard on each vehicle and local collision data.

The department said state law doesn't let it require crash history. It tied public access to Government Code Chapter 552. Austin's asks fell outside its rulemaking authority.

Required since May 28th, the authorization asks for contact details and a certification that DPS has a first responder plan. No fee. No expiry. A light lift for operators. Less than the cities asked for.
