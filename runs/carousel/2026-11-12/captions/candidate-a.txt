Waymo lists 1,259. International Transport Eng lists 7.

Both sit on one Texas DMV report, data as of October 29th. It counts 3,164 automated vehicles from 17 companies. Waymo and Tesla Robotaxi hold 74 percent.

The rule is known. Since May 28th a company running automated vehicles commercially in Texas needs a state authorization. It carries no fee and never expires. The door in is wide.

What the department can't require is crash history. The City of Dallas asked for it. The department said the Transportation Code doesn't allow it.

A concern about serious bodily injury or death goes to the department's Enforcement Division. Below that bar it calls administrative action unlikely.

The deck parks the list in one lot, a car per ten vehicles.

Senate Bill 2807 created the authorization. Should lawmakers let the department require crash history?
