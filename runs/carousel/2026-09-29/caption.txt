Up to five years in El Paso Electric's plan. The life of the plant in the judges'.

The plant would be 813 gas generators beside Meta's AI data center in northeast El Paso County. The data center would carry the plant's capital costs for a bridge of up to five years. After that the utility wanted flexibility on who pays. Any change would need Commission approval in a separate case.

Two judges recommend approval only if other customers are held harmless from the plant's capital and operating costs for its life. Otherwise they would recommend denial. The condition lifts only if the utility shows the plant meets the statute's test.

El Paso City Council has authorized a counteroffer for a full hold harmless.

The judges' findings put the plant's total estimated cost at approximately $499.8 million. How much of that has a named payer after the bridge?
