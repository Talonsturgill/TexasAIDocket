# Critic round, before the panel

Five pixel critics (slides 1 and 2, 3 and 4, 5 and 7, 6 and 8, 9) and one flow critic read the
first full render. All nine frames came back revise. What each named, in short, and what the run
did about it:

- Slides 1 and 2. Blank slabs for units with no transformer, a mannequin with no contact shadow,
  asphalt-dark ground, a flat topped 3 reading as 5 in the hook. The near units became the full
  kit model, the transformer went into frame 2, the sun went from 3.5 to 9 degrees, and hook
  numerals were set in the sans.
- Slides 3 and 4. Mauve ground, a dead foreground, the 366 bar lost against the beam's shadow,
  labels crossing leaders, red flags on the stakes. The aerial was dollied in, the plant became its
  own bar, the labels were bracketed over their own segments, the flags went.
- Slides 5 and 7. Frame 5 was a desk interior where the dossier declared a transformer door, and
  the quotes floated on the wall. Frame 7 was an object in a void. Frame 5 was rebuilt at the door
  with the judges' lines set on the page, the vendor frame rebuilt with ground, horizon and riser.
- Slides 6 and 8. The fence was seen along its length, so five spans took about 45 percent of it
  and the ratio lied. It was rebuilt side on with painted panels, post 0 at the left.
- Slide 9. The foreground read as a lake, the rows were unidentifiable, the sun floated whole over
  the ridge. One ground, rows brought forward, the year fence placed, the grade darkened.
- Flow. Frame 5 left the world, frame 7 sat between the before and after, every frame one
  skeleton, the edge tease not visible. The vendor frame moved to 6, the fence frames became 7 and
  8, and the edge tease was dropped from the storyboard after panel round 1.
