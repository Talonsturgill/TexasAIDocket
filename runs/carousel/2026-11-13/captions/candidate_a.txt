Every Large Load answered ERCOT.

That is all 245 loads of 75 MW or greater, in a preliminary ERCOT summary for the utility commission's November 5th open meeting. In total 433 of 465 loads responded, covering about 177 GW. West Texas holds the largest share, 73 GW.

What went unanswered?

Respondents answered 83% of nearly 67,000 questions. Cooling drew the largest share of the unanswered ones. Respondents indicated many projects had not determined their final cooling configuration. Projects asking to energize in 2028 or later answered N/A 30% more often than those due sooner.

ERCOT is targeting a published final report on December 10th. The commission's calendar lists an open meeting on December 17th.

Will the December 10th report count how many projects still haven't settled on cooling?
