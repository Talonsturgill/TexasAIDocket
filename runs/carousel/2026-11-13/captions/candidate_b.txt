ERCOT set itself December 10th.

Its September notice said it expected to publish a data center report by then. A preliminary summary went to the Public Utility Commission on November 5th.

The projects could say how much power they want. 433 of 465 loads answered, covering about 177 GW. All 245 Large Loads of 75 MW or greater responded. West Texas holds 41% of the capacity requested, 73 GW.

What many could not say yet was how they would cool the buildings. Of nearly 67,000 questions, respondents answered 83%. Cooling held the largest share of the rest.

The power ask is specific. For many projects the cooling plan is not.

The commission's calendar lists an open meeting on December 17th. Will the December 10th report say more about cooling than the preliminary one could?
