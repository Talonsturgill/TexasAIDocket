Every Large Load answered ERCOT.

That is all 245 loads of 75 MW or greater. The count comes from a preliminary ERCOT summary for the utility commission's November 5th open meeting. In total 433 of 465 loads responded, covering about 177 GW. West Texas holds 41% of the capacity requested, 73 GW.

What went unanswered?

Respondents answered 83% of nearly 67,000 questions. Cooling questions drew N/A answers at the highest rate. Respondents indicated many projects had not determined their final cooling configuration. Projects asking to energize in 2028 or later answered N/A 30% more often than those due sooner.

ERCOT is targeting a published final report on December 10th.
