A vial of fruit flies with one gene switched off.

They had trouble moving and lived shorter lives. The fly gene is sff. Its human version is BRSK1.

A child in the Texome Project got no answer from standard tests. An AI tool called AI-MARRVEL then flagged a rare change in BRSK1.

The normal human gene largely corrected the flies. Patient versions only partly did. Researchers at Texas Children's and Baylor College of Medicine call the BRSK1 variants a likely diagnosis. The software pointed. Flies ran the test.

AI-MARRVEL's published test reports 98% precision on one confidence metric. It found 57% of diagnosable cases among 871. Neither is an accuracy rate.

Texome offers free genetic testing to people in Texas who can't pay. The address is on the last frame.

Who looks for the diagnosable cases AI-MARRVEL doesn't flag?
