# Pixel and flow critics, carousel no. 40

Summarised from the critics' returned verdicts. The full panel cards are in `scores/r1` to `scores/r5`.

## Pixel round 1 (five critics, slides 1 to 9)

Every verdict was revise. Named on most frames: the 2D lens blur was masked by the vial's
silhouette, so the city stayed sharp through the glass and blurred around it; there was no golden
hour sun on the coping; the tape was a flat billboard; flies, pupae and condensation read as one
field of specks at 432 px; the four rooms showed no light source and no chair; frame 6 read as a
pill bottle with the green tape on a frame declared accent none; frame 7's fly heights were the
same in all five vials and its labels sat on three baselines; frame 8 had no foot and repeated
frame 6.

## Pixel round 2 (three critics) and the flow critic

Every verdict was still revise. The rooms lacked a visible window and the hero was too small in
them; frame 6, the declared turn, read as a cork with ants; frame 5's tapes misstated 98 to 57 on
screen, since the two tapes sat at different depths; frame 9's tape faced the camera; the coping
read as a dark slab. The flow critic named frame 6 as the turn that does not land, the close as
two reprises, and the hero too small on 2, 3, 4 and 9.
