Everyone guesses more screens.

Houston ISD built Future 2 for what its superintendent calls an AI-enabled world. The district describes its afternoon experiences as screen-free. That is a claim about those experiences and not about the whole school day.

What the record holds. C. Martinez and Gregg elementary convert to pre-K-8 for the 2026 to 2027 school year. Instruction runs 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. with optional programming to 6:00 p.m. The first four hours are the standard NES curriculum, down to a subject called Art of Thinking.

What the record does not hold. The schedule names a one hour AI literacy and design thinking course, a 110 minute experiences block and a 90 minute workshop block, then never says which one falls when. The accelerated platform offered to the top percentile scorers has no vendor named.

Board policy CQD(LOCAL) on artificial intelligence reached second reading on August 13th, 2026. No action is recorded and the minutes are still in draft, which is ordinary this soon after a meeting. The status line on the item reads "Agenda Ready".
