# Storyboard — Texas AI Docket, Carousel No. 2 — August 18th, 2026

## The story

Houston ISD built a school model for the AI era. The clearest thing the district publishes about
its afternoon is what is not in it. Around that removal the record is provisional: the campus page
states no total, the schedule does not say which block falls when, the platform has no vendor named,
and the board policy that would govern artificial intelligence reached second reading on
August 13th with no recorded action.

Record anchor: `tx-2026-0053`, re-verified this run.

## Synthesis note

Three treatments were pitched. The spine and the hero come from **the withheld screen**, whose
risk analysis was the sharpest of the three and whose opening image is the strongest. Two
structural laws are grafted from **the shape of the day**. The record-fields close is grafted from
**the unfinished record**.

**REJECTED from the shape of the day: the 510 minute accounting.** It proposed that the
instructional day is 510 minutes, that the named blocks account for 500, and that the schedule does
not describe the other 10. The arithmetic runs, but c4 says the curriculum separates the day into
three parts and c3 says the first four hours follow the standard curriculum, and no source states
those are disjoint. Adding them may double count. A residual computed from an unstated assumption
is an inference wearing a measurement's clothes, which is the exact defect that shipped a wrong
FIVE in the sibling product. The number is not published anywhere in this deck.

## Two structural laws, deck wide

**NO CIRCLES.** No clock faces, no dials, no radial figures. A school day is a cut segment rather
than a cycle, and the bar-not-dial doctrine is a rule about shape as much as about verdicts.

**THE EVIDENCE GRAMMAR.** A filled form means the source states it. An outlined form with no fill
means the source calls it optional or does not describe it. Taught on slide 4 and paid on 7 and 9. **Slide 5 does NOT use it**, and the flow critic
was right to say so: the unstated thing there is position, which is expressed by non-alignment
rather than by outline. The plan is corrected rather than the slide, because outlining a tile
would say its LENGTH was unstated, and the length is the one thing the source does give.
This is what lets the deck be exact about its own evidence without a word of hedging.

A post-war Houston ISD elementary building standing on the Gulf Coast prairie of Harris County.

**Red is unspent.** No comment window in this story closes in a period a reader can act on. The
second reading has already happened and no action is recorded, so there is no deadline to offer.
Painting a pending policy red would dramatise a hole.

**Light is declared once and holds:** upper left, shallow, flat overcast, minimal cast shadow.
