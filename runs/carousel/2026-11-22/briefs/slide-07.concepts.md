# Slide 07 concepts, QUESTION THREE (c11 c7)

**A. The handoff bench, from behind the chair.** A long limestone bench runs diagonally away from a seated clinician seen from high behind, three brass handoff trays crossing it at three stations named ask, synthesize and diagnose, every shuttle parked on the clinician's edge and every far pan empty, a laptop glowing cyan at the far end of the bench. Only this beat has a handoff at three points as a diagnosis moves along, and trays at rest show the line the reasoning could cross without claiming it ever did.

**B. The pass-through wall.** An eye level view of an illustrative exam room wall with a three drawer pass-through hatch, like a bank teller drawer, set in limestone, the clinician's side in warm light and a cyan glow behind the frosted far side, all three drawers shut on the near side. It reads as handing over through a wall, but a wall filling the frame repeats the "walls fill the top half" charge and puts the tool behind glass rather than within reach.

**C. One tray, macro.** A worm's eye macro along a single brass tray track, three index cards stacked in the near shuttle pan, the far pan empty and catching a cyan glow from out of frame. Close and strong on material, but it is a close still life on a slab, the same medium and scale as frame 06's slips, and three cards in one pan blur the three stations of c7.

**Pick A.** It is the only one where the three acts of c7 stand as three separate stations along one evolving line and the handoff is a direction across the bench, so swapping in another headline breaks it. It also differs from 06 (close still life) and 08 (low raked counter) by camera, a high three quarter from behind a seated person at medium scale.

## Composition with the type

The bench enters at the lower right, where the clinician sits from behind at station one, and climbs to the upper left, where the laptop glows at its far end. That leaves the upper right triangle of cerulean sweep quiet: the headline sits there, right aligned in three lines, with the dek in a narrow right column under it. The lower left floor is quiet sweep with the bench's long shadow. The three label pills sit on the bench's near edge beside each station, and the three brass question tabs stand on the bench front at station one's end, the third lit.
