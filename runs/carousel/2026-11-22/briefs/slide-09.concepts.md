# Slide 09 concepts, NEXT, THE CURRICULUM

1. **The teaching room before the bell.** From behind the front desk of an empty teaching room, two identical closed laptops lie side by side with one cyan sleep light each and a coiled stethoscope between them, while rows of empty student desks recede either side of a centre aisle and morning sun falls in bars from tall windows. Only this beat has it, because the frame is about where the study's answer lands: a classroom waiting on results, both tools shut, nothing decided yet.

2. **The unwritten syllabus.** A course binder lies open on a lectern in the explainer studio, its tabbed dividers printed with greeked course weeks and the last divider blank, a capped pen across it. It belongs only to the close because "curriculum" is the noun of the headline and a blank tab is the decision the release says the results could inform.

3. **The lecture theatre from the lectern.** A worm's eye from the lectern up the tiers of an empty raked lecture theatre in morning light, two closed laptops on the lectern ledge in the near foreground. It is the close because it turns the camera from the study's encounter rooms to the audience the results are meant for, the next class.

**Pick: 1.** It answers the headline with the place the curriculum lives and keeps the deck's pair of tools (both closed, equal light) as the last image, so it could only close this story; 2 reads as any education story and 3 shares concept 1's room with a weaker subject. Neighbour 8 is a low raked studio counter, so an interior with windows, sun bars and a long one point aisle is a different world, camera and scale.

Composition with the type: headline high over the deep cerulean back wall and the ceiling band, three lines centred to right of the windows; the dek low over the shaded oak front of the desk, under the laptops. The middle band, rows and sun bars and the two laptops, stays clear.
