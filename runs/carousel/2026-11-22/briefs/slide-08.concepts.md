# Slide 08 concepts, ALREADY IN THE CLINIC (c15 c14)

1. **Two rolls on a stainless island.** A stainless clinic counter carries two mounted rolls of exam paper side by side, the back sheet unrolled 1.62 m and the front one 0.76 m (each share times 0.02 m), with a perforation every ten points, and a clinician seen from behind at the far end holding the long sheet's end flat. Exam paper is the clinic's own consumable, so a survey share drawn as paper pulled off a roll can only belong to a sentence about AI already being in the clinic.

2. **The exam table as the gauge.** Two padded exam tables stand side by side in a bright studio, each with its paper pulled down the vinyl from the head roll, one sheet stopping at 0.76 m and the other running 1.62 m nearly to the foot. The table is the clinic itself, so the reading is "how much of the clinic is covered", but frames 1 and 3 already stand exam tables, so it repeats a neighbour's object.

3. **Wall dispensers in a cut away exam room.** A TXT.interior exam room wall carries two wall mounted paper dispensers whose sheets hang straight down the painted wall to 0.76 m and 1.62 m, the long one brushing the baseboard, a tall portrait picture with the type in the empty half of the wall. It reads vertically and is honest, but a wall of hanging paper reads at feed size as two banners, a chart standing up, which judges have charged as "flat elevations reading as UI".

**Pick: 1.** A horizontal pair of sheets on one counter compares by eye at a raked three quarter, it keeps a physical object with depth, edges and contact (not an elevation, not a neighbour's exam table), and the paper roll plus a clinician's hands on it says "clinic" and "already in use" without any screen or result.

Composition: a raked high three quarter from the front left. The counter top and its two sheets take the middle band, the clinician's back and arms fill the upper right, and the type sits LOW, bottom left, over the counter's deep indigo cabinet front, two lines of headline and the dek under it, so the cabinet face is the quiet the type was drawn into.
