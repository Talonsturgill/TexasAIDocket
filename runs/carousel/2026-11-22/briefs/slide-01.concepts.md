# Slide 01 concepts, THE SPLIT

1. **The cutaway twins.** Two mirrored simulation rooms built as an architect's cutaway model, ceilings off and side walls raked down toward the viewer, standing on a cerulean studio sweep and sharing one glass wall, each with an exam table, a seated actor, a clinician from behind on a task chair and a cart whose screen glows the same cyan. Only this beat needs both halves of the experiment visible at once and equal, and a model seen from above is the one view that shows two rooms as one apparatus split down the middle.

2. **The seam from the corridor.** A low eye level view straight down the shared glass wall of the two rooms, the glass seam running to a vanishing point, one room to each side in strict symmetry with the two carts glowing at the same height. It says "split" through one perspective line, though it hides the rooms' contents and reads as a hallway before it reads as a test.

3. **The floor plan in limestone.** A raised relief floor plan of the simulation suite carved in limestone, two equal rectangles with furniture inlaid in brass and two cyan inlays for the tools, seen in plan. It makes the equal widths literal, but a plan view drops the people and reads as a diagram, the "top down tray reading as a chart" judges already charged.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where a stranger sees, in one look, two matched rooms with people in them and one thing to compare, which is the headline's "half of them" made physical, and a three quarter aerial cutaway is a world, camera and scale neither the pegboard relief of frame 2 nor any recent deck used.

**Composition.** The model fills the upper three fifths, its back walls rising against the sweep under the kicker and counter. The headline sits low, one line across the quiet front of the sweep, with the dek under it in four lines over the indigo-tinted floor, clear of the claim ids and the site line. The two tool pills float over each room's back wall.
