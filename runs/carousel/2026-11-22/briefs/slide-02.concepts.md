# Slide 02, EIGHTY, three concepts

1. **The staff room wall.** Two white oak pegboard panels on a cerulean painted wall, a strip of bare wall between them, and on steel J hooks eighty stethoscopes hang, forty on each panel, every coil standing off the oak and throwing an indigo shadow down it under a raking morning key. Only this beat has it because a hung stethoscope is the one object that says "a clinician works here" without a face, and the strip of wall between the panels is the "two halves" of the headline made physical.

2. **The issue drawers.** An oak supply cabinet with two shallow drawers pulled out side by side, each lined in felt with forty coiled stethoscopes in a five by eight compartment grid, seen at a raked three quarter from above. The two matched drawers say even split and issued kit, which no other frame's sentence asks for, though a top down tray risks reading as a chart.

3. **The coat line.** A long steel rail of eighty white coats on oak hangers, short student coats and long faculty coats mixed, split into two runs of forty by a gap in the rail, shot along the rail from a low side angle. Coat length is the real marker of faculty, residents and students, but a mix by length would imply a count per level the claims don't carry, and eighty overlapping coats can't be counted at feed size.

**Pick: 1, the staff room wall.** It is the only concept where every unit stays countable at 432 px, the split is a physical seam between two panels, and the object itself means clinician, so swapping in another slide's headline breaks it. It also differs from frame 1 (a wide cutaway model from above) and frame 3 (an interior through a doorway): a head on wall at medium range, built in txthree with a raked key rather than a plan relief so the hooks show depth and the coils cast real shadows.

**Type.** The cerulean wall above the panels carries the headline, centred over the seam in two lines, with the dek centred under it, so the seam runs down from the type into the field.
