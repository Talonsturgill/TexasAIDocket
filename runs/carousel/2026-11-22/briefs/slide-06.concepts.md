# Slide 06 concepts, QUESTION TWO (c10)

1. **Two slips on a clinician's blotter.** Two identical printed output slips lie side by side on a deep indigo leather desk blotter set in a white oak clinic desk, the left squared and untouched, the right turned a few degrees with cyan pen marks in its margin and an indigo pen with a cyan tip lying across its corner, casting a long low shadow over the paper. Only this beat is about what a reader does to the same answer, accept it as printed or work on it, and the same paper under two different hands is that question with no result in it.

2. **One slip, two trays.** A single long printed slip feeds out of a slot in a limestone counter and splits at a perforation, one half sliding flat into a brass in tray, the other half pinned under a clip with a pen cap resting on it. It carries the idea of one output met two ways, though a split strip reads as a process diagram and a tray in plan reads as a chart, which the judges charged lately.

3. **The magnifier and the stamp.** On a lab bench a rubber approval stamp stands on one slip while a jeweller's loupe stands on the identical slip beside it, both under the same studio key. Strong at feed size, but a loupe ran on November 20th and a stamp reads as a verdict, which edges toward a result this study has not produced.

**Chosen: 1.** The two slips are the only picture where "accept" and "interrogate" are two acts on the very same answer, so it can't belong to any other sentence, and the oak desk and indigo blotter take it off the limestone studio slab the neighbouring frames stand on. The type sits low and centred on the dark near half of the blotter, under the symmetric pair, so the question lands between the two slips rather than over either.
