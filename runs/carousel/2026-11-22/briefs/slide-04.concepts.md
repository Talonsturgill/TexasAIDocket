# Slide 4 concepts, AI OFF (c5)

1. THE WALL PLATE (the planned picture). A brushed steel wall plate with two slotted screws on an ice blue painted wall, its gloss rocker thrown with the proud end in cyan and a long cast shadow, a TXBENCH relief. It says "off" cleanly, but a bare switch could sit under any headline about switching something off, so it does not carry "the comparison half still has a real encyclopedia".

2. THE REFERENCE VOLUME WITH A SWITCH IN ITS COVER. A thick coral clothbound medical reference volume lying on a cerulean painted desk in plan, gilt Staff of Asclepius and gilt rules on its board, and a brushed steel rocker plate screwed into the lower cover with the rocker thrown, its proud end cyan, the whole book throwing a deep indigo shadow, a TXBENCH relief. Only this beat has it, because the picture is literally the sentence: the encyclopedia is still there and whole, and one capability built into it is switched off.

3. THE LIBRARY ALCOVE. A worm's eye 3D interior of a reading alcove, a run of identical encyclopedia spines on an oak shelf under a window, and beside the shelf on the limestone wall a light switch thrown off, its proud end cyan, the room still bright with daylight, TXT.interior. It says the reference is there and the extra power is off, but it is a WIDE room like frame 3's door jamb interior and the switch would be under a tenth of the frame.
