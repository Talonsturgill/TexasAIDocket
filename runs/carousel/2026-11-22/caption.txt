The split belongs to the AI Learning Lab, announced October 20th by the IC² Institute and Dell Medical School. Its 80 clinicians are faculty, residents and students. Each will meet a paid actor playing a patient.

Researcher Sookja Kang asks how fast clinicians click on their AI tool, whether they accept its output or interrogate it, and how much reasoning gets handed off. The release reports no findings.

What the release does cite is a 2026 American Medical Association survey, with 81% of doctors using AI against 38% in 2023. Vice Dean of Education James Korndorffer says AI is going to be used and should be.
