# Carousel, November 22nd, 2026: the fair test, eighty clinicians and one tool switched off

## The story, and what the fact check did to it

The IC² Institute and Dell Medical School at UT Austin announced the AI Learning Lab on October 20th, 2026 (c1), to launch later that month with 80 Dell Med clinicians (c2), a mix of faculty, residents and students who meet patients in simulated encounters (c3). The patients are paid actors (c6). Each clinician meets the patient, asks questions, synthesizes information and arrives at a diagnosis (c7). Half have Claude to help (c4). The other half rely on UpToDate with its AI capability disabled (c5). Researchers compare both groups' reasoning across experience levels (c8) with rubrics, observation and post encounter interviews (c16). Researcher Sookja Kang asks how quickly participants click on their AI tool (c9), whether they passively accept AI output or actively interrogate it (c10), and how much reasoning work gets handed off as the diagnosis evolves (c11). The encounter is live rather than a static text vignette (c12), because a large part of solving a real case is talking with the patient (c13, Tu-Quyen Dao). The release cites a 2026 AMA survey, 81% of doctors using AI against 38% in 2023 (c15). Vice Dean of Education James Korndorffer says AI is going to be, and should be, used (c14). Results could help Dell Med and other schools decide how and when AI enters the curriculum (c17). The fact check carried 20 claims and rejected any study dates, any team roster, the project page's Executive Vice Dean title and any claim that the study has data.

Docket item tx-2026-0257, Travis County, health-and-education.

## Why this treatment, and what was grafted

Base: THE FAIR TEST (the controlled split lens). No results exist, so the only honest picture is the apparatus, and a fair test is a physical thing with two matched halves and one switch thrown. Grafted from THE WEIGHT OF A THOUGHT: Kang's three questions built as three measuring instruments, each shown at rest before anything is measured (frames 5, 6 and 7). Grafted from THE REACH: the wall of eighty stethoscopes as the countable cohort (frame 2) and the two unrolled sheets of exam paper as the cited survey (frame 8). Declined: a macro hand and a multiple exposure arm, because kit hands at close range are the deck's named risk, and the coat wall, because eighty coats is more geometry than meaning.
