Tesla certified its own Cybercab.

Tesla put a small number of Cybercabs into commercial service in Austin on September 3rd, 2026, and told NHTSA it had certified them as compliant with all applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards. The Office of Defects Investigation opened Audit Query AQ26002 the same day. An audit query is not a defect investigation and it is not a recall.

Zoox self-certified a robotaxi with no steering wheel and no pedals in 2022. It later filed for a temporary Part 555 exemption from eight federal standards. Final approval came in July 2026. Zoox did both, in that order.

The audit examines the process and the technical data behind Tesla's certification. Under 49 CFR 552.3 any interested person may petition NHTSA to decide whether a vehicle meets an applicable standard. Which standards did Tesla read as inapplicable?
