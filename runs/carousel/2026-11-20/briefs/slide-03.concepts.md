# Slide 03, THE NEXT VALLEY, three concepts

1. **Two paths, two landforms (aerial oblique).** A low aerial over a copper grass ridge on the right and the next valley on the left: a frosted periwinkle lawnmower sweep lies untouched down the whole near length of the crest, while one amber line shares only its first far legs, then slides off the flank into the valley and runs back toward us to a small drone, studded with time beads and trailing its own shadow on the grass. Only this beat has a ridge and a valley side by side, so the plan and the flight can be read as two places at one glance.

2. **Worm's eye from the unsearched crest.** The camera lies in the dry grass on the ridge top looking up: the frosted sweep glides overhead against the periwinkle sky casting no shadow on the stems, and far down past the edge, small in the valley, the amber line and its shadow reach a drone. It is the ridge's own point of view of being skipped.

3. **The ridge as a sectioned clay block.** A cut block of terrain on a sweep, sliced so the ridge and valley read in profile like a geology model, the frosted sweep floating above the crest and an amber wire bending down the cut face into the valley. It explains the drop by scale and section rather than by place.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where a stranger sees, in one second, a plan left whole on one landform and a real path on another; 2 hides the valley and 3 reads as a lab model, close to the studio and lab neighbours on frames 2 and 4.

**Type.** Hook high in the sky at the left in two lines, the dek under it over the hazy far end of the valley and the shaded far wall, which the light leaves dark and quiet. The ridge, the sweep and the drone keep the lower two thirds and the right.
