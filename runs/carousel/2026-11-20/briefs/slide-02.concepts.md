# Slide 02 concepts, A FALSE FIX

1. ONE MACHINE, TWO PLACES. A survey quadcopter stands large and solid on an apricot studio sweep, its feet pressing four plum contact shadows, while a frosted periwinkle glass twin hangs up and to the left in the bright sweep with no shadow anywhere under it. Only a story about a receiver told the wrong location splits one drone into a real body and a reported one, and only the real one is allowed to touch the floor.

2. THE PUCK THAT LISTENS. A macro on the drone's GPS puck on its mast, the real shell out of focus below, with a glass copy of the puck lifted off and drifting away from its mast. It is the receiver itself being lied to, though at feed size a lone dome reads as a lamp, and frame 5 already owns the macro board and an unplugged puck.

3. THE WRONG PAD. A low studio floor with a painted landing pad where the real drone stands, and a frosted twin settling onto bare sweep a long way off, shadowless. It speaks to "set it down somewhere unsafe", yet it spreads the pair across the floor small and far, the look judges charged lately, and it loses the confident vertical offset.

PICK: 1. It makes the false fix a single readable act (same aircraft, two positions, one shadow) at CLOSE scale, the only studio object frame between frame 1's ridge and frame 3's aerial, and the headline sits in the sky beside the lie it names.
