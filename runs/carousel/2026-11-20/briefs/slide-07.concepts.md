# Slide 07 concepts, NEVER SEEN

1. **The sealed case.** A studio sweep in an orthographic 35 degree rake: 74 porcelain tiles, each engraved with its own flight's track, laid out on an open maple tray, and beyond a gap of bare floor 22 smoked plum glass tiles shut inside a clear glass case on its own plinth. Only this beat has a set of whole flights physically shut away from the rest, so the picture is the fairness of the test itself.
2. **The card drawer.** A warm records room: two drawers of a flight log cabinet, one pulled open and thumbed with 74 cards, the other shut and tied with tape around 22 cards. It says "held back" as a filing act, but a cabinet reads as an archive more than an exam, and a closed drawer can't show its count.
3. **The exam desk.** A raked view of an examiner's desk: 74 worked tiles stacked in a study pile, 22 face down in a ruled exam tray under a glass paperweight. It names the exam idea, but face down tiles hide the flights and a desk leans toward a person the beat doesn't need.

**Pick: 1.** The case is the one picture where "never seen" is literally a wall of glass between the flights the models learned from and the 22 they were judged on, and every tile can be counted. It also differs from frame 6's plan relief (orthographic rake on a studio sweep, not clay in plan) and from frame 8's brass close up.

Type: a column at upper right, three line hook and the dek under it, over the quiet back wall of the sweep. The blocks run from upper left (far) down to the case at lower right (near).
