# Slide 08 concepts, TEN READINGS

1. **The surface plate.** A brushed brass ribbon of motion readings, a row of brass pins of changing height, runs across a polished plum granite metrology plate at 45 cm, a machined glass loupe with an amber frame stands astride exactly ten pins, and in front of it one rose enamel bar sits in a pale machined rail, filled to the score, with the periwinkle sky beyond the plate's far edge. Only this beat reads a window of ten readings and turns it into one length on one rail, and the inspection plate says "one measure" in the language of measurement rather than of alarms.

2. **The paper strip in the sun.** A long printed motion trace curls off a spool across a weathered tailgate on the ridge, a reading glass held by a turned away hand frames ten of its peaks, and the shadow of the hand falls across the strip. It ties the number to the people tone, but it repeats frame 9's tailgate and ridge world, the strip leans on the tape metaphor the treatment declined, and kit hands have been charged soft.

3. **The abacus of ten.** A walnut counting frame on a classroom desk under window light, its one amber edged rod holding ten brass beads pushed together, beside a single rose bar slotted into a long ruler groove. It says ten and one score, but a bead frame reads as counting things rather than reading numbers over time, and the lab room world belongs to frame 4.

**Pick: 1.** Only it shows both halves of the sentence as two physical objects, ten readings framed under glass and one score as one length on one rail, and its world (a granite plate against open sky at macro scale) differs from frame 7's pale studio sweep and frame 9's wide ridge at last light.

Type plan: the headline in one line low left over the dark polished granite foreground, the dek under it in a wide column, the ribbon and loupe in the upper middle against the sky, the rail between them and the type.
