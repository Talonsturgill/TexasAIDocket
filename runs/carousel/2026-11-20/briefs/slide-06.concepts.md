# Slide 06, 96 REAL FLIGHTS, three concepts

1. **The wire field.** Ninety six bent wire flight loops stand on their own cast pucks on a wide caliche pad in late apricot light, eight rows of twelve, 42 in pewter at the back and 54 in copper at the front, every loop throwing a long plum shadow. Each copper loop carries a frosted periwinkle glass branch that hops off it or slowly peels away and casts no shadow, so "54 were lied to" is a thing a stranger counts, in the deck's own grammar of real metal that shadows and false glass that does not.

2. **The clay tablet (the storyboard's plan).** A caliche clay tablet seen in plan under the chassis key, pressed with 96 raised tracks in rows of twelve, pewter normal and copper spoofed with a step or bend, an enlarged pair at the foot. Only this beat counts flights, but frame 7 is already pewter and copper tiles with engraved tracks seen orthographically, so this would read as the same picture twice.

3. **The flight log shelf.** A lab shelf of 96 flight recorder cases in rows of twelve under a window, 54 carrying copper tags and a frosted glass twin of their track card. It counts, but a wall of boxes is a filing image that could carry any dataset, and the November 17th deck already drew a card drawer.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where the headline's two numbers are two materials a thumb can count at a glance and where the lie is drawn as the deck's shadowless glass, and its world (an outdoor pad, raked three quarter, low key) differs from frame 5's studio macro and frame 7's orthographic studio tiles.

**Type.** Headline top left in two lines over the far pad and the hazy periwinkle horizon, dek under it in the same quiet band. The field fills the lower three fifths and bleeds off the bottom, the pills sit on one pewter and one copper loop in the near rows.
