# Slide 05 concepts, ITS OWN MOTION

1. **The board and its three arrows (MACRO, studio sweep).** A raked macro of the drone's flight controller, solder mask, gold pads, a pinned processor and the small motion sensor chip, with three machined amber arrows rising out of that chip, one straight (how fast), one bending off (which way) and one curling round like a clock sweep (how long). The GPS puck lies unplugged beside the board in frosted periwinkle glass, its plug resting short of an empty socket, so the picture says in one look that the check comes from the chip and never from the receiver.

2. **The sealed drone (CLOSE, studio).** A drone under a glass bell jar on a turntable, the GPS mast unscrewed and standing outside the jar, amber motion trails drawn inside it around the body. It says self contained, but frame 2 already shows a whole drone in the studio and the bell jar reads as a museum, not a sensor.

3. **The sensor cut open (CUTAWAY, macro of the die).** The motion sensor chip cut away to its silicon spring and proof mass, three amber arrows bending the mass, the GPS antenna a frosted ghost far behind. It is the truest picture of how a motion sensor works, and the hardest for a stranger to recognise at feed size.

**Pick: 1.** Only this sentence wants a board where the arrows come out of the motion chip while the GPS plug lies a finger short of its socket, and it is the deck's one macro, between frame 4's lit lab and frame 6's clay relief.

**Type:** right column, top right, headline in three lines over the quiet sweep, dek right aligned under it. The board bleeds off the left and bottom, the arrows climb the left half, the frosted puck sits low right under the dek.
