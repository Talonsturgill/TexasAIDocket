# Slide 9 concepts, NEXT, IN FLIGHT

1. THE TAILGATE AT LAST LIGHT. A pickup parked side on along the crest with its tailgate dropped toward the camera, the survey quadcopter sitting on the gate with its props folded beside an open hard case of sealed flight records, one searcher sitting on the gate's far corner looking out across the valley, the empty periwinkle evening sky over the far ridges holding the headline. Only the close can have it, because the drone is grounded and the whole sky above it is the flight that has not happened yet, which is exactly what "next, running the check in flight" says.

2. THE CASE, MACRO. A 50 mm look down into the open hard case on the tailgate, the folded drone nested in its foam cut out and the sealed record cartridges standing in their slots, the valley a soft plum blur beyond the gate edge. It belongs to this beat because the held out records come back sealed and the aircraft is packed, but it repeats frame 8's close studio scale and loses the sky the next step lives in.

3. THE EMPTY LAUNCH PAD. A worm's eye from the grass at the crest, a folded drone on a small rubber launch mat in the foreground and nothing above it but a tall last light sky, a pickup's tail light and a person's boots at the frame edge. It says "not yet flown" with the sky alone, but at feed size the subject is small and low, the charge judges keep making.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where the grounded drone, the sealed records and the open sky the test will fly in are all in one picture, so the headline reads as the caption of that sky and no other slide's sentence fits it. It also changes world, camera and scale from frame 8's close studio rail.

Type: the headline in the sky, top left, two lines, the dek under it over the hazy far ridges, the subject owning the lower half and the right.
