Nonbinding doesn't mean a no goes nowhere.

The deck lays the ballot beside the council's record in road paint.

The ballot says nonbinding. Harris County's sample ballot asks League City voters "As a nonbinding statement of voter preference" whether police should keep license plate reader cameras at fixed locations on public roadways. The council amended that word in on August 11th. Harris County vote centers take voters from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3rd.

The council's record says more. On October 13th it passed Resolution No. 2026-185 by 8 to 0. It directs removal of the fixed cameras if a majority rejects their continued use. Mayor Nick Long told the council "there's no second vote needed," the Tribune reported.

If Tuesday's answer is no, what is the word nonbinding still doing?
