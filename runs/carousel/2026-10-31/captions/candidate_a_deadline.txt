League City answers Tuesday, November 3rd.

The question is whether police keep the fixed plate reader cameras. The Texas Tribune calls them AI-powered and the vote a Texas first. The ballot frames the answer as "a nonbinding statement of voter preference." The council added that word on August 11th. So it sounds advisory.

On October 13th every member on the roll voted to direct removal of the fixed cameras if a majority rejects them. Mayor Nick Long said there is "no second vote needed," the Tribune reported.

It also reports roughly 200 police uses in an average 30 day period and more than 400 agencies with access.

The deck lays the record in road paint.

Kyle and Laredo plan votes too, the Tribune reported. Will either council settle what a no means before its voters answer in May?
