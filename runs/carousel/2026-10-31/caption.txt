A "nonbinding" no would reach League City's cameras.

The word is the ballot's. On Harris County's sample ballot, Proposition A asks "As a nonbinding statement of voter preference" whether police should keep the fixed plate reader cameras. The council added the word on August 11th. League City lies in Galveston and Harris counties. Harris County vote centers take voters from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3rd.

The council's own record goes further. On October 13th it passed Resolution No. 2026-185 by 8 to 0. It directs removal of the fixed cameras if a majority rejects their continued use. Mayor Nick Long told the council "there's no second vote needed," the Tribune reported.

By the Tribune's account Long put the start of bagging at 72 hours after certification. If a majority votes no Tuesday, when will the count be certified?
