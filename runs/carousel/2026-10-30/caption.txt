$390,000 is not what ChatGPT costs Plano.

It is the ceiling on one item from the October 12th City Council agenda. ChatGPT Enterprise is $190,000 of it. Darwin AI is $150,000. Its maker says the platform tracks AI use across users, tools and departments. Microsoft Enterprise adds $10,000. The last $40,000 is held for growth.

For every chatbot dollar the item sets aside 79 cents for Darwin.

The item sat on the consent agenda, which the council acts on in one motion. The minutes note it approved. The October 26th minutes mark those minutes approved, and both sets are still marked draft. Plano's Legistar lists the council's next meeting for November 9th.

The deck weighs the item on a brass balance, one weight per $10,000.
