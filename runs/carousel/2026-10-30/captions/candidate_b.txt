$390,000 is not what ChatGPT costs Plano.

It is the ceiling on one item from the October 12th City Council agenda. ChatGPT Enterprise is $190,000 of it. Darwin AI takes $150,000 for a platform its maker says tracks AI use across users, tools and departments. Microsoft adds $10,000. The last $40,000 is held for growth.

For every chatbot dollar the item sets aside 79 cents for Darwin. The tool and the oversight arrive together.

The item sat on the consent agenda, which the council acts on in one motion. The minutes note it approved. Those minutes are still marked draft. Plano's Legistar entry shows no vote count, no license count and no planned use.

The deck weighs the item on a brass balance, one weight per $10,000.

How much of the $390,000 comes with a public account of what it is for?
