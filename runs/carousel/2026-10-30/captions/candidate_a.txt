What does ChatGPT cost Plano?

$190,000 for ChatGPT Enterprise from Carahsoft. It sat on the City Council's October 12th consent agenda. Microsoft Enterprise adds $10,000. A $40,000 reserve covers growth in licenses and usage. The ceiling is $390,000. Read that way it is a city buying a chatbot.

The next largest line buys a platform its maker says monitors AI activity. Darwin AI is $150,000. That is 79 cents for every chatbot dollar. Darwin says it tracks AI use across users, tools and departments. Buying the tool and paying to govern it came in one consent item.

The deck draws each line as $10,000 weights on a brass balance.

The minutes that note the item approved are still a draft. Plano's Legistar entry shows no license count and no planned use. How does a resident weigh $390,000 on four dollar lines?
