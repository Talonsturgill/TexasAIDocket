In July 2025 the PUCT approved the resiliency plan of Southwestern Public Service, Xcel Energy's utility across most of the Texas Panhandle. Its order lists the "number of artificial intelligence camera fire detections" among the agreed metrics for each annual report.

SPS's report answered that line with "33 cameras installed" and 34 projected for 2026. It does count 24 ignitions associated with overhead power lines and 26 wildfires reported in counties SPS serves. SPS says a partial year can't yet show effectiveness against projections.

What that line lacks is one number, the count of detections the order named.

SPS says its 2027 annual report will carry a full year of data, and under the order it carries the same line.

Will that report count fire detections, or cameras again?
