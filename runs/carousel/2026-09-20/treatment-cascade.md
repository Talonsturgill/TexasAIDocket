# Treatment A — THE CASCADE (director 1, returned)

**Concept.** Walk one small Texas water system from a Brazos river intake to a household meter
box. The award is money to see one component further down that line.

**Rotation (checked by the director against the current rule):**
FULL_BLEED, CLOSE_CROP, FIGURE_SCALE, DIAGRAM, FULL_BLEED, GRID, DOCUMENT, CLOSE_CROP, FULL_BLEED
6 distinct, no adjacent repeat, 7 of 9 bleed, rects 1.00 0.78 0.61 0.53 1.00 0.67 0.59 0.81 1.00.

**Continuity devices.** Panorama spine (8,640 px canvas, camera walks 1,080 px per frame, ~1.1 km
east, one 300 mm main entering left and leaving right of every frame) and edge tease. Second pair:
motif evolution (identification paint moving down the chain) and camera move (frames 2 and 8 the
identical kneeling camera at the two ends of the same pipe, frame 9 restoring frame 1's camera).

**World.** Post Oak Savannah of the Brazos Valley, late August, 12:40.
ground #101310 (post oak shade on Brazos clay, near black, green cast, NO violet)
ink #E8E2D0 (hot-dip galvanised chalked by sun)
mids: canopy #3A4034, alluvium #4A3A2C, utility door green #7E8C76, drive gravel #9A9488
accent rust #8C5A3C
**Nothing blue anywhere in the deck**, which is the answer to September 19th being a water story
with a comal accent.

**Light.** az -22, el 66. In words: straight-up August sun a little west of the camera axis, so
galvanised steel blows out to bare ink on top faces, every shadow is a short hard puddle tucked
under the thing that cast it, and nothing is backlit.

**Screen.** halftone cell 7, angle 15, gamma 1.2, floor 0.04, on all nine.

**Camera.** standing eye 1.62, horizon 790, f 880 default (frames 1,3,4,9). Frames 2 and 8 drop to
kneeling eye 1.05 at f 1600, identical to each other. Frame 5 horizon to 1020. Frame 6 f 2400 at
Z 16. Frame 7 pitched over the tailgate. Value arc rises monotonically, no adjacent jump over 8 L*.

**THE ACCENT LAW.** rust is NEVER corrosion or staining. It is the utility's own identification
paint and lands on exactly ONE element per frame, always the component that frame is named for.
Trash rack band (1), pump volute through the open door (3), riser band (5), the two marked words
on the page (7), meter lid tag (8). Absent on 2, 4, 6, 9. Frame 4's absence is deliberate because
that frame is the map of the chain rather than a stage of it.

**The no-list that keeps the cascade from reading as an outage.** No water anywhere it should not
be, no spray, no flooding, no cracked pipe, no boil-water notice, no emergency vehicle, no
flashing light, no running figure, no flashlight. Every figure is doing ordinary daytime work.

**Risks the director named.** Noon at el 66 removes the long rake, so objects float if contact
shadows come back weak at 432 px (acceptance: two-part carve, shadow >= 0.35 of object height,
checked on the thumb). Rust is the nearest neighbour to September 14th's Dickens red bed, fallback
mesquite #6E7A5A on the same five elements. Frame 6 shows only 87 and a reader could carry away
that Texas has 87 systems. Four of nine subjects are not in the catalogue. Frame 7 is the largest
adjacent L* jump. **The state comparison v4 to v7 is on no frame** and the director spends it in
the caption, naming it a real loss and a clean graft point.
