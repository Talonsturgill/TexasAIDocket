# Treatment B — THE SHAPE OF SMALL (director 2, returned)

**Concept.** One small water plant drawn whole at true scale on a hot afternoon, and one sheet of
paper where 4,749 marks, 3,579 marks and a single short row of 87 each name the query that
counted them.

**Rotation:** FULL_BLEED, FIGURE_SCALE, TYPE_AS_OBJECT, DIAGRAM, GRID, DOCUMENT, CLOSE_CROP,
FIGURE_SCALE, OBJECT_AND_CAPTION. 8 distinct, TYPE_AS_OBJECT once, 7 bleed.
FIGURE_SCALE twice non-adjacently on purpose, because 8 is the identical camera to 2 from the
other side of the tank, which is the move the 2026-09-16 judge singled out.

**World.** Post Oak Savannah / Brazos Valley, sandy loam over lignite, 2:10 in the afternoon.
ground #14100A (wet sandy loam gone nearly black, WARM and brown where Sept 19 was cool blue-green)
ink #DDD5C0 (post oak deadwood, chalky zinc bloom on galvanised steel, cooler and greyer than
caliche on purpose because zinc is grey and caliche is pink)
accent #B4664F dusk_ember
paper insert stock #D9D1BC printed in #14100A, the deck's own ink and ground swapping jobs, so
the inversion is ONE MATERIAL SEEN TWICE rather than a second palette arriving.

**Light.** az -18, el 62. Ten past two, sun nearly overhead and a little behind the camera's left
shoulder, every shadow short hard and pooled directly under the thing that threw it.

**Screen.** halftone cell 7 angle 22, all nine.

**THE ACCENT LAW.** dusk_ember is RED IRON OXIDE PRIMER, which is what gets painted on the parts
of a small water plant a person has to PUT A HAND ON OR READ. It touches exactly three things:
the 0.35 m discharge valve handwheel (frame 2 full, frame 8 at its dimmest), the meter register
face at the customer's curb (frame 9), and on frame 5 the 3,579 marks of the block whose stanza
carries POPULATION_SERVED_COUNT less than 3301, because that is the set the award's first
direction names. Never the tank, the pipe, the type, the rules, or the sky.

**Honesty rules the director built in.** No name on the gate, no sign board, no population figure
and no county on any yard frame, so no reader can take the plant for a named real system. The
chart recorder and the meter register carry NO arc segment, NO coloured zone and NO needle at a
meaningful position, because a dial is a verdict this project's data can't carry.

**Risks named.** The partition misread is the one that kills the deck, answered structurally
(full-width rules, complete stanzas, unequal left-aligned lengths, no operator, accent on block B
only). 8.2 px mark pitch will moire against halftone cell 7, so marks are drawn flat in the
TXINK.print OVER pass and never go through the screen. Frames 5 and 6 are the deck's only hard
value cuts and its bespoke_check similarity risk. TYPE_AS_OBJECT at el 62 gives almost no cast.
A noon scene on a near-black ground can read as an unmotivated night sky, and the probe frame has
to prove it before eight more are built on it.
