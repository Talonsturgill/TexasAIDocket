Aimed at small systems. Texas has 4,749.

Those are the active community water systems on the EPA's record. 3,579 of them serve fewer than 3,301 people. A separate count puts 87 over 50,000.

The National Science Foundation made a standard grant to the Texas A&M Engineering Experiment Station on September 9th. Work began September 15th. The award expires August 31st, 2029. The abstract's first stated direction is predictive digital twins for small and mid-sized systems.

Utility practice often relies on monitoring, historical records and reactive emergency protocols. That is a complete account of what already happened. The abstract says the project can support earlier warning of how a local anomaly escalates. No deployment is recorded.

TCEQ sends a resident to their own system's annual drinking water quality report. Which of those 3,579 ever gets a twin?
