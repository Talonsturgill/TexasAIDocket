What this deck does not say, because the sources do not.

TOP500 publishes no university category. Neither its list page nor its system detail page calls
VISION the fastest university supercomputer in the United States. That framing belongs to the
System and to Tarleton State, and VISION's own site says only that it is one of the
highest-performing AI supercomputers at any North American university.

No page fetched names a county or a city for the West Campus Data Center, so this record names
none.

No dated Board of Regents action was found. The newsroom states the approval and gives no
meeting date and no agenda item.

The compound counts are printed as their own sources wrote them and are never joined. Nothing
in the record connects them, and about a week is not a duration this record can turn into a rate.
