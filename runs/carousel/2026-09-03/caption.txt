Who gets an invitation?

The Texas A&M University System's VISION supercomputer ranked No. 66 in the world at 34.82 petaflops measured. It sits in the West Campus Data Center at Texas A&M University.

VISION's own documentation states the present condition. Access is invitation-only and approved via TAMUS governance. The current status is controlled beta.

Two System pages describe who VISION is for. Both use the future tense. One reads "VISION will be available to researchers of all disciplines across the entire Texas A&M System".

Onboarding is expected to begin with the institutions that finished integration, and the front page is seeking beta testers. The documentation states the access rule and states no number. How many researchers can use it today?
