**What it is.** The Public Utility Commission of Texas has proposed a new rule, 16 TAC Section
25.521, "Large Load Demand Management Service". It would require ERCOT to develop a service that
competitively procures demand reductions from large load customers. Comments close September 4th,
2026 and every comment must refer to Project Number 58482.

**THE ANGLE, and it survived a fact check that killed the first one.**
The rule never uses the words "data center". Not once. An independent fetch of the published rule
in the August 14th, 2026 Texas Register returns ZERO occurrences of the phrase. What the rule does
instead is draw a line: a large load customer is an entity with a total non-coincident peak demand
at a single site equal to or greater than 75 megawatts.

The threshold is the definition. The rule describes an industry without naming it.
