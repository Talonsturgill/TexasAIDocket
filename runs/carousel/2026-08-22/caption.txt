September 4th shuts the comment window.

Until then it is open on 16 TAC Section 25.521, the Large Load Demand Management Service rule proposed by the Public Utility Commission of Texas. It would have ERCOT competitively procure demand reductions from large load customers.

The rule as published in the Texas Register never writes the words data center. It draws a line instead. A large load customer is an entity with total non-coincident peak demand at a single site of 75 megawatts or more.

Comments run through the interchange on the commission's website at interchange.puc.texas.gov. Each should refer to Project Number 58482.

The index stands at 34 filings. The Data Center Coalition is in it. So is CyrusOne, LP. So is item 33, under the party name Helen Bryant with item type PC.

That is all the index carries. An item number. A file stamp. A party name. An item type. A filing description. Nothing on that page says who Helen Bryant is.
