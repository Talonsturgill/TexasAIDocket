The problem is not the megawatts.

ERCOT's board just heard that the real risk is speed. Large Loads tied to AI training can swing fast enough to strain a generator's shaft and potentially damage it. The draft rule would cap that swing at 10 MW per 5 seconds.

A ride through rule took effect August 1st. A better modeling rule reaches the Board this month. That swing rule itself has no number yet. What it has is a plan to file it in September or October.

Six pages name no data center, no company and no generator. ERCOT also says its own models are too inaccurate to study these loads' grid impact, so it went to Texas A&M for a new one.

How much of that swing still runs on a model ERCOT calls inadequate?
