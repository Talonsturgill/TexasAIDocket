# Selection — 2026-09-21

## The story

**The City of Dallas mounted AI cameras on its sanitation brush trucks, and the first citations
have arrived.** Docket item `tx-2026-0179`, admitted this run.

In a December 9th, 2025 briefing to its own Finance Committee the city set out the program's
terms and then drew a line under what the system would not be. Four things, on one slide, under
the heading What this IS NOT. The cameras went fully operational over the summer of 2026. A city
manager memo dated September 8th, 2026 reported the first enforcement, and two Dallas stations
read it.

The tension is a document arguing with itself. The memo says a courtesy notice is not a warning
or a violation. The notice in the mailbox carries the words violations identified and asks the
owner to correct them to avoid further enforcement action. The assistant director of Code
Compliance, asked about the difference, said you could call that a warning.

The second half is the harder one and it is the reason this is worth a deck rather than a post.
A machine that photographs every parcel on a monthly route treats every street the same way. The
citations did not land that way. 39 of the 57 went to three council districts in southern Dallas.

## Why this and not the others

Five scouts came back with material and four other candidates were live.

**Saronic and the Secretary of War in Austin** was the most dramatic thing on the table and it is
the one that could not be sourced. The department's own site refused every client this run, so
the combat claims rest entirely on two newsrooms' accounts of remarks nobody here read. A claims
gate would have taken most of it and the deck would have been built on what survived.

**El Paso ISD turning off ChatGPT and Gemini on student accounts** is a genuinely good story with
a real primary document behind it. It is off the table on the thirty day rule rather than on
merit. Education carried the deck on September 11th and again on September 16th, and a third
classroom deck inside ten days is the drift this beat list exists to stop.

**Battalion Oil pointing an oilfield model at Ward County** is admitted to the record this run
and is not a deck, because nothing has happened yet. The company's own timeline puts the first
engineer in front of it at about day 90, and a deck about a subscription agreement is a deck
about a press release.

**Leander and Bastrop cancelling their plate reader contracts** is the strongest community signal
of the week and it is the closest call. It loses on repetition. Plate readers and Flock have
carried three decks inside thirty days, on August 20th, August 30th and September 8th, and the
Hays County deck on September 18th was a fourth camera story. Leander is admitted to the record
where it belongs.

Dallas wins because it is none of those things. It is a machine already running against people
who did not ask for it, with a measured outcome, a primary document that states the promise it
is being measured against, an unequal result the city has to account for, and a dated public
meeting three weeks out that a reader can walk into.
