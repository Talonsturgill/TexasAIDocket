Is a courtesy notice a warning?

Dallas says no. A city manager memo dated September 8th reads "A courtesy notice is not a warning or a violation." The city has sent 3,700 courtesy notices. The one an Oak Cliff homeowner opened carried "violations identified" in bold near the top. It asked her to correct the violations promptly to avoid further enforcement action. Pressed, Code Compliance assistant director Jeremy Reed said "you could call that a warning."

No automated ticketing was the December 2025 promise to the Finance Committee. It held. A code officer inspects in person before any citation issues.

The deck sets the briefing's three refusals beside the letter's own sentences.

That memo is not posted at any public address this record could find. How does a homeowner weigh a letter against a document they can't read?
