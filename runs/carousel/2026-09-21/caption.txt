Is a courtesy notice a warning?

Dallas says no. NBC 5 reported the city manager's memo saying "A courtesy notice is not a warning or a violation." The city says it sent courtesy notices to 3,700 property owners. The one an Oak Cliff homeowner opened carried "violations identified" in bold near the top. The notice asks owners to correct the violations to avoid further enforcement action. Pressed, Code Compliance assistant director Jeremy Reed said "you could call that a warning."

No automated ticketing was the December 2025 promise to the Finance Committee. FOX 4 reported the memo saying a code officer inspects in person before a citation issues. That is the city's account of its own process, not an audit.

That memo is posted at no public address this record found. How does a homeowner weigh a letter against a document they can't read?
