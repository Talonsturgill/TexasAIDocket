A dollar on car insurance bought the cameras. Now it's a local call.

In 2023 the Legislature added $1 to Texans' auto insurance. A state authority turned it into at least 3,200 Flock cameras. In late August the Governor ordered the money paused. At least 14 cities and counties have shut off more than 900 cameras, the Tribune reported.

It reads like a statewide shutoff. Town by town the answers split.

El Paso's council directed their removal within 60 days. Leander dropped them September 4th over community concerns. Kyle kept its contract 6-1. College Station kept its cameras 4-2. Its police chief says the city can't afford the officers to do that work. Searches there need reasonable suspicion and are kept indefinitely.

League City set its vote on August 11th, before the pause. Will its nonbinding November 3rd answer decide whether police keep the cameras?
