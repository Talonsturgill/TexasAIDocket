League City answers on November 3rd.

In 2023 the Legislature added $1 to Texans' auto insurance to fight catalytic converter theft. A state agency turned the fee into at least 3,200 Flock cameras. In late August the Governor ordered the funding paused.

At least 14 cities and counties have since shut off more than 900 Flock cameras. Councils are splitting. El Paso's council ordered its Flock fixed cameras removed within 60 days. Leander stopped "in response to community concerns". Kyle kept its contract 6-1. College Station kept its cameras 4-2 and requires reasonable suspicion to search. Laredo asks voters in May.

League City called a nonbinding vote on August 11th, before the pause. Its ballot asks if police should keep fixed plate reader cameras on public roadways. What will a voter who drives past one say on November 3rd?
