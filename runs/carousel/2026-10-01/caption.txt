League City answers on November 3rd.

In 2023 the Legislature added $1 to Texans' auto insurance. A state agency turned the fee into at least 3,200 Flock cameras. In late August the Governor ordered the funding paused. It came as scrutiny over the "AI-powered surveillance devices" mounted, the Tribune reported.

At least 14 cities and counties have since shut off more than 900 Flock cameras, the Tribune reported. El Paso voted on a 60 day clock for its fixed Flock cameras and passed it as revised. Leander stopped "in response to community concerns". Kyle kept its contract 6 to 1. College Station kept its cameras 4 to 2. Laredo asks voters in May.

League City called a nonbinding vote on August 11th, before the pause. Its ballot asks if police should keep the fixed cameras. What will a voter who drives past one say on November 3rd?
