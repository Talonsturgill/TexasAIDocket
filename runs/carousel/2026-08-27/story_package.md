# Story package for 2026-08-27 — the lead and the material around it

## THE LEAD

**Docket item `tx-2026-0084`**, admitted to the record today.

Amazon announced on August 19th, 2026 that it has selected Austin, in Travis County, for a
new manufacturing facility that will build the robotics systems used in its own fulfillment
network. The Office of the Texas Governor announced the same siting the same day. Austin's
mayor is quoted in Amazon's own release saying the company asked for no economic incentives
and no public money.

## THE ANGLE

The record this deck links to is, at the moment, largely a record of Texas local governments
being asked for money. Abatements, reinvestment zones, moratoriums, tax breaks. Here is a
multi billion dollar plant that asked for none of it, and the thing it will build is the
machinery that changes warehouse work.

The counter-image the deck must keep honest: a company that asks for nothing also faces no
hearing. No public comment window attaches to a private siting that seeks no public money.
