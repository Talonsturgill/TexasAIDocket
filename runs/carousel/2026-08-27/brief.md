# Directors brief, 2026-08-27, carousel no. 9

## THE STORY, and it is settled

Docket item `tx-2026-0084`, admitted to the record this run.

On August 19th, 2026 Amazon announced it has selected Austin, in Travis County, for a new
manufacturing facility that will build the robotics systems used in its own fulfillment
network. The Office of the Texas Governor announced the same siting the same day. Both
releases put the plant at 300 to 500 jobs in manufacturing and engineering. The Governor's
release calls the development multi billion dollar and gives no capital figure. Austin's
mayor, Kirk Watson, is quoted inside Amazon's own release saying the company asked for no
economic incentives and no public money.

## THE ARGUMENT THE DECK IS MAKING

The record this deck links to is, right now, largely a record of Texas local governments
being asked for money. Reinvestment zones, tax abatements, moratoriums, outside counsel hired
to write agreements a county says its own template will not hold. Here is a multi billion
dollar plant that asked for none of it.

And the honest other half, which the deck must carry rather than dodge: **a company that asks
for nothing also faces no hearing.** No comment window, no public testimony, no vote. The
absence of a subsidy and the absence of a public say arrive together. That is not a complaint
about Amazon. It is how the instrument works, and it is the most interesting thing on the page.

The third thread, and the one that keeps this from being an advertisement: what the plant
makes. Amazon's own words are that these robots "handle repetitive tasks and heavy lifting"
alongside its employees. A factory whose product is the machinery that changes warehouse work.
