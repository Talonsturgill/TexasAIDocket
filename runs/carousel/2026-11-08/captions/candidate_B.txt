Which of 630,003 protein pairs meet?

Biochemists at Texas A&M in College Station ran every pairing of 1123 human mitochondrial proteins through Google DeepMind's AlphaFold-Multimer. Each pair got a yes or no. The computing was Texas A&M's own.

Asked. Do these two meet?
Answered. 2,895 likely interactions, the paper's 0.46%. More than half had never been reported.

Asked. How often is a yes wrong?
Answered. A false discovery rate of about 15% on the authors' test set.

Asked. How tightly does each pair bind?
Answered. The method can't say.

Asked. Does it hold up in cells?
Answered. Several pairings held up in yeast and human cells after about two years of lab work.

The deck runs from a plate of every pair to the lab bench.

All 2,895 predictions are free online as MitoMatch. The release says cures for mitochondria-linked diseases may still be far off. Which pair on that list earns a lab's next two years?
