Copper has a delivery route inside mitochondria.

Texas A&M biochemists in College Station mapped how it reaches cytochrome c oxidase, guided by an AI screen. The team recast Google DeepMind's AlphaFold-Multimer as a yes or no test on protein pairs. The computing was Texas A&M's own. Of 630,003 possible pairs it kept 2,895 as likely, the paper's 0.46%. 57% of them were in no existing interaction database.

Mutations in nearly 30% of mitochondrial proteins are tied to human disease. The method can't say how tightly a pair binds. The release says cures may still be far off.

The deck goes from two proteins to a plate of every pair, all drawn to illustrate.

All 2,895 predictions are free online as MitoMatch. The set carries an apparent false discovery rate of 15%. Which pairs are the false ones?
