Which of 630,003 protein pairs inside mitochondria actually meet?

Texas A&M scientists in College Station recast Google DeepMind's AlphaFold-Multimer as a yes or no test on pairs of mitochondrial proteins. Texas A&M High Performance Research Computing supplied the computing. The screen kept 2,895 of 630,003 pairs as likely, the paper's 0.46%. 57% of the 2,895 were in none of the databases checked. Lab work guided by the predictions defines copper's route to cytochrome c oxidase.

Mutations in nearly 30% of mitochondrial proteins are tied to human disease. The method can't say how tightly a pair binds. The release says cures may still be far off.

All 2,895 predictions are free online as MitoMatch, at mitomatch.web.app. The set carries an apparent false discovery rate of 15%. Which pairs are the false ones?
