## THE SYNTHESIS, and what came from where

Three directors pitched. **Lens A, THE ROOM IT LANDS IN, is the spine.**

It is the spine because the story's force IS its thesis. There is no AI unit, no AI course and no
AI announcement. There are three expectations scattered through a class about money, so a deck
drawn in the register of policy would be drawing something that does not exist. It exists as a

the three expectations sitting unlit below it. **That frame lets the deck carry a document's copy
without drawing paper**, which matters because flat printed copy and a field print in the sheet's
own plane both shipped inside the last week.

**GRAFTED FROM LENS C, and it is the graft that saves the middle.** C was the only director who
defended the three-posture middle with a MEASUREMENT rather than an argument. Slides 4, 5 and 6
now each declare `data-encodes` on the IDENTICAL rect with `"reads":"differ"`, so the deck
asserts that one footprint reads as three different things and `qa.py` holds it to that at 432px.
If the three postures are one drawing relit, the probe comes back under 4.0 dE and the deck
fails its own assertion. A pitched those three frames with no such guard, and the brief had
warned all three directors that this is exactly where a deck collapses into a template.

**Also grafted from C**, its sixth risk, adopted as a law: **absence is drawn exactly three times
and a fourth would be a tic.** They are slide 2's cut-through void, slide 3's five points in a
wash, and slide 7's six counted zeros. Each is a different kind of nothing: a hole in a surface,
a signal lost in a field, and a number. **No repair pass adds a fourth.**

**GRAFTED FROM LENS B.** B's insistence that **a placeless story carries Texas in its MATERIAL**
is right, and it is the answer to A's own stated biggest risk, which is that VCT, CMU, troffers
