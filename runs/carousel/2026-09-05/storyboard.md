# Carousel no. 16, storyboard, 2026-09-05

**Story.** ARPA-E funded a University of Houston led coalition, GAMBIT, to use AI to design permanent
magnets that beat neodymium iron boron while weighing supply constraints. Rice's part is choosing
which candidate materials get made and tested.

**CORRECTED AT THE SCORING GATE.** This line read "without imported rare earths" and no claim in
the file says so. c11 gives the target against neodymium iron boron and c14 gives the goal as new
materials while balancing supply constraint concerns. The rare earth free reading is an inference
off c12 and c13 and it reached a published frame as slide 6's dek, which is where a scorer caught
it. Boride and carbide are anion classes, rare earth is a cation class, and Nd2Fe14B, which this
deck prints on slide 3, is a rare earth boride, so the project's NAME excludes nothing at all.

**Record item.** `tx-2026-0124`. **Claims file.** `out/2026-09-05/claims.json`, 18 verified.

## The synthesis, and what came from where

Three directors, three lenses, and the deck takes its spine from one, its cover from another and its
close from the third.

- **The SEARCH lens supplied the organising law**, which is that a body seated on a surface with a
  contact shadow is a thing the record says exists, and a cavity cut into that surface is a thing the
  record only names. The deck ends on a cavity because that is where the record honestly ends. It also
  supplied the rule that nothing is countable except what a claim counts, which is the guard against
  this deck's one real temptation, printing a size for the candidate space that no claim carries.
- **The SUPPLY CHAIN lens supplied the cover and the attribution law.** One colour is the imported
  thing. It is on frames 1, 2 and 3 and it is gone from 6 onward, so the argument that the dependency
  could be designed out is made in paint before it is made in words.
- **The OBJECT lens supplied the close**, frame 9, which is the best single image any of the three
  produced and is where the invention debt gets paid.

**What was rejected from each.** The supply chain's foreshortened word running away from the reader on
frame 2 is the best idea in that treatment and it is a smear at 432px, which its own author said. The
search lens's East Texas ore palette argued itself out of Houston on a real argument and still ends up
asserting a place the story is not in. The object lens's wind turbine is not in c15's confirmed quote
and does not appear.

## Structural laws

1. **LILAC IS THE IMPORTED THING.** `neodymia` appears only where a frame draws rare earth material or
   the incumbent magnet. Frames 1, 2 and 3 carry it. Frames 6, 7, 8 and 9 carry none.
2. **SEATED MEANS MADE. CUT MEANS IMAGINED.** A body with a two-part contact shadow is something the
   record says exists. A cavity with a lit lip, a side wall and a floor is something the record only
   names. Never a black rectangle, which reads as a redaction.
3. **NOTHING IS COUNTABLE EXCEPT WHAT A CLAIM COUNTS.** No claim gives the size of the candidate space,
   so no frame prints one and no field of bodies is bounded or totalable. The only countable sets in
   nine frames are the five import shares on frame 1 and the four partners on frame 9.
4. **NO QUANTITY IS DRAWN AS A LENGTH.** The import shares are drawn as AREA in true plan. The two
   award figures are never put on one baseline, never scaled against each other and never subtracted,
   because the difference is a number neither page carries.
5. **THE TWO AWARD FIGURES ARE IDENTICAL BY CONSTRUCTION**, asserted by frame 8's own script. The
   record does not prefer one, so the drawing may not either.
6. **ONE KEY**, computed in the drawing step and asserted to `window.__txProbe`. Frames 3 and 5 declare
   no drawn light, which is their argument rather than an omission.

## Risks, named before they happen

- **A dark focal can lose at 432px.** Most frames put the eye on the darkest area. Every magnet needs a
  lit plated edge to prove it is a body rather than a hole in the file.
- **Frame 3's undrawn region can read as an unfinished render.** The drawn portion needs real
  per-pixel variance and a hard boundary so the emptiness is a decision.
- **Frame 5 is one light frame against eight dark ones** and could read as another deck's slide. That
  is the intent and it is also the failure mode.
- **Frame 6's intaglio is deck 13's recorded failure**, where a declared engraving shipped as 190
  low-alpha lines a judge could not see. The acceptance item carries a coverage number, not an opinion.
- **The close has no door.** No date, no comment window, no action. That is the honest state of this
  record and the close does not manufacture one.
