The surgeons didn't get faster.

Houston Methodist put four wall cameras in each of the 15 operating rooms of its cardiothoracic suite at Walter Tower in December 2022. Its blog says operative minutes did not fall while turnover times did, so more cases fit the same hours.

The June 27th paper in the Journal of Imaging says less. It estimates about 25 more cases a month over a baseline of about 339. The 95% interval runs from 8.3 to 41.0. The authors call that associated, not caused. A change management program came with the cameras and the study can't separate the two.

The hospital owns part of the vendor, Apella. An Apella employee ran the original analyses and a hospital analyst replicated them.

The deck draws the count, its range and the disclosure.

If the cameras and the program arrived together, which one is another Texas hospital buying?
