About 25 more cases a month.

That is an estimate, not a count. A Journal of Imaging study ties it to the image based AI Houston Methodist put in Walter Tower's 15 room heart surgery suite, four wall cameras to a room.

The paper is firm on the baseline of about 339 a month. Operative minutes showed no significant change. The hospital's blog says turnovers got shorter.

A change management program came with the cameras, and the study can't say which did the work. The authors call the rise associated with the deployment, not caused by it. The hospital holds a minority equity interest in Apella, the vendor.

The study's 95% interval sizes the unknown. It runs from 8.3 to 41.0 cases a month.
