About 25 more surgeries a month.

That is an estimate, not a count. A Journal of Imaging study ties it to the image based AI Houston Methodist put in Walter Tower's 15 room cardiothoracic suite, four wall cameras to a room.

The paper puts the baseline at about 339 a month. Operative minutes showed no significant change. The hospital's blog says turnovers got shorter.

A change management program came with the cameras. The study can't say which did the work. The authors call the rise associated with the deployment, not caused by it. The hospital holds a minority equity interest in Apella, the vendor.

The study's 95% interval sizes the unknown. It runs from 8.3 to 41.0 more cases a month.

The deck draws each case of the month as one sterile container.
