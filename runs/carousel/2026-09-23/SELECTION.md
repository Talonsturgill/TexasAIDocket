# Selection — carousel no. 32, September 23rd, 2026

## The story

**`tx-2026-0182`.** The Office of the Texas Governor directed the Texas Commission on
Environmental Quality on September 21st to issue no permits sought by data centers until the
grid operator completes its interconnection audit, and told every state agency to hold
regulatory approvals related to data center development until that information is in hand. The
agency reports back on its compliance by October 19th.

## The angle, and why it is not the angle any deck in the window already spent

**The audit could only reach what was in the queue. The permit reaches what was never in it.**

The August 3rd directive audits data centers *undergoing ERCOT's interconnection process*
(`tx-2026-0072`). A project that contracts with a plant next door, or builds reciprocating gas
sets behind its own meter, never enters that process and the audit never sees it. The air
permit is different. It is the one state approval that follows the ENGINES rather than the
connection, so a data center that skips the grid entirely still has to come to this agency.

The record already carries that lane twice, by name and by permit number, which is what lets
the deck argue this from documents rather than from inference:

- **`tx-2026-0064`**, Taylor County. The agency's preliminary decision and draft permit for
  Crusoe's gas power plant on the Abilene data center campus. Permit **182126**. Still open.
- **`tx-2026-0058`**, Bexar County. The Vantage Data Centers TX11 air permit O4791, whose
  notice and comment hearing the agency canceled.

## WHAT THIS FILE ASSERTS ABOVE THAT THE FACT CHECK THEN REFUTED

Appended on the day, after two scorers read this file as the committed plan of record and found
it still asserting things the run had cut. **Everything above this line is the plan as it stood
before verification. It is left intact because a plan that was wrong is evidence, and rewriting

correction is what the deck actually shipped on.

  air permit is "the one state approval that follows the ENGINES", and that it is therefore the

  one. No published surface of this deck asserts the mechanism, and the web edition states in its
  own section that none is asserted.

  record carries is a canceled notice and comment hearing on a data center permit in Bexar

  exactly that and no more.

  fact check could establish neither the applicant, the county, that it is an air permit, that
  comments are open, nor a deadline. This file says at line 83 that the frame is "the deck's last
