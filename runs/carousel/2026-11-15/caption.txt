86% is not a share of teachers.

It is the share of Texas school and district leaders who reported teacher use of AI in fall 2025. Texas 2036 published the figure on November 10th in a preview of its report. Report author Renzo Soto says AI can be a useful tool for teachers when used responsibly and effectively.

Only 29% of those leaders reported a district policy on student AI use. Just 20% reported one for teachers and staff.

By Texas 2036's count 42 states have issued some form of statewide AI guidance for schools. Texas is not among them.

Texas 2036 puts statewide guidance first among its four priorities. Safety standards, teacher training and AI literacy follow. The guidance would build on laws the Legislature already passed.
