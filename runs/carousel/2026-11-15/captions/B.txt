AI isn't coming to Texas classrooms. It's already there.

Texas 2036 previewed its report on AI in schools on November 10th. It says 86% of Texas school and district leaders reported teachers using AI in fall 2025, and 61% reported student use.

What hasn't arrived is the rulebook. Only 29% of those leaders reported a district policy governing student AI use. Just 20% reported one for teachers and staff. By the group's count 42 states have issued some form of statewide AI guidance for schools. Texas is not among them.

Texas 2036 also reports that nearly 85% of Texas voters call protecting student data and privacy extremely or very important if schools use AI.

The deck walks one school day to the board room. The first of the group's four priorities is statewide guidance built on laws the Legislature already passed. Has your school board written its own?
