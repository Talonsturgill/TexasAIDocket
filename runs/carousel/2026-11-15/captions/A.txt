86% is not a count of teachers.

It is the share of Texas school and district leaders who reported teacher use of AI in fall 2025, per Texas 2036 on November 10th. Report author Renzo Soto calls AI a useful tool for teachers when used responsibly and effectively.

Just 29% of those leaders reported a district policy on student AI use. Just 20% reported one for teachers and staff.

Nothing statewide sits above them. By Texas 2036's count 42 states have issued some form of statewide AI guidance for schools. Texas is not among them.

So Texas 2036 puts statewide guidance first, built on laws the Legislature already passed. Safety standards, teacher training and AI literacy follow.

The deck walks one school day, gym to board room.

Statewide guidance is the first ask. Should it come from Austin or stay with each school board?
