Rule 203.6(a) lets a written transcription of a recording be used like a stenographic deposition. A court may still require a certified reporter's transcript first, for good cause shown.

203.6 Use. (a) Non-stenographic recording; transcription.
A non-stenographic recording of an oral deposition, or a written transcription of all or part of such a recording, may be used to the same extent as a deposition taken by stenographic means.
However, the court, for good cause shown, may require that the party seeking to use a non-stenographic recording or written transcription first obtain a complete transcript of the deposition recording from a certified court reporter.
