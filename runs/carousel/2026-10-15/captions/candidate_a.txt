OPENING MOVE: the object. STRUCTURE: Ladder.

A deposition transcript an AI platform made.

It came from a Montgomery County wrongful termination suit, where the deposition was also on video. The trial court struck it in April 2025 because no certified shorthand reporter prepared it. The Ninth District denied relief. The Supreme Court of Texas heard the mandamus petition on October 6th, with 8 of 9 justices sitting.

Justice Kyle Hawkins asked why the court should set aside "this efficient, cost-effective tool." Justice Jane Bland asked who stands behind the transcribed product.

The deck draws the 18 months as a stair, with the transcript on every slide.

The court has not ruled. Rule 203.6(a) already lets a judge require a certified reporter's transcript for good cause. If anyone can check the page against the video, does it still need a person behind it?
