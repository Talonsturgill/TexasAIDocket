OPENING MOVE: the before and after. STRUCTURE: Clock.

A Montgomery County court struck a deposition transcript made with AI. 18 months later the Supreme Court of Texas heard argument over it.

The deposition was recorded on video and transcribed by an AI platform. Courthouse News reports the employer moved to strike the transcript weeks later. It was struck in April 2025 because no certified shorthand reporter prepared it.

The mandamus petition was filed May 29th, 2025. Eight of nine justices heard it on October 6th. Justice Kyle Hawkins called the platform "this efficient, cost-effective tool." Justice Jane Bland asked who stands behind the transcribed product.

The court has not ruled. A Texas judge can already order a certified reporter's transcript for good cause. If the video recording is the check, what will count as good cause?
