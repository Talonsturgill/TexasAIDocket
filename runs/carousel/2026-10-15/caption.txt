A Montgomery County court struck a transcript made with AI. 18 months later the Supreme Court of Texas heard argument over it.

The case began as a wrongful termination suit, Courthouse News reports. The employer did not object to the software at the deposition. Weeks later it moved to strike. In April 2025 the trial court struck it because no certified shorthand reporter prepared it.

Justice Kyle Hawkins asked why the court should set aside "this efficient, cost-effective tool," Courthouse News reports. Justice Jane Bland asked where the court would find the assurance of a human being standing behind the transcribed product.

The court's orders through October 9th carry no ruling. A judge can already order a certified reporter's transcript for good cause. If the deposition's video is the check, what will count as good cause?
