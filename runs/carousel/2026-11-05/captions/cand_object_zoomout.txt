Laser chips, made only in Sugar Land.

Applied Optoelectronics makes optical transceivers for internet and AI data centers. Its quarterly report places that work in its Sugar Land facility in Fort Bend County.

The chief financial officer says the company's 2027 expansion in the United States will take longer, citing "longer than expected time to obtain necessary electrical power among other construction related issues."

The company still expects capacity for over 1 million 800G and 1.6 Tb transceivers a month by the end of 2027. Its chief executive cites a recent agreement with a Tier 1 hyperscale customer.

So the wait for power is not only a data center's. Here a parts maker names it. The record does not say which building waits.

The deck walks a dark factory bay taped out for machines, drawn to illustrate.
