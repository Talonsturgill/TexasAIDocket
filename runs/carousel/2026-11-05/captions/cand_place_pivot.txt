The laser chips are made in Sugar Land and nowhere else.

Applied Optoelectronics of Sugar Land in Fort Bend County builds optical transceivers for internet and AI data centers. Its chief financial officer expects equipment for over 500,000 pieces of 800G and 1.6 Tb products a month by the end of 2026. Capacity for over 1 million a month is expected by the end of 2027.

The same November 5th results release says the company's 2027 expansion in the United States will take longer. The reason given is a longer than expected wait for electrical power, among other construction issues. It does not say which building is waiting.

The slides walk a new bay taped out for machines and lit only through its dock door, drawn to illustrate.

If the wait for power stretches on, does the end of 2027 still hold?
