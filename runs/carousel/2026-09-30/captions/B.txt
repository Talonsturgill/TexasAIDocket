Who reads a STAAR answer first?

Unless the test is in Spanish, a machine does. The Texas Education Agency has scored written answers that way since December 2023. The machine's score stands on about 75% of them.

It learned from about 3,000 human scored answers per question. It has to agree with scorers as often as scorers agree with each other. Close calls, blank answers and a random sample go to people, and the person's score counts.

This fall about 27,200 students got more credit after content experts reread short typed answers. The agency said the engine scored none of them.

A parent can take a concern to the district. Its testing staff can request a human rescore, and the fee is waived if the score changes.

The deck runs from one desk alone to a field of 27,200.
