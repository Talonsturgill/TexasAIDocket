Written STAAR answers were fully human scored. Since December 2023 an engine reads them first.

The agency said keeping full human scoring would have cost millions more a year. Now the engine gives the score of record on about 75% of answers. Close calls, flagged answers and a random sample go to two people. One scores and one audits. Spanish-language answers are 100 percent human scored.

This fall about 27,200 students got more credit. The agency said none of those answers had been scored by the engine.

A parent or guardian with a concern can go to the district. Its testing staff can request a rescore for a fee. A rescore goes to people. If the score changes, the fee is waived.

The deck follows one student desk from an empty practice field to a field of 27,200.
