# Slide 9 concepts, BUILT IN TEXAS

1. THE MIRROR (chosen). Frame 1's exact aerial camera, restaged over an undamaged Texas street at peach evening: limestone ranch houses with whole shingle roofs, live oaks throwing long violet shade on caliche verges, no rims at all, and the gold route arriving from the left edge onto the empty street. Only the close can have it, because the picture is frame 1 with the storm and the judging taken out, so it asks the dek's question (where is the first Texas user) by showing Texas ground the tool has not yet been used on.

2. THE CLOSED CASE. A rugged drone hard case, latches shut, set on the limestone steps of a drawn county courthouse at evening, studio key raking across the stone. It reads as a tool waiting at a Texas door, though it risks implying a refusal and a courthouse is a real building type that drifts toward a likeness.
