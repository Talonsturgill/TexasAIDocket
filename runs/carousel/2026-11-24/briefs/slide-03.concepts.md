# Frame 3, STITCHED, three concepts

1. **The plates settle into the map (TXBENCH plan relief).** A fan of loose anodised drone frame plates, each carrying its own patch of the neighbourhood in shallow relief, lies stacked and turned on a violet bench at upper left, casting onto one another. Lower down they fall into line and the patches fuse into one flush slab of map that fills the bottom half, where every building footprint rises as a hollow cool rim and the streets carry raised footprint ribbons. Only this beat has it because the picture is the stitch itself: the same imagery is loose and rimless above, joined and aligned to footprints below, and the rims on the joined plane are FIG.buildings.

2. **The light table (txthree studio, raked macro).** A pile of printed drone frames on a backlit table, the top prints sliding off the pile at a low raking angle, the lower ones butted edge to edge and glowing through as one sheet with cool footprint outlines laid over them. It belongs to this beat because a print pile is "many pictures" and a backlit single sheet is "one map", but it reads as a desk still life and the November 22nd deck already ran studio benches.

3. **The camera footprints over the ground (txthree aerial at dawn).** A survey quad crossing low over the neighbourhood, its overlapping camera footprints drawn as pale rectangles on the roofs, overlapping like shingles, and behind it the ground already resolved into one rimmed map. It is the only beat that shows where the frames come from, but it shares frame 1's aerial camera and world and frame 2's outdoor key.

**Pick: 1.** Only it shows the operation the headline names, many separate pictures turning into one footprint aligned map, in a medium (plan relief) neither neighbour uses, and its rim count can be the figure exactly.

## Composition with the type

Frame 2 seats its type at the bottom and frame 4 top left, so this frame's type sits top right, a left aligned column x 470 to 1016 over the quiet violet bench, hook in two lines, dek under it. The loose plates cascade down the left column beside the type, their casts reaching toward it, and the stitched map takes the full width of the lower half, bleeding right and bottom, with one rim cut by the right edge.
