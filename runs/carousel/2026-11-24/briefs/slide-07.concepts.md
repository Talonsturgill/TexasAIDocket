# Slide 07, THE ROAD OUT, three concepts

1. **The junction (chosen).** A white lineman bucket truck has stopped at a Gulf Coast residential junction because a fallen live oak and its torn gumbo root plate lie across the street ahead in sky-coloured standing water, and the gold route bends right in front of its bumper and leaves the frame's right edge while a lineman, three quarters away, points the same way. Only this beat has a vehicle at a choice of roads, a blocked way and a way through, so swapping in any other headline leaves the gold turn meaning nothing.

2. **The puddle worm's eye.** The camera sits 40 cm over wet asphalt, a sheet of standing water fills the bottom half and mirrors the cobalt and peach sky, and the gold route skirts the puddle's rim and swings away past a root plate standing up like a wall. The route as the edge of the water is the road model's job made literal, but the truck and the crew read as scale only, and the picture says "flooded" more than "routed".

3. **The routed street map in relief.** A raked tabletop model of the drawn block in gumbo clay and oyster shell, streets cut as channels, a tiny oak and puddle blocking one, the gold route as a raised brass wire threading the detour. It explains routing cleanly, but it repeats frame 3's relief medium and frame 1's overhead neighbourhood, so it fails the neighbour test.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where a crew, an obstruction and the turn around it are all in one second's read, and its medium camera at street height differs from frame 6's dusk tailgate close up (cobalt dusk, operator and laptop) and frame 8's interior pegboard.

**Composition with the type.** Camera 3.4 m up, behind and left of the truck, honey key from the front right (az about 40, el 16) so the oak, truck and lineman throw long shadows toward the camera over the wet street. The sky owns the top 30 percent above the far roofs and their filled plates. The headline "The road out" sits top right in the sky, one line, large, right aligned, and the dek in a narrow right column under it over the far sky and roofs, clear of the truck's boom on the left. The bottom third is wet asphalt, curb and the gold turn.
