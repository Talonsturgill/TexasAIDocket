# Slide 05 concepts, WHAT IT LEARNED ("Taught partly on Harvey")

1. **The archive rack, one flooded plate lifted.** A long powder coated cradle of anodised plates leans like file cards along an indigo epoxy lab bench and runs out of the left edge, each plate's face a sliver of drawn aerial neighbourhood, while a nitrile gloved hand lifts one plate clear into the window light, its face a drawn Gulf Coast block under brown floodwater. Only this beat has it because the rack is the dataset made countable (one plate per orthomosaic) and the one plate held up is the flood the headline names.

2. **A core sample of storms.** A tall acrylic column on a studio sweep holds the plates stacked flat like sediment, the lowest layers stained with a brown flood line, seen from a low worm's eye so the stack towers into the cobalt top of the frame. It belongs to this beat because "taught partly on" is layered learning, but a stack hides the faces and the flood reads as a stain rather than a neighbourhood.

3. **A light table of loose frames.** Overhead, a backlit light table covered edge to edge in small aerial tiles, a loupe resting on one flooded tile, warm window light raking across. It speaks to "learned from drone imagery", but it is top down tiles on a flat surface, the tray-as-chart look the judges just charged, and it echoes the stitched-map frames earlier in the deck.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where the count (265 plates) and the Harvey thread (the one flooded plate lifted from the many) are the same object in one glance, and it is an interior close shot between frame 4's outdoor roof and frame 6's outdoor dusk tailgate.

**Composition.** The rack enters from the left edge in the middle band and rakes away to the back left; the lifted plate hangs upper right against the cobalt wall with the window's warm patch behind it. The bench top's indigo resin fills the lower third and is the quiet field: the headline sits bottom left in two lines over it, the dek under it at 25 px, ending above the footer. The wall top left stays as soft violet shade so the kicker breathes.
