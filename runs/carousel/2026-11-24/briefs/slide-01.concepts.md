# Slide 01 concepts, AFTER THE STORM

1. **The judged subdivision, aerial oblique at evening.** A drone's eye from 30 m over a Gulf Coast subdivision after a storm: two kit brick roofs cut by the bottom edge and one between them stripped to tan decking with a blue tarp, a fallen live oak across the street, one gold route bending past its crown, and every roof to the hazed horizon carrying a thin hollow cool rim. Only this beat has it because the headline is "every roof", and the picture is every roof of a whole neighbourhood at once, each already outlined but not yet judged.

2. **One roof, macro, at shingle scale.** A 40 cm wide crop of a torn roof plane, shingle tabs lifted and OSB nail rows bared, with one hollow cerulean rim edge running across the frame like a drawn line laid on the real roof. It answers "read" (one structure judged) but loses "every" and "in minutes", so it could serve a later close up frame as easily as the cover.

3. **The drone's frame as a lit window over the dusk.** A survey quadcopter in silhouette in the upper sky with its camera footprint falling as a soft lit rectangle across a dark neighbourhood, roofs inside the footprint rimmed and roofs outside plain. It says "drone imagery" strongly, but the November 20th deck already drew a survey quadcopter, and the footprint reads as a spotlight rather than an assessment.

**Pick: concept 1.** It is the only one where the picture fails without the word "every": the count of rims to the horizon is the sentence, and frame 9 can restage the same camera over undamaged ground.

**Composition with the type.** The headline sits high in the cobalt sky, top left, two lines, the dek directly under it across the hazed far rows where the carpet dissolves into peach fog, seated with CH.seat. The near third of the frame belongs to the cut roofs and the oak, so nothing near or detailed sits under the type. Neighbour frame 2 is a ground level long lens road, so an aerial at 30 m is a different world, camera and scale.
