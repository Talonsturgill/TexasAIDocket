Lockheed Martin made this call.

On October 14th the company said Hadrian will bring a highly automated cell of 24 machines into its Grand Prairie plant, run by Lockheed's own production workforce and expected to be operational in early 2027.

The company's own statewide figure is 2,000 Texas jobs.

A state filing puts the plant at 1701 West Marshall Drive in Dallas County. Lockheed is investing $300 million there.

Nearly 800 production employees work at the plant and made 250,000 parts in 2025. More than 30 high precision machines went in over a few months. Nearly 100 new ones are expected by the end of 2026.

Hadrian calls its software Opus AI powered. The release never says what AI does inside this cell.

The slides draw the cell as 24 empty bays beside the plant's own machines.

If the cell is operational in early 2027 as expected, will Lockheed say what AI does at those 24 bays on West Marshall Drive?
