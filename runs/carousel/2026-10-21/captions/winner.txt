On October 14th Lockheed Martin said Hadrian will bring a highly automated 24 machine cell to the Grand Prairie plant. A state filing puts the plant in Dallas County.

Announced. A cell expected in early 2027, to be run by Lockheed's own workforce. A $300 million investment in the plant. Plans for 2,000 Texas jobs, the company's own statewide figure.

Already done, by Lockheed's count. More than 30 machines added in a few months. The plant made 250,000 parts in 2025.

Hadrian says its own factories combine process engineering, AI and robotics. It says its software Opus "interprets legacy designs and automates manufacturing and inspection". Neither statement says what AI would do in this cell.
