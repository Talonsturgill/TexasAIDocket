The AI in this deal has no job description.

Lockheed Martin said on October 14th that Hadrian will bring a highly automated 24 machine cell to its Grand Prairie plant in Dallas County.

Announced. A cell expected in early 2027 and run by Lockheed's own workforce. A $300 million investment in the plant. 2,000 Texas jobs, the company's own statewide figure.

Already done, by Lockheed's count. More than 30 machines added in a few months. Nearly 800 production employees made 250,000 parts in 2025.

Hadrian calls its own factories AI powered. It says its software Opus "interprets legacy designs and automates manufacturing and inspection." Neither list says what AI will do in this cell.

The deck runs from a finished part to 24 empty bays.

The cell is due in early 2027. Will anyone say what its AI does before it cuts a part?
