# STORYBOARD — carousel no. 18, 2026-09-08

## THE SYNTHESIS, and what each of the three rooms contributed

The spine taken is **THE SPECIFICATION**, because it is the truest reading of the record. This is
not a debate and it is not a scandal. It is a purchase instruction with an exclusion clause in
it, and every interesting fact in the file is a fact about the SHAPE of that instruction.

All three rooms independently found the same physical fact and it is the deck's turn. **The
sentence that draws Austin's boundary around artificial intelligence runs off the foot of page
one at the word "artificial" and finishes at the top of page two.** Nobody invented that. It is a
property of the executed document, confirmed against a local extraction of the city's own PDF
made in this run, and the deck spends frames 6 and 7 on it so the reader's own thumb performs the
page turn.

- **From the SPECIFICATION room, the light model, and it is the whole variety argument.** All
  eight excluded registers are an interior or a lightless field with exactly ONE source. This
  deck goes outdoors and **replaces the key with a dome**. There is no key light and **no cast
  shadow anywhere in nine frames.** Every value is a function of how much sky a surface can see.
  It is the physical inverse of deck 17, where a plane source under the floor made top faces
  darkest and ran every cast upward.
- **From the SPECIFICATION room, the integrity point that makes the register honest.** The
  equipment is ORDERED and NOT ACQUIRED. So nothing in this deck is installed and nothing is
  switched on. There are no lit fixture pools, because there are no fixtures yet, and frame 4's
  pole carries a bare mounting boss. Drawing lit equipment would assert a deployment that has not
  happened, which is the fastest available way to fail this project's own standard.
- **From the SPECIFICATION room, the inversion's position.** A bright full bleed document sheet
  has occupied slot 5 for five decks running and no gate can see that. This deck's inversion is
  at **frame 7**, where it also lands on the resolution's own page break, so the value arc
  performs the swipe before a word is read.
- **From the NEGATIVE SPACE room, the absence discipline.** Every frame that says something is
  missing carries a mono locator naming Resolution No. 20260812-017 and the extent read. No frame
  asserts a silence wider than that one document.
- **From the NEGATIVE SPACE room, the brightest-absence idea, which is the best single move in
  the deck.** Frame 3's schedule has five ruled column heads and nothing under any of them, and
  that empty run is the FRAME'S BRIGHTEST AREA rather than a black bar. An unfilled field drawn
  dark ships as a redaction. Drawn as a lit enamel surface it ships as a form nobody has filled.
- **From the PARK room, the sky itself.** Austin's night sky is amber and lifted rather than
  black, which is true of any greenbelt park on the Balcones side and is what gives this deck the
  value band the ledger asks for on physics rather than on taste. **The last three decks got dark
  by removing light. This one gets dark by drawing the correct amount of it.**
- **From the PARK room, the rule that nothing may read as a subject.** Zero human, face,
  silhouette, animal or vehicle forms across nine frames, and a camera's output is never drawn as
  an image. This record does not draw people being watched.

**ONE ROOM'S LAW WAS RELAXED, and the reason is a briefing error of mine.** The specification
room wrote a law forbidding any drawn count, on the grounds that the record counts nothing and
that c6's equipment list was truncated. That was true of the BRIEF and not of the record. The
claims brief handed to all three rooms truncated every quote at 230 characters, which cut c6
mid-list at "non-re". The full claim carries the whole enumeration, and `compute.py` parses the
counts out of the documents' own numbered and lettered lists. **A count is licensed here when
`compute.py` produced it from a source enumeration and the frame names the set it counted over.**
Nothing else is countable.

## STRUCTURAL LAWS — every frame obeys all five, and each is checkable off the PNGs

**L1. THE SKY DOME IS THE ONLY LIGHT. NO KEY, NO CAST SHADOW, NOWHERE.** Every body carries three
declared values, an up face, a side face and an under face, with a minimum separation asserted by
the frame's own script, and the up face is the brightest region of that body on every frame. A
body is seated by an occlusion gradient at its foot, declared through `data-contacts` and
measured like any other contact. **The second half of the law is what stops nine soft grey
fields: every frame carries at least one hard silhouette against the sky.**

**L2. A PART IS HARDWARE. A PROPERTY IS A FINISH.** Artificial intelligence is never drawn as a
body, an eye, a lens, a brain, a network or a glow, on any frame. The record forbids a property
of two named parts, so the drawing treats it as a finish specified out of a part. This is the law
that stops the deck inventing the one thing the record does not contain.

**L3. NOTHING IS INSTALLED AND NOTHING IS SWITCHED ON.** The order is placed and the equipment is
not acquired. No frame draws a lit fixture, a powered camera, a flying drone or an energised
anything. Frame 4's pole carries a bare mounting boss and that is the deck's whole position on
the state of the world.

**L4. A BREAK IN THE RECORD IS A BREAK IN THE DECK, AND THE DECK SAYS WHOSE BREAK IT IS.** c8
reaches the foot of frame 6 at "artificial" and c9 enters at the head of frame 7, because that is
the resolution's own page break and it is verified against a local extraction. Nothing is capped
with an ellipsis and no break is invented.

**L5. EVERY ABSENCE NAMES ITS SEARCH.** A frame asserting that something is not in the document
carries a 26px JetBrains Mono locator reading `RESOLUTION 20260812-017 / READ IN FULL, BOTH
PAGES`. Frames 3, 7 and 9 carry it.

### THE COLLISION CHECK WAS BUILT, IT FIRED, AND ITS THRESHOLD TURNED OUT TO BE UNSATISFIABLE

The specification room asked for a perceptual distance check against the last eight decks' tokens,
run before the first frame rather than after a judge finds a collision by eye. It was built. It
extracts every hex in the last eight `artwork.json` entries, which is **74 distinct tokens**, and
measures CIELAB distance from each of this deck's.

**Seven of thirteen came back under dE 10 and an optimiser was written to move them.** It was
wrong, twice, and both attempts are recorded because the second failure is the finding.

The first run maximised distance with no constraint and returned galvanized steel as `#83AE7C`, a
green, live oak as `#2E0119`, a maroon, and laser toner as `#092F01`. Every token satisfied the
number and none of them was its own material any more. The second run added a hue constraint and
returned galvanized steel as `#77A68E`, still a green, because HSV hue is meaningless for a near
neutral and a two count change in one channel swings it across the wheel.

**The honest reading is that the neutral bands are saturated after eight decks.** Seventy four
prior tokens spread across the pale warm neutrals, the near blacks and the cool greys leave no
room at dE 12 for a material that IS a pale warm neutral, a near black or a cool grey. Galvanized
steel is grey in every deck that draws it. A dE floor applied blindly does not produce variety, it
produces a deck that no longer looks like anywhere.

So four tokens were moved deliberately, each in the direction its own material argues for, and
the rest are stated with their measured distance and the word **material bound** rather than
optimised into a lie. `flag_red` is dE 0.0 from four prior decks BY DESIGN, because it is the
reserved brand token and a reservation with a per-deck variant is not a reservation.

**The variety this deck actually buys is its light model**, which is a dome with no key and no
cast shadow anywhere, and that is a stronger claim than a hex value. The threshold question is
written up as an upgrade proposal rather than left as a number nobody can meet.
