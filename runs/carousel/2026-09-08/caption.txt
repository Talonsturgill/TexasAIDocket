The rule arrived inside a parks item.

Austin City Council adopted Resolution No. 20260812-017 on August 12th, 2026. It directs the City Manager to buy and turn on parks security equipment. The same text bars that officer from considering a camera or drone that depends upon artificial intelligence to conduct surveillance or analysis.

April had already said no. Artificial intelligence or machine learning tools are already among what is not permitted in City Code Chapter 2-19. That listing leaves an exception for whatever the city's own policy allows. The August clause leaves none. It also binds one officer for one kind of purchase.

The council's own action notes record the item approved and send the vote details to minutes approved at a later meeting. The resolution defines that dependence nowhere. Who decides whether a camera depends upon it?
