The rule arrived inside a parks item.

Austin City Council adopted Resolution No. 20260812-017 on August 12th, 2026. It directs the City Manager to buy and turn on parks security equipment. The same text bars him from considering a camera or drone that depends upon artificial intelligence to conduct surveillance or analysis.

The forbidding one is the weaker instrument. The council's April measure is codified at City Code Chapter 2-19 and gates a purchase on council approval and an assessment filed four weeks ahead. This one directs a manager. It creates no offense and reaches only what the city has yet to buy.

The city's voting record has loaded no meeting later than May 28th, 2026. The resolution defines that dependence nowhere and names nobody to apply it. Who makes that call when a camera comes up?
