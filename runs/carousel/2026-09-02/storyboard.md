# Carousel no. 13 — storyboard, 2026-09-02

## THE SYNTHESIS, and what was taken from where

Three treatments came back. All three independently reached the same three disciplines, which is
the strongest signal in the room: spend the reserved red only on September 22nd, deny it to
September 3rd because a canceled date is not a door, and draw no person anywhere.

**The spine, the register and the light are lens 1's**, the empty room. Granite Mountain sunset red
granite is what the Capitol is actually faced in, and one key at azimuth 118 degrees and 8 degrees
elevation is the light that gets into a room nobody is using. A grazing key lights edges and leaves
flat faces dark, which is what an empty room looks like, so the lens is bought by one physical
choice rather than by a mood.

**The value argument is lens 1's and it is the best single idea in any of the three.** What the
state PUBLISHED is the brightest frame in the deck, frame 5, the interim charge on bond with no
drawn light at all. What the state did NOT publish is the darkest, frame 3. A reader gets the
argument out of the value structure before reading a word.

**The integrity law is lens 3's and it is the sharpest.** A FRAME ONLY DRAWS A PLACE THE RECORD
PUTS THE THING IN. c19 names a street corner so a street corner may be drawn. No claim puts the
scheduling tool inside a building, so no frame draws a hospital, and **c18 is excluded from the
deck entirely** rather than trusted to stay apart from c17 in a layout. A layout delivers joins
that no sentence makes.

**Frame 8's construction is lens 3's.** Dell's 60.9 is a SEGMENT figure and 47.0 is the company,
and 60.9 is the larger of the two, so any drawing implying containment or comparison is false. Two
engraved fields, a deep uncut gutter, no rule or axis or bracket between them, no shared baseline,
and the words "AI server business" inside the same bounded field as the figure.

**The colour law is lens 2's, adapted to granite.** Material carries attribution. Dressed stone
carries the record's own published words. Machined stainless carries a party's own words. Bond
carries the charge. Nothing crosses, so a reader who never reaches the mono attribution line still
sees that a committee's charge and a union member's characterisation are made of different stuff.

**Rejected: lens 3's screen-spill palette**, on its own evidence. It flagged that frames 1, 3 and 7
would sit within nine L star of each other under one light idea, which is the exact fault deck 12's
ledger entry names. Its warning about granite is heeded a different way: deck 1 and deck 8 both
shipped #9A3B2A, so this deck's stone token is #6B3C39, violet-leaning and measurably off both.

**Rejected: lens 2's cast bronze plate**, reluctantly and for one reason. A cast committee plaque
is an object no source describes, and the deck's subject is a record that did not say something. A
register that invents an artifact to talk about an absent document is the one place an integrity
judge should push, and lens 2 named that risk itself. Its fastener idea survives in frame 4.

## THE ONE STRUCTURAL LAW

**ONE MACHINED THING PER FRAME, SEATED ON GRANITE, AND THE FRAME'S BRIGHTEST PIXELS ARE ALWAYS ON
THAT THING'S EDGES AND NEVER ON ITS FACE.**

Second clause, and it is why the law is written rather than assumed: **nothing in nine frames is
broken, spalled, fractured, torn, struck through or knocked out.** No document says anything was,
and a deck about an unexplained cancellation must never draw a cause.

Checkable per frame: name the machined thing, then measure that the frame's top one percent of
luminance falls on its edges.

renders its OWN cancellation note in class="redText", so the source's red marks the CANCELED row

a provenance claim about a colour nobody fetched. The exception is bought HERE instead, in the
doctrine, which is the more honest place for it. One frame in nine may buy an exception by
declaring it. A second one may not.

## THE RESERVED RED

Spent once, frame 9, on September 22nd and on nothing else in nine frames. As a filled GROUND under
feldspar mono, never as ink, because #BF0A30 has a relative luminance of 0.1149 and cannot clear
4.5 as ink on a dark frame without being walked toward white until it stops being red.

**September 3rd is denied the red on every frame it appears on.** A canceled date is not a door, and
the deck's argument is that the date which looked like the decision is not the one a reader can act
on.

Priors are 27.7, 22.2, 15.6 and 21.2, so a median near 30 is an unoccupied band and still
decisively mid to dark. ONE light frame, not a light deck. **This array is a plan and the ledger
entry takes measured L star off the rendered PNGs.** Deck 7 declared a band without measuring it
and deck 12 shipped on top of the value it meant to avoid.

**HALF THE ARGUMENT LANDS AND HALF IS ONLY TRUE IN THE NUMBERS, and saying which is which is the
point of measuring.** Frame 5 at 91.8 against a deck median near 20 is unmistakable in the contact
sheet, so a reader gets "the one thing the state published is the only lit thing here" before
reading a word. The dark half does not land the same way: frames 3, 7 and 8 sit inside about two L
star of each other, which no reader can see, so frame 3 reads as the dark end because its upper
half is empty rather than because it is measurably darkest. The ordering is real and the
PERCEPTION of it is carried by composition rather than by value.
That ordering was NOT true when the frames were first built: frame 3 rendered as a mid maroon at
20.0 while frames 1, 4 and 8 sat in the low teens, so the deck's best single idea was inverted in
the pixels and every gate was green. A pixel critic reading the two frames side by side is what
caught it, and the fix was to darken frame 3 and lift the three that had drifted.

The deck median of 20.4 sits between two priors rather than in the unoccupied band the plan aimed
at, and that is recorded rather than rounded toward the plan. The number a run publishes about
itself is measured off what it made, or it is not a measurement.
