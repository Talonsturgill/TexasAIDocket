The interim charge
Senate Committee on Economic Development. Titled "Preparing the Texas Workforce for AI (Artificial Intelligence)".
"Study the impact of AI on the Texas workforce and its implications for economic competitiveness."
The charge closes by asking the committee to "Make recommendations to ensure the Texas labor market remains a national leader in growth and opportunity."
