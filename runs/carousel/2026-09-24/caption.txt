A pod comes down 330 feet on a line.

Zipline wants FAA approval to begin drone package delivery in Amarillo, Austin, El Paso, Houston and San Antonio. The FAA's draft environmental assessment of the plan is open for comment. The draft says Zipline prepared it under FAA supervision.

At a delivery the aircraft would hover about 75 seconds. On the way down the pod steers itself sideways and evaluates the spot. The draft says an image goes to an operator when the pod can't identify the target and judge it. It gives no figure for how often.

The last slide carries the FAA's comment address. Comments close October 11th. Will the final version say how often an operator is sent an image?
