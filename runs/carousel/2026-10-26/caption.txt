Rowlett zoned for drone hubs. Round Rock hasn't.

May 20th, 2025. Rowlett's City Council voted 6 to 0 to add drone hubs to its zoning code. Zipline was the applicant. DroneXL reports the rule came before Zipline opened there.

October 9th. Zipline began taking orders in Round Rock, DroneXL reports. The city puts the hub in its extraterritorial jurisdiction near North Kenney Fort and Joe DiMaggio Boulevards. Its zoning doesn't specifically address drone hubs.

Now the city is working with Zipline toward an in-city hub behind Renaissance Square on Interstate 35. It says that site will help it write rules for future hubs. It routes operational feedback to Zipline and safety concerns to the FAA.

The deck weighs what Zipline says the aircraft decides against what the city still decides.

Which order serves neighbors better, the rule first or the hub first?
