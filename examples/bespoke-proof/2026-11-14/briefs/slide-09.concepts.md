# Slide 09 concepts, the close: No install date announced

1. **The split-flap board.** A free-standing split-flap departure board in the studio, seen low from its near end as it runs back into the room: the top row reads AWARD over $19 million on cyan flaps, and the IN SERVICE row below is a date field of blank flaps, grouped like a day, a month and a year, with nothing on them. A split-flap board is the one object whose whole job is to announce a time, so the one row it cannot fill is this headline and no other.

2. **The tagged shipper.** The November award's nineteen wafers stacked between spacer rings in a sealed clear shipping jar on the studio sweep, a manila tag tied to its lid with its IN SERVICE line left blank. Packed but undated says the award is real and the date is missing, but a sealed shipment also hints at a delivery the announcement never mentions.

3. **The band dater.** A big office band dater stamp raised over a sheet, macro and low, its date bands turned to their blank faces and the paper under it unstamped. It answers "when does Texas Tech put a date on it" literally, but a band dater is an office object fewer readers know at a glance, and the empty sheet reads as nothing as easily as no date.

**Pick: 1, the split-flap board.** It is the only picture whose subject exists to announce a date, so swapping in any other headline breaks it, it reads in a second at feed size as a schedule with a blank, and it is a medium (type built as an object) no other frame in either earlier deck used. The frame before is the chips (dense compute), so a single wide board in a bright studio, seen from below, differs in world, camera and scale.

**Composition (as built).** The board spans the top half of the frame at a slight turn, the eye level with the award row so that row runs level. Its two posts frame the lower half, and the headline sits between them in two lines with the dek under it, like the board's own caption, on the violet floor. The blank IN SERVICE row is the lowest row of the board, so the empty date sits directly above "No install date announced". A first pass had the camera low at the near end with the row in steep perspective; qa.py read the flap edges of a slanted row as rules through the award label, so the row was levelled and its glyphs kept inside the flap faces.

**Honesty.** Nothing on the board implies a date: the date cards are blank cream on every face, the slashes between the groups only say the field is a date, and the one card tipped on its hinge is blank front and back, so it shows waiting and never a value. The AWARD row's flaps number the label's own length, computed in code, and the code throws unless that label carries FIG.nov_wafers. The dek's picture sentence becomes "The board is drawn to illustrate."

**Rework (critic round 1).** The empty date is now the loudest thing on the board: the IN SERVICE row is built at 1.45 times the award module in pale cream cards with raised slashes between the groups and one card tipped mid fall, the award row is dimmed a step, the louvre block is gone, the posts are taller so the feet and their contact shadow fill the lower frame, and the ground is a polished violet resin that fades into the sweep. A mirrored reflection of the lit face was not built because at this eye height it falls below the frame.
