# Frame 5 concepts, "May added roughly $4.5 million"

1. **Light through the gap.** Seen from high above a deep blue studio floor, four whole wide bandgap wafers and one cleaved half float on steel lift pins in a receding row, clear crystal tinted amber, tea green, honey and lilac, and the back light passes straight through each one and lays a coloured print of it on the floor beside its pins. Only this beat could have it, because being clear to visible light is the plainest thing a wide bandgap does, and the rough cleaved half at the front of the row is the "roughly" in the headline.

2. **The cleave, at 30 cm.** A macro of one amber silicon carbide half wafer standing on its edge, its raw cleave face in the foreground showing the crystal's stepped terraces, backlit so the crystal glows, with four whole wafers soft and out of focus behind. Only this beat carries a cleave, but at that scale the four whole units go soft and the count stops reading.

3. **The furnace window.** A crystal growth furnace's sight glass in a lit interior, the boule of silicon carbide glowing inside, four sliced wafers and a broken half laid on a cooling plate below it. It says where wide bandgap material comes from, yet it is a room story, the count sits small in a corner and the furnace pulls the eye from the figure.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where the picture itself explains what bandgap research works on (crystal light passes through, where every other frame of the deck shows opaque silicon mirrors) while the count stays four and a half at a glance. It is not the boat on a bench (no boat, no bench, a high aerial camera rather than eye level), and the type sits bottom left over the darker near floor, not top left.

Composition. The row recedes from the big cleaved half in the lower right toward the far upper left, so the lower left stays an unlit stretch of deep blue floor. The headline sits there in two lines with the dek under it, the whole block ending above the footer.

## Rebuild (critic round, REBUILD at 4.8)

The critic found concept 1 arbitrary (a rainbow that fits any "$4.x million"), read the coloured floor prints as ghost copies that muddied the count, and the pins and flat discs as primitives. The frame is rebuilt on a new concept.

4. **The widening gap.** Four whole wide bandgap wafers and one cleaved half lie flat in a diagonal row on a deep blue sweep, each lit from inside the way such a crystal glows under ultraviolet light. The glow climbs from amber at the near end through coral and rose to bright violet at the far end, the order a wider gap moves light toward shorter colours. The cleaved half is the nearest and largest thing in the frame, its fracture ragged and lit by the key with chips beside it, so the half reads as approximate. Only this beat could have it, because the single colour progression explains what bandgap research works on, and the broken half carries "roughly" without a word.

**Pick: 4.** One progression in place of five arbitrary colours, no ghost prints (each wafer's own light spills softly onto the floor and its contact is baked into the sweep), polished faces with a sheen, visible edge thickness and a bright bevel line where the rim faces the key. The camera is low and close over the near end, unlike the deck's eye level boat and unlike frame 4's split. Per the deck rule, the type sits in the right column, right aligned, with the row rising up the left side to keep that column quiet. No material is named.
