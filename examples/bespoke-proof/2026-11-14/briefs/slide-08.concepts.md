# Slide 08 concepts, "The newest Nvidia chips, the dean says"

1. **A sea of chips.** A camera a few centimetres off the floor looks across moulded trays of unmarked accelerator packages laid edge to edge at the deck's 16 mm pitch, the rows running from huge in the foreground to a glittering shimmer at the far wall, a backlit key throwing a glint path across thousands of mirror dies. Only the chips beat can have it, because it is the one sentence about the silicon itself and its quantity, and density is what the reader must feel.

2. **The hall from above.** An orthographic plan straight down on a data hall, rows of racks laid out like a city grid under coloured light, the cluster read as one system. It answers the dek's definition of a cluster, but it is a picture of servers rather than of the newest chips, and it would fit a capacity story just as well.

3. **Worm's eye up one rack.** The camera on the raised floor looking up a 42U rack of compute sleds that towers into a blue ceiling, status lights climbing out of frame. Strong on scale, but it says "data centre" in general and loses the chip the dean actually named.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one that could not carry another slide's headline: the subject is the chips themselves, and their sheer count is the feeling the beat asks for. It is a low macro of a manufactured field, unlike frame 7's crystal growth and frame 9's plate, and unlike the wafer boat on a bench that ran before.

**Composition.** The tray field fills the upper half to the far wall, its near edge set by code just above the type. The type sits bottom left over the deep indigo floor in front of the trays, headline in two lines, the dek under it, the whole block ending above the footer band.

## Rework (critic round, 5.5)

**New pick, concept 1 turned toward "newest".** A macro lens at tray height. One unmarked package hangs from a vacuum nozzle just above its empty pocket, crisp under a warm key, while its siblings in the trays go soft into violet haze behind it through a depth of field computed in the slide. The one chip being placed is the turn this sentence needs, and the soft field behind it still carries the count from the figure's pitch (the hero plus the field together equal the computed total). The type moves to the right column, right aligned, over the haze beside the hero.
