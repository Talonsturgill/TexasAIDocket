# Slide 01 concepts, the cover

1. **The staked lot.** A camera four centimetres off bare South Plains dirt looks up at the November boat stood on its end in an empty staked lot, so its mirror wafers stack like the floor slabs of a tower and its quartz rods run on past the last wafer and out of the top of the frame like rebar on a building nobody has finished, with survey stakes, pink string and a grain elevator small on the flat horizon around it. Only this beat has it because the sentence is the contrast itself: the money stands monumental where the cluster it pays for is still string on dirt, and the open rods carry "over" without a total.

2. **The empty rack.** An orthographic front view of one open server rack frame in a studio sweep, every rail bare, with the wafers standing in a slotted tray on its floor where the machines should hang. It says "not built" but reads as an equipment story and could run under any data centre headline.

3. **The site plan.** A plan view material relief of an architect's site plan on drafting film, the cluster's footprint a dashed outline on a blank parcel, with the wafers fanned across the empty footprint. It shows the plan but loses the size of the money, and a plan view is the medium the map frame after it is likely to use.

**Pick: 1.** It is the only one where swapping the headline breaks the picture (a huge sum standing where a building is not), it trades the old bench and top band for an outdoor world, a worm's eye camera and type in a column, and the open rods carry "over" without implying a total.

**Type:** a column high on the left over the deep part of the sky, headline in three or four lines, dek under it, while the tower rises up the right side and out of the top of the frame, and the stakes and string run off behind the column to a low horizon. A row lying down could not share the frame with a column of type, which is why the boat stands on end. The sun sits up and to the right behind the wafers, so the left sky stays the deep cerulean the cream type needs.
