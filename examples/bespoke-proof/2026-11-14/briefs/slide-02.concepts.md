# Slide 02 concepts, "Lubbock, by way of the governor's office"

## 1. The county table (chosen)

Texas laid out as a tabletop of 254 glazed county tiles cut from the committed county geometry, seen from a low aerial south of Austin, with a miniature Capitol standing on Travis County and one lit arc leaping from its dome across the Hill Country to Lubbock County, the only tile raised and glazed in the award's cyan. Only this beat has the capital as the origin and Lubbock as the landing, so the sentence "Lubbock, by way of the governor's office" is literally the path the light takes.

## 2. The routing envelope

A buff interoffice routing envelope lying on a desk under a window, string tie undone, its printed routing grid filled in from the Office of the Governor to the Texas CHIPS Office and on to Lubbock, shot as a 40 cm macro. It names the channel the money runs through, but it is a kit object staged on a surface, the very family the last four decks wore out, and printed routing lines risk reading as a real record.

## 3. The caprock road

A two lane highway at morning climbing the Caprock Escarpment from the red breaks onto the flat South Plains, the camera low on the shoulder looking northwest, a long straight line running to the horizon where Lubbock sits. It gives the feeling of distance, but nothing in it says "governor's office", and a road to a horizon could carry any rural story.

## Pick

Concept 1, because it is the only one where both halves of the headline are in the picture at once (the Capitol and Lubbock County), the place is placed by real geometry rather than a sign, and swapping in any other slide's headline breaks it.

Neighbours: the cover sells the size of a money promise and frame 3 shows a clean room, an interior. A cartographic tabletop at an aerial camera shares neither world nor camera with them. It also departs from what ran (wafer boat on a bench, type top left): the type sits low over the darkened South Texas tiles, and the arc owns the sky.
