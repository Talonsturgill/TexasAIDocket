# Frame 7 concepts, "AI to steer how the crystals grow"

**A. The cut crystal, at atom scale.** A macro of a crystal block built atom by atom, its top a staircase of one atom terraces with islands of a new layer spreading across them, and arriving atoms riding bent cyan paths down out of the blue to marked landing sites at the island and step edges. Only this beat has atoms being placed on a growing layer by a guide, which is what "steer how the crystals grow" says, and the block's cut front face shows the stacked layers the growth adds to.

**B. The reactor in cutaway.** An exploded cutaway of a growth chamber with a heated wafer on its platen and gas streams flowing over it, sliced open like a technical drawing. It explains where epitaxy happens, but the subject is a machine, so the picture would serve any frame about lab equipment and does not show a crystal growing.

**C. The terraced map.** The growing surface as a contour relief seen from straight above, like a topographic map of terraces, with a projected path plan in light marking where the next layer should go. It carries "steered" as a plan, but an orthographic relief of terraces is what the look v2 proof already ran for this beat, and a plan view loses the layers in depth.

**Pick: A.** It is the only one where a stranger sees the two halves of the sentence at once, crystals growing layer on layer and something guiding where each atom lands, and it is a 3D macro rather than the relief or the boat and bench this beat ran as before.

**Composition.** The block fills the frame edge to edge and bleeds off both sides. The terraces, islands and landing paths own the upper 55 percent, with the arrivals coming out of the blue wall at the top. The key comes from behind and to the left, so the block's cut front face falls into deep violet shade across the lower half, and the headline and dek sit there, bottom left, in two lines and a five line dek, on the layers the growth is building. The accent cyan marks only the steering paths and landing sites, which are the work of the November award's AI cluster.

## Rework (critic round, 6.6, unlike_neighbours 4)

Frame 8 also uses a high oblique camera over an orange on blue lattice with the type at the bottom left, so concept A was rebuilt as **A2, the step edge at eye level**. The camera now sits a few atoms above the lower terrace and focuses on one advancing step edge with a shallow depth of field, so the terrace goes soft in front and behind. One amber beam from a turned metal emitter tip at the upper right sets a single arriving atom down on the front of the coral layer that is growing out from the step. The eye goes beam, then atom, then growing layer. The headline sits high in the open blue above the crystal, and the dek sits low on the soft foreground. The beam and its glows no longer use the November accent. There are no target rings and no other paths.
