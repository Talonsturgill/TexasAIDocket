# Slide 04 concepts, the match

**A. The split platter (macro, overhead, studio).** A round wafer carrier seen from 45 degrees above, the kind a batch tool spins, split down its diameter into a pale frosted quartz half and a dark silicon carbide half, each holding twelve mirror wafers in pockets around one ring, the two halves butted at a seam with one hub joining them. Only the match has this picture, because it is literally two equal halves that only make a whole circle together, and the circle is the $24 million project.

**B. The level beam (worm's eye, studio sweep).** A long quartz beam balanced on a single fulcrum seen from floor level, twelve wafers standing on its left arm and twelve on its right, dead level. It says "equal" well, but a balance says two things weighed against each other, not two things joined into one, and a balance ran on Oct 30.

**C. Two stacks becoming one (side elevation, cutaway).** Twelve wafers stacked flat in a quartz cylinder and twelve in a carbide cylinder, slid together end to end into one tube of twenty four, drawn as a lengthwise cutaway. Reads as one whole, but it is a boat in a tube by another name, close to what both earlier decks ran.

**Pick: A.** It is the only one where the whole exists only because the halves meet: take either half away and the circle, the project, is gone, which is the sentence. It is also a different camera (overhead macro), scale and object from the boats on a bench the shipped and v2 decks drew, and from a clean room interior or a bandgap close up on the neighbours.

**Composition.** The platter sits whole in the upper two thirds, lit in a warm pool by a key from high front left, the floor falling off from apricot into deep indigo toward the bottom. The headline sits centred low in that indigo, two lines, with the dek under it, so the circle stands above the words like a seal over a caption. Labels "state" and "Texas Tech" sit on their own halves inside the ring.
