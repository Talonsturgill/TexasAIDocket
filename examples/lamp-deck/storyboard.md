# Storyboard — carousel No. 26R, "It was built to not tell you"

The reference build for the deck chassis system. Same story, same claims and same copy as the
deck shipped on 2026-09-16, rebuilt so the two contact sheets are an A/B on artwork alone.

CONTINUITY: CAMERA_MOVE, MOTIF_EVOLUTION

**CAMERA_MOVE.** One world, a desk in Arlington after dark, and the camera moves through it:
inside the page at page height (1), back to the desk (2), out to the room (3), down to the page
again (4), across the desk surface (5), to the second room with the lamp moved (6), back to a
document (7), to three of them side by side (8), and finally to frame 1's exact camera (9). The
close is the open, which is what lets the last frame argue by absence.

**MOTIF_EVOLUTION.** The hint tick, the machine's entire contribution to a page. One mark in the
margin on frame 1, one beside the government's words on 2, none in the room on 3, two on 4
because what she saw was the same thing twice, none on the carved frame, one in the second room,
two on 7 for the two funders, three on 8 for the three readings, and NONE on 9, because the award
has not started and nothing has been graded. The argument is drawn rather than captioned.

VALUE CUT: none. The deck holds one ground the whole way, measured at a mean adjacent jump of
5.39 L* against a ceiling of 14.9, with no adjacent pair over 25.

THE LIGHT. az 64, el 26, declared once in `assets/js/deck/2026-09-16-lamp.js`. The lamp is up
and to the right and every cast runs down and to the left. Frames 2 and 6 swing the CAMERA
rather than the lamp, which is why their pools land from the other side while every object's
shadow still runs the same way.
