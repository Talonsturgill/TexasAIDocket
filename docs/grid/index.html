Separate feeds, and everything generated inside the grid is consumed inside it, so they must agree. They differ by 0.2%, about what ties and losses account for. Both print because this catches either reader breaking.

The shape is the story

A data center draws about as much at four in the morning as at five in the afternoon. Constant load lifts the overnight floor faster than the afternoon ceiling. Weather lifts both. The floor is where new demand shows first.

The fingerprint

Not yet. Comparing the trough against the peak needs at least 14 settled days, so that a weekday and weekend pattern has repeated twice. The record holds 8. Nothing is drawn from fewer, because a line through two points is noise with a slope.

Forecast accuracy

Across 8 days its day ahead peak forecast missed by 1,012 MW on average, or 1.2% of peak. Worst so far 2,503 MW.
