This room can move a generator.

Published September 15th 9 slides

The deck

The story

This room can move a generator.

The grid operator told its own board that a data center's AI training can swing its draw hard enough to reach the machines.

Follow the wire both ways.

At one end a building that can swing what it draws. At the other a machine that has to take it.

This is one large load.

The grid operator's own term for it. The record names no company and no county for any of them.

The word in the paper is fatigue.

The grid operator says a load's swing can reach here. It does not say that it has.

Three risks. Three states of repair.

One rule in force. One at the board this month. One not written, and it carries no number.

Sensitive compute equipment trips offline during normal grid events, leading to grid imbalance and instability.

NOGRR 282 / NPRR 1308. Ride-through requirements for Large Computational Loads.

The PUCT approved NOGRR 282/ NPRR 1308 in July, effective on August 1.
Certain data centers processes can oscillate in a way that damages grid equipment.

Draft NOGRR. Large Load processes must not oscillate in a manner that would cause damage.

ERCOT plans to submit NOGRR in September or October.
Dynamic models for Large Loads have proven to be inaccurate and thus are inadequate for studying their grid impacts.

PGRR 144, model quality rules. Texas A&M is developing more accurate models.

TAC endorsed PGRR 144 in August and is up for Board consideration in September.

One model is finished. The other is not.

The grid operator says its models of these loads have proven inaccurate, and that Texas A&M is building better ones. The crypto model is released. The data center model is still being written.

The limit is drawn. The load is not.

The grid operator's draft rule would hold a large load's swing to this. It has not been filed and it carries no number.

Every county in Texas. Not one of them named.

The presentation names no data center, no company and no generator. Texas A&M is the only institution in it besides the grid operator and the commission.

The rule to watch has a page.

The model rule reached the board this month. The swing rule has not been filed, and the grid operator gives September or October. Both are tracked at texasaidocket.com.
