TRAIGA preempts city and county regulation of artificial intelligence. What is this decision?

Chapter 552 of the Business and Commerce Code supersedes and preempts any local ordinance, resolution, rule or regulation about the use of AI systems. Texas cities and counties that might otherwise write their own AI rules are displaced by the state statute. The preemption is written broadly and without a carve out on its face.

Utilities Code limits how the PUCT may direct ERCOT, and bars verbal directives. What is this decision?

Texas law restricts the channels through which the Public Utility Commission may order ERCOT to act. A verbal directive is prohibited outright. The ERCOT board selection committee picks ERCOT's directors and designates its chair. The governor does not.
