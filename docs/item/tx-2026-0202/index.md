# ERCOT pauses energizing new data centers and crypto mines of 75 MW or more until its audit is done

ERCOT told its board that it has paused the energization of new large load data centers and crypto mining facilities. The pause covers sites of 75 MW or more that are not yet connected to the ERCOT system. It lasts until ERCOT completes the verification and audit and the community impact review it began after the Governor's August 3rd, 2026 directive. ERCOT says it will file both reports with the Public Utility Commission of Texas by December 10th, 2026. The update went to the ERCOT board meeting of September 14th and 15th, 2026.
