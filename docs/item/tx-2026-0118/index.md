# Federal commodities regulator opens comment on trading derivatives whose underlying commodity is compute

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission published a request for comment on August 21st, 2026 asking what a designated contract market should weigh before listing derivatives with compute as the underlier. The document ties the supply side of a compute price to the cost of deploying and operating data centers, and to the cost of chips and energy. Comments are open until October 20th, 2026. The notice names no state, and Texas carries a large share of the data centers whose cost such a market would price.
