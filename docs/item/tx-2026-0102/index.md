# UT Southwestern says AI now does most of the grading on its medical students' clinical exam notes

UT Southwestern Medical Center built a process that reads and grades the post encounter notes students write after their objective structured clinical examinations. The medical school says it has replaced more than 91 percent of human grading since the autumn of 2023 and returns results in days rather than weeks. The stated reason is that grading those notes by hand is slow and inconsistent. The page names no model and no vendor, and it carries no publication date, so how current the figure is can't be established from it.
