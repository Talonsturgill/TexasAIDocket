# UTMB puts OpenEvidence's AI answers inside its health record and says more than half of its clinicians use it

The University of Texas Medical Branch announced on September 22nd that OpenEvidence, an AI platform that answers clinical questions with citations to medical literature, sits inside its electronic health record. UTMB says the integration went live in March and that clinicians sign in with their usual UTMB credentials. It says more than half of its clinicians now actively use the tool for clinical decision support. UTMB has hospitals on four campuses and more than 100 clinics across Southeast Texas. The release reports use and query growth but publishes no measure of whether the answers are accurate or whether patient care changed. UTMB says it will keep evaluating use and clinician experience.
