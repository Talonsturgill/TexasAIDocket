# Texas Tech and a Fort Worth contractor give the Army an AI maintenance tool for its aircraft fleet and an engine life tracker

Texas Tech's Institute for Materials, Manufacturing and Sustainment and Fort Worth based AVX Defense Technologies built two tools under the Army's FleetSpace program. One is an AI enabled decision tool for aviation maintenance planning. The other tracks the remaining useful life of aircraft engines. Texas Tech said on November 20th that both have been provided to the Army. The test results it reports come from testing, not from service in the fleet.

### Texas Tech's Institute for Materials, Manufacturing and Sustainment worked with Fort Worth based AVX Defense Technologies under the Army's FleetSpace program to build the FleetSpace User Interface, an operations and maintenance platform.

> Texas Tech’s Institute for Materials, Manufacturing & Sustainment (IMMS) partnered with Fort Worth-based AVX Defense Technologies, under the U.S. Army’s FleetSpace program funded by the Army Aviation and Missile Command (AvMC), to develop the FleetSpace User Interface (FSUI), an integrated operations and maintenance platform.
