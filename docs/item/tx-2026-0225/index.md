# Lockheed Martin brings Hadrian's 24 machine automated machining cell into its Grand Prairie missile parts plant

Lockheed Martin announced on October 14th, 2026 that it is investing $300 million in its Grand Prairie operation and expanding it to more than 735,000 square feet. Hadrian will bring a highly automated machining cell of 24 machines into the plant. Lockheed's own production workforce will run it, and it is expected to be operational in early 2027. Hadrian describes its factories as combining process engineering, AI and robotics. Its software Opus interprets legacy designs and automates manufacturing and inspection. The release does not say what the AI does inside this cell. The plant supports the PAC-3 MSE, THAAD, PrSM and JATM programs. Nearly 800 production employees made 250,000 parts there in 2025. Lockheed says it plans 2,000 jobs in Texas. That figure is the company's own.
