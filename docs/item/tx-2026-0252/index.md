# UT Arlington leads a USDA funded project to build an AI smart trap that tells wild screwworm flies from sterile ones

UT Arlington is coordinating a $3.1 million project funded by the U.S. Department of Agriculture against New World screwworm, a parasite that has reemerged with more than 120 confirmed animal cases, most of them in Texas. The center of it is a smart trap that uses AI to tell wild flies from the sterile ones a federal program releases, and females from males. It would keep the wild females and let the rest go. Today's traps catch everything and need sorting by hand. The trap does not exist yet as a field system. Its lead researcher expects a prototype next spring, and the project runs through June 2028 with fieldwork in Texas and other states.
