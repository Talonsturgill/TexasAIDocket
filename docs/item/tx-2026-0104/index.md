# National Science Foundation funds a five year robotics center at UT Austin built around robots adapting to the people they work beside

The National Science Foundation made award 2535195 to the University of Texas at Austin on August 25th, 2026. It funds a Science and Technology Center on human and robot co adaptation. The award record carries an estimated total for the full five years in the agency's own award service. It records the fiscal year 2026 obligation as $5,999,999.00. The project runs from September 1st, 2026 to August 31st, 2031. Joydeep Biswas is the principal investigator. The stated problem is that robots leaving the laboratory for homes and hospitals and workplaces have to understand and adapt to people over time.
