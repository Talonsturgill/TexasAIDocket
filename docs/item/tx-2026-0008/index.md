# Texas Government Code Chapter 2054 carries three conflicting Subchapter S's on AI, with duplicated section numbers

Three separate acts of the 89th Legislature each added a Subchapter S to Government Code Chapter 2054, and two of them number their sections identically. Section 2054.702 exists twice with entirely different commands, as do Sections 2054.701 and 2054.703 through 2054.705. The codifier has flagged the conflict in the published code rather than resolved it. Which text governs a state agency's AI duties is unsettled on the face of the statute.

## Evidence

### The published code carries an editorial note recording three separate Subchapter S enactments.

> Subchapter S, consisting of Secs. 2054.701 to 2054.713, was added by Acts 2025, 89th Leg., R.S., Ch. 1148 (S.B.  1964 ), Sec. 5. For another Subchapter S, consisting of Secs. 2054.651 to 2054.654, added by Acts 2025, 89th Leg., R.S., Ch. 951 (H.B.  5195 ), Sec. 1, see Sec. 2054.651 et seq., post. For another Subchapter S, consisting of Secs. 2054.701 to 2054.705, added by Acts 2025, 89th Leg., R.S., Ch. 1079 (H.B.  2818 ), Sec. 1, see Sec. 2054.701 et seq., post.

Source (primary_official): https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2054.htm

### One version of Section 2054.702 concerns generative AI technology.

> Sec. 2054.702. GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGY.

Source (primary_official): https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2054.htm

### The other version of Section 2054.702 requires a statewide AI code of ethics.

> Sec. 2054.702. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM CODE OF ETHICS.  (a) The department by rule shall establish an artificial intelligence system code of ethics for use by state agencies and local governments that procure, develop, deploy, or use artificial intelligence systems.

Source (primary_official): https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2054.htm

### That code of ethics must align with a named federal framework.

> the artificial intelligence system code of ethics must include guidance for the deployment and use of artificial intelligence systems and heightened scrutiny artificial intelligence systems that aligns with the Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

Source (primary_official): https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2054.htm

### Local governments as well as state agencies must adopt the code of ethics.

> (c) State agencies and local governments shall adopt the code of ethics developed under this section.

Source (primary_official): https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2054.htm
