# The Hays County Clerk asks the commissioners court to buy AI that writes the county's official minutes

The Hays County Commissioners Court agenda for September 15th, 2026 carried an item to authorize the County Clerk's Office to obtain AI Minutes through a vendor. The first year's amount is stated on the agenda. The same item asks the court to waive the county purchasing policy that requires three quotes, so the request is to buy this product without comparing it against others. The vendor's own site describes the product as using AI to write official meeting minutes from a recording, a live session or a pasted transcript. What the agenda does not say is whether a person reviews or certifies the machine's draft before it becomes the county's official record. That is the question the item leaves open. The county's own Court Minutes archive for 2026 carries nothing later than July 28th, so the disposition is not on the public record today.
