# NSF awarded Prairie View A and M a grant to let AI split up the grid optimization problem across processor cores

The National Science Foundation made an award to Prairie View A and M University on August 11th, 2026 under its HBCU Excellence in Research program. The project builds a framework that uses graph based artificial intelligence and reinforcement learning to decide how to break up electric grid optimization problems. It then assigns the pieces to processor cores as the work runs. The award record states the obligation as FY 2026 = $699,999.00 and runs the project from October 1st, 2026 through September 30th, 2029. The math it targets is the math that decides which generators run in a grid the size of the one carrying the state's new load.
