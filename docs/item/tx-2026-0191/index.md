# PUCT wildfire plan blueprint lists cameras suggested, not required

The wildfire mitigation plan rule, 16 TAC §25.60, reaches every electric utility, municipally owned utility and electric cooperative that owns transmission or distribution lines in Texas. In Project 59053 commission staff filed a pro forma plan and guidelines that a utility may use and is not required to use. Its situational awareness section names cameras or other monitoring technology among the tools a plan describes. That section is marked suggested rather than required. Pano AI sells AI smoke detection cameras and names Xcel Energy, CenterPoint Energy, American Electric Power and Austin Energy as its Texas customers. It had asked staff to make a description of situational awareness tools a minimum requirement. A House State Affairs hearing reported on September 21st, 2026 heard that only eight of 160 utilities in fire-prone areas had complied with the plan law. The same hearing heard that Southwestern Public Service, which operates as Xcel Energy in the Panhandle, has deployed AI smoke detection cameras across its service territory.
