TRAIGA preempts city and county regulation of artificial intelligence

state-policyTexas LegislatureStatewideClosed

Chapter 552 of the Business and Commerce Code supersedes and preempts any local ordinance, resolution, rule or regulation about the use of AI systems. Texas cities and counties that might otherwise write their own AI rules are displaced by the state statute. The preemption is written broadly and without a carve out on its face.

How to take part

The preemption is enacted law and takes no comment. Changing it takes the Legislature, which next convenes in regular session in 2027, or a court. A resident whose city wants to act on AI can raise it with their legislator.
