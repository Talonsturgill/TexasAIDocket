# DARPA's Triage Challenge finals end at TEEX's Disaster City in College Station, where drones and ground robots triaged staged mass casualties

The Texas A&M Engineering Extension Service staged the finals of DARPA's Triage Challenge at Disaster City in College Station from November 5th to November 13th. Autonomous drones and ground robots worked alongside human medics to find simulated casualties, read vital signs from a distance and relay critical data to the medics. The scenes included a tornado hitting a market, smoke and near darkness. RoboScout won the systems competition and $1.5 million. Disruptors won the data competition and $1 million. The casualties were role players with mock injuries made by moulage artists.
