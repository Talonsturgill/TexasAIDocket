# West Texas A&M wins a USDA grant to build a research feedlot near Canyon that will test feeding, health and handling with AI powered methods

The U.S. Department of Agriculture's National Institute of Food and Agriculture awarded West Texas A&M University a grant to build a beef cattle research facility. The university calls it the largest federal award in its history. A dollar for dollar match from the A&M System and other sources completes the project. The plans call for a precision cattle handling facility, a veterinary hospital, a feed and production center and a metabolism center. The release says feeding, health and handling techniques there can be evaluated with traditional and AI powered approaches. It names no AI tool and reports no result yet.
