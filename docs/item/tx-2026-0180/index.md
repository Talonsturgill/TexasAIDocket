# Leander stops paying for its plate reader contract and says the police department is no longer using the cameras or the data

The Leander City Council directed the city to discontinue funding its contract with the automated license plate recognition vendor Flock at its September 3rd, 2026 meeting. The city posted a notice saying that as of September 4th, 2026 the police department is no longer using Flock cameras or Flock data. The city's own account gives the reason as community concerns and says the system had been started to enhance public safety. What the notice does not say is what happens to the images already collected or whether the hardware comes down.

### The city gives its reason.

> The City originally initiated an Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) system in an effort to enhance public safety for our citizens and business community. In response to community concerns, the City Council decided to discontinue Flock services. While we will no longer have Flock as a resource, our vigilance and commitment to public safety remains unwavering.
