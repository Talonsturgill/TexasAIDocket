# Pflugerville's City Council adopts SAFE standards for how the city uses technology, with facial recognition covered

The Pflugerville City Council approved Resolution RES-1443 on October 27th, 2026. It adopts standards the city calls SAFE, for secure, accountable, fair and explainable technology use. It also directs staff to carry them out. The staff overview added a section that creates a Safe Technology Committee. Staff confirmed the policy covers facial recognition when a council member asked about it. Every member voted in favor. Voters in the same city decide a charter section on its use of AI on November 3rd, 2026.
