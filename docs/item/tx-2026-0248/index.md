# Texas 2036 finds AI in most Texas classrooms and a written AI policy in few districts, and asks the state for guidance

Texas 2036 is a nonprofit policy group. On November 10th, 2026 it published a preview of its report In Ctrl of AI in Schools. The preview reports a survey of Texas school and district leaders taken in fall 2025. In it 86% said teachers were using AI and 61% said students were. Only 29% reported a district policy governing student AI use. Just 20% reported a policy for teachers and staff. The group's own analysis counts 42 states with some form of statewide AI guidance for schools and says Texas is not among them. Its first ask is statewide guidance for every district. It also wants safety standards for AI tools before they reach classrooms, training for teachers and AI literacy for students. The report is a recommendation. No state agency or district has acted on it.
