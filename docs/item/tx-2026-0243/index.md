# Texas A&M builds a language model that sorts disaster posts for emergency managers, and it has not yet been used in a live disaster

Texas A&M University reported on November 6th, 2026 that researchers in its Zachry Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering are developing an AI tool. It is meant to help emergency managers sort the information that floods in during a hurricane or another crisis. The tool is a large language model. It sorts crisis posts into disaster, weather and humanitarian information, then labels each post by event type, informativeness and the kind of humanitarian information it carries. The team tested it on a benchmark of human annotated social media posts from several disaster events. The university says the system is still under development and has not been deployed during an active disaster. The team next plans an AI agent emergency professionals can talk to. The release gives no accuracy figure and names no emergency agency testing the tool. The National Science Foundation is listed as the funder.
