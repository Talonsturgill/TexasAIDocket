# San Antonio council requires its own approval for every new data center

San Antonio City Council passed a new process for considering data centers on October 29th, 2026. Mayor Gina Ortiz Jones said it ensures the council can review each data center application. Texas Public Radio reports the vote was 9 to 2 and that approved data centers may go only in commercial or industrial zones. The mayor said her own provisions on transparency, public health and property values were left out of the resolution and will serve as her office's standards for each application.
