# ERCOT will publish a data center's water and noise answers only if its developer opts in by December 4th

ERCOT asked data center developers in a September 14th, 2026 survey about their state and community impact. On November 20th it told them it will not publish any project's answers without the developer's written authorization. Developers who agree must send ERCOT a form by December 4th, 2026. The answers that could be released include projected annual water withdrawal and consumption at full buildout and expected noise levels. ERCOT intends to report how many developers authorized release in its December 10th, 2026 report to the PUCT, then file and post the authorized answers. Every other answer stays confidential.
