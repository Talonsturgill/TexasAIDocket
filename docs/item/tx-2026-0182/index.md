# Governor directs the environmental agency to issue no data center permits until the grid audit is done

The Office of the Governor issued a directive to the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality on September 21st, 2026. It tells the agency to halt all permits sought by data centers until the grid operator completes the interconnection audit the Governor ordered on August 3rd, 2026. The directive goes wider than one agency. It states that no state agency is to move forward with regulatory approvals related to data center development until that information is in hand, because the utility commission, the grid operator and the water board need it to decide anything. The release sets out what a data center project must do, which is cover all electrical infrastructure costs, lower residential bills, finish the grid audit, stay off water local communities need, report its electricity and water use, and meet setback requirements. The agency is told to align its permitting decisions with the directives and to use what the audits find. It must report back on its compliance by October 19th, 2026. The release closes by saying the Governor will work with the Legislature next session to eliminate financial incentives for data centers.
