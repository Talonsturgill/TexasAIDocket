# Hays County takes up a policy on how it governs AI in surveillance

The Hays County Commissioners Court agenda for September 8th, 2026 carried an item on how the county governs its own use of artificial intelligence in surveillance. The item names equipment and software alongside the collection and analysis of data. It contemplated adopting a policy statement that would set guidelines and limits on the AI enabled surveillance technologies the county uses. The reach is general rather than product specific. That makes it the standing rule beside the narrower promise the same court posted a week earlier. The item immediately after it on the same agenda concerns two contract amendments with a different vendor. One of those is for report writing services. Nothing on either page connects the two. The county's own Court Minutes archive for 2026 carries nothing later than July 28th. The disposition is not on the public record today.
