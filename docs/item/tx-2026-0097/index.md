# Texas requires AI training for state and local government employees, and this is the first annual cycle

House Bill 3512 of the 89th Regular Session created artificial intelligence training programs for certain employees and officials of state agencies and local governments. Its author was Giovanni Capriglione and its sponsor was Cesar Blanco. It took effect on September 1st, 2025. The Legislature indexes it under artificial intelligence and under the Department of Information Resources, which is the agency that certifies which programs satisfy it. Which programs carry that certification is unconfirmed. So is the date by which an entity files its compliance certification.
