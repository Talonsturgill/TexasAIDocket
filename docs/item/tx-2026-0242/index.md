# ExxonMobil says three of its Permian rigs now drill with robotic pipe handling, and about half its Permian fleet will by 2028

ExxonMobil chief executive Darren Woods spoke on the company's third quarter earnings call on October 30th, 2026. He said the company has three automated drilling rigs in operation in the Permian and is the only operator there using them. He said the company expects automated rigs on about 25% of its Permian fleet next year and approximately half by 2028. The company says robotic arms and systems can now do much of the heavy pipe handling. That work once put workers beside moving machinery on the rig floor. It expects similar staffing levels on automated rigs. Reuters reported in August that the company runs more than 30 drilling rigs in the Permian. It placed one automated rig in Midland, where a gate around the drilling floor marks it a restricted area. Helmerich & Payne said on October 7th, 2026 that it expects to deploy seven additional FlexRobotics systems for ExxonMobil over the next 12 months, bringing the total operating to nine. The company calls the safety benefit a potential and gives no measured injury figure. None of the sources describes these rigs as artificial intelligence. They describe automation and robotics.
