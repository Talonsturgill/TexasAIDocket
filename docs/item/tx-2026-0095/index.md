# UT System Regents put the first billion dollars of the UT Dell Medical Center into the capital program

The Board of Regents approved adding the first phase of the UT Dell Medical Center in Austin to the capital improvement program. It acted at its August 12th, 2026 meeting. The agenda book the board took up asks it to amend the program at a total project cost of one billion dollars for that phase. It also asks the board to appropriate that money from revenue financing system bond proceeds. The phase itself is early work and site preparation with a building cost of zero. The board item describes a 27 acre academic medical campus built around a digitally enabled infrastructure, with digital technology, robotics and automation folded into operations. It names MD Anderson as the partner for cancer care. The board's own document never uses the words artificial intelligence. That framing comes from the university's own April release, which describes a center where technology, data and AI are embedded to support clinicians. The debt is repaid from pledged revenues rather than from an appropriation, so the bet is on clinical income.
