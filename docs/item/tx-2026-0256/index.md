# TACC opens Horizon, a 4,000 GPU academic supercomputer in Round Rock, to its first researchers

The Texas Advanced Computing Center at UT Austin opened Horizon to early users. It is the centerpiece of the National Science Foundation's Leadership-Class Computing Facility and runs in a new liquid cooled data center in Round Rock. Its first phase is 4,000 GPUs, and it ranks No. 24 on the November 2026 TOP500 list at 132 petaflops. CPU servers are to be added in early 2027. Researchers reach it through allocations rather than by buying time on a private cloud.
