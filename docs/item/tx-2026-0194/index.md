# A machine scores the longer written answers on English STAAR first

The Texas Education Agency scores the constructed responses on English language STAAR tests with an automated scoring engine first. At least 25 percent of those answers then go to people. Where a person scores an answer, that score is the one that counts. A March 2024 agency presentation puts the engine's score as the score of record on about 75 percent of responses. Constructed responses on Spanish language tests are scored by people alone. The agency began the hybrid model in December 2023, and its presentation says full human scoring would have cost $15 million to $20 million more per year. On September 4th, 2026 the agency said about 27,200 students received additional credit after content experts rescored short typed answers. It said none of the regraded responses had been graded by the engine. A district can ask for a constructed response to be rescored by a person for a fee, and the fee is waived if the score changes.

## Evidence

Every fact above rests on one of these. The words are the source's own.

### On STAAR tests not given in Spanish, the agency's scoring engine scores constructed responses first, and at least a quarter go on to people.

> This means that the responses are initially scored by an automated scoring engine (ASE) and at least 25 percent of student responses are then routed to human scorers.

Source (primary_official): https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/scoring-process-staar-constructed-response-1.pdf

### Constructed responses on the Spanish language tests are scored by people and never by the engine.

> Student responses to SCR and ECR questions for STAAR Spanish assessments are 100 percent human scored and do not get scored by the ASE.

Source (primary_official): https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/scoring-process-staar-constructed-response-1.pdf

### Where a person scores an answer, the person's score is the one that counts.

> Any student responses that are routed for human scoring maintain the score assigned by the human scorer as the score of record.

Source (primary_official): https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/scoring-process-staar-constructed-response-1.pdf

### The engine learns from answers people have already scored in field tests.

> The ASE is trained on student responses and human scores from the field-test data.

Source (primary_official): https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/scoring-process-staar-constructed-response-1.pdf

### A rescore is done by a person.

> Rescore requests are sent for human scoring.

Source (primary_official): https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/scoring-process-staar-constructed-response-1.pdf

### The rescore fee is waived when the score changes.

> If the score on the constructed-response question changes, the fee is waived.

Source (primary_official): https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/scoring-process-staar-constructed-response-1.pdf

### The agency's presentation puts the engine's score as the score of record on about three in four responses.
