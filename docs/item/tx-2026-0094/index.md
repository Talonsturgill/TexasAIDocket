# A generative AI platform at UTMB reached the UT System Regents only on its third amendment, at $9,850,000, and now covers all seven health institutions

The University of Texas Medical Branch at Galveston contracted in December 2024 for a platform giving it a secure way to work with generative artificial intelligence models. The agenda book for the August 12th and 13th, 2026 meeting of the University of Texas System Board of Regents carries the third amendment. It is item 65 on the consent agenda. It states the first agreement was set at $825,000 and did not require Board approval. A first amendment raised the value to $4,250,000 and still sat inside the same delegated authority. The third amendment adds $5,600,000 and brings the total to $9,850,000. It extends the platform to all seven University of Texas System health related institutions and is funded under an initiative appropriated by the 89th Texas Legislature. The agenda book also states the agreement was competitively procured. No minutes for the meeting are published, so what the Board did with the item is not yet on the public record.
