DIR must run an AI regulatory sandbox letting state agencies test systems outside normal rules

state-policyTexas Department of Information ResourcesStatewideNo formal process

The Department of Information Resources is required to establish a programme letting eligible public entities contract with registered vendors to test AI systems before deployment, expressly without full compliance with otherwise applicable regulations. Vendors apply, and participants file quarterly reports. The department must report to the Legislature by November 30th of each even numbered year, which makes November 30th, 2026 the first reporting date.

How to take part

The application process runs to vendors registered with DIR, not to the general public, and the statute provides no public comment step. The public visibility point is the report the department owes the Legislature by November 30th, 2026. A reader can ask DIR for the vendor application process and for any quarterly reports already filed.
