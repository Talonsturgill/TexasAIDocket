# Texas A&M takes a $2 million WoodNext Foundation grant to build the Aggie AI Foundry, where AI and robots would design and test new metal alloys

Texas A&M's Department of Materials Science and Engineering announced on October 22nd, 2026 a $2 million grant from the WoodNext Foundation. The money will build the Aggie AI Foundry, a campus facility meant to develop new metal alloys from idea to testing with AI and robotic arms. The university says the process would cut alloy development from months to a week or less. That is a goal and has not been measured. Its own researcher says no computational tool anywhere can predict how an alloy will behave, so the materials still have to be made and tested. No opening date is given.
