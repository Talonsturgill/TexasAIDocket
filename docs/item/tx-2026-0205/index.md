# TxDOT starts Project Nexus, a three year state test of next generation aircraft whose first flights carry no passengers

The Texas Department of Transportation launched the first phase of Project Nexus on September 10th at Fort Worth Alliance Airport. It is the state's part of a federal pilot program for electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft. The first phase tests across the state with helicopters and fixed wing planes, and those flights carry no passengers. They collect data and validate routes. Later phases move medical supplies and organs between rural facilities and medical centers in Austin and San Antonio, and the last phase carries passengers across the Texas triangle. At the kickoff an invited company, Merlin, flew a Cessna Caravan to show software it says handles takeoff, landing and radio calls to air traffic control. That account of the demonstration is the company's own.
