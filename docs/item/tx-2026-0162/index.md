# Memorial Hermann clinicians send Houston patients an AI drafted explanation of their own test results

Memorial Hermann Health System says its clinicians are using an artificial intelligence assistant built into Epic to draft the note a patient receives with a lab or imaging result. The draft is written in plain language rather than clinical terms and the clinician reviews, edits and personalizes it before the patient sees it. The health system reports that patients sent 32% fewer follow up messages about test results when the clinician started from the draft. It reports the tool has helped care teams write over 55,000 messages and that clinicians starting from a draft saved an average of nine seconds per result comment. The feature was piloted in December of 2025 in the system's innovation clinic and use has since expanded across the system. A health system trade publication that read the same announcement noted every one of those figures comes from the software vendor and its customer. No baseline, control arm or denominator is published beside them, and the share describes only the clinicians who chose to work from a draft. The system's chief medical information officer says the technology is designed to support rather than replace the clinician's judgment.
