# Federal regulators open an audit of how Tesla certified the Cybercab, on the day commercial deployment began in Austin

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration opened Audit Query AQ26002 on September 3rd, 2026, the same day Tesla began commercial deployment of the Cybercab in Austin. The agency records that the vehicles lack permanently attached, conventional manual controls, naming a brake pedal, a gas pedal, a steering wheel and mirrors. Tesla certified the vehicle itself as meeting every applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard and told the agency it had done so. The audit does not ask how the vehicle drives. It examines the process and the technical data behind that certification. The agency says it will consider how far that certification rested on Tesla deciding that certain standards do not apply. The vehicle was built without the controls those standards were written for. The estimated population is a thousand vehicles and Tesla has told the agency it plans to add more vehicles and more locations.
