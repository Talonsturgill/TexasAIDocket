# Austin City Council takes up a resolution to write data centers into a land development code that has no definition of one

Item 61 on the Austin City Council's regular meeting agenda for August 27th, 2026 asks the council to approve a resolution. It would initiate amendments to Title 25 of the City Code, the Land Development Code. The amendments would establish land uses and site development regulations and development processes for data center use in certain zoning districts. The same item covers business regulations and permit requirements for data center operations. The posted item carries no megawatt or square footage threshold and the agenda records the item as having no fiscal impact.
