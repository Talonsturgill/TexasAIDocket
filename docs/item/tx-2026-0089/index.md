# The House committee on government efficiency is charged with studying whether the state should use artificial intelligence to find fraud in its own spending

The Speaker's interim charges give a charge to the House Committee on Delivery of Government Efficiency. It is to study how the state can use technology to detect fraud and waste and abuse of taxpayer resources. The charge names artificial intelligence among that technology. A second charge asks whether the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives Texas residents and businesses adequate protection. The same document puts the Department of Information Resources and the Sunset Advisory Commission and the Texas Space Commission under the committee's oversight. A charge is an instruction to look at something and report, and it is the stage before a bill exists.
