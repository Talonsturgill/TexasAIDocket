# Texas A&M scientists use AlphaFold to screen every pairing of human mitochondrial proteins and publish the likely partners free

Texas A&M AgriLife Research reported on November 5th, 2026 on work in the Texas A&M Department of Biochemistry and Biophysics. Its scientists used an AI tool to look for which human mitochondrial proteins work together. The tool is AlphaFold-Multimer, which Google DeepMind built to predict the shapes proteins form together. The team ran it on every possible pairing of about 1,100 mitochondrial proteins. Out of 630,003 possible pairs it catalogued 2,895 likely interactions, and the release says more than half had never been reported before. It also gave 85 previously uncharacterized proteins their first probable partners. About two years of lab work followed and confirmed several predicted partnerships in yeast and human cells. The predictions are posted free as MitoMatch. The paper appeared in Nature Communications. The release says cures for mitochondria linked diseases may still be far into the future.
