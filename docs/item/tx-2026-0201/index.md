# UT REAL Health AI funds 12 pilot projects that put AI into UT System hospitals and medical schools

The University of Texas Research, Engineering, and Application Laboratory for Healthcare Artificial Intelligence announced funding for 12 pilot projects. The laboratory is known as UT REAL Health AI and was launched by the UT System in 2025. The pilots were chosen from 120 systemwide submissions. The page says the awards total more than $3.6 million and that three moonshot projects each received up to $500,000. Among them is an AI clinical assistant for emergency triage and transfer decisions at seven UT hospital sites. Others are a smartphone app that listens for fetal movements, referral triage at UTMB Galveston and a warning system against wrong tooth extraction at UTHealth Houston. One project is AI no-show reduction. The page credits it with saving more than 6,500 appointments and $900,000 in revenue at UTHealth Houston, and it is set to expand to UT Health San Antonio. The page cites no measurement or audit behind those results. The page carries no announcement date.
