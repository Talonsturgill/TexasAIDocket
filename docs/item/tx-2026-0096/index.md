# Senate Water, Agriculture and Rural Affairs takes up data center cooling water on September 1st

The Senate Committee on Water, Agriculture and Rural Affairs carries an interim charge directing it to examine how much water high consumption cooling technologies use. The charge names data centers. The Legislature's own upcoming meetings listing sets the committee for Tuesday, September 1st, 2026 in hearing room E1.012 at the Capitol. It is marked a public hearing, which is the room where a member of the public may testify. The charge list also carries desalination and permitting efficiency. The water question is being taken up as one supply problem rather than as a data center problem alone. How a witness registers is set out in the formal notice and is unconfirmed.
