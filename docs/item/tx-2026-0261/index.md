# TSMC applies for a JETI school tax limitation for a chip plant in Brazos County, and the Comptroller posts its recommendation packet

TSMC Arizona Corporation has applied for a tax limit under the Jobs, Energy, Technology and Innovation Act. It would cap College Station ISD maintenance and operations taxes on a proposed semiconductor plant in Brazos County. The Comptroller posted the application on November 23rd, 2026 and its recommendation packet the next day. The applicant says the plant would make leading edge logic chips on a 2 nanometer or more advanced process, and it ties them to the expansion of AI infrastructure. It projects construction from 2027 to 2031. The packet estimates the company's school tax benefit at $354,128,128. The applicant says it is still weighing Brazos County against Arizona. The College Station ISD board has not acted and no agreement is signed.
