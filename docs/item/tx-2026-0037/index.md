# Laredo City Council declined a data center moratorium, and its direction to write permanent rules has reached three agendas with no recorded action

Laredo City Council declined to impose a temporary moratorium on data center permits at its meeting on July 27th, 2026. The council item asks staff to evaluate the impacts of high intensity data processing facilities on municipal infrastructure, water resources, electrical demand and land use compatibility. It also asks staff to recommend regulatory standards. The city's own record carries that item on the June 15th and July 27th agendas and records no action taken at either meeting. It returned to the August 3rd, 2026 agenda and the city records no action there either. The city attorney told the council that Laredo's existing Land Development Code already prohibits uses that are not specifically permitted. Planning staff reported that neither the city nor Webb County had received an application for a data center.
