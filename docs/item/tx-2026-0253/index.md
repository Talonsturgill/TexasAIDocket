# East Texas A&M researchers test AI that flags a drone fed false GPS, using its own motion sensors and no GPS readings

Researchers in East Texas A&M University's computer science department in Commerce tested whether AI can tell when a drone's reported location is false. The method ignores GPS and reads the drone's own motion sensors for speed and direction over time. The team used a public set of 96 real flights. Of those 54 flew under controlled spoofing attacks. Eight models were compared and pretrained time series models did best. One model given 10 consecutive readings scored 97.5% on a measure that balances caught attacks against false alerts. The study used recorded flights only and running the check in flight is the next step. It was accepted at the WATCH 2026 workshop with National Science Foundation funding.
