# Austin Community College drafts what it calls its first AI policy under a five rule framework and opens it to a town hall on October 12th

Austin Community College says it is developing what it calls its first-ever artificial intelligence policy. The policy is to follow a framework the college calls TRUST, which two collegewide AI committees developed. Its five rules ask people to say where AI was used and to check what it produces. They also ask people to keep their own judgment, to use AI for honest work and to respect copyright. The college's AI resource site already links a PDF titled Acceptable Use of Artificial Intelligence Policy, marked for faculty and staff only. The new policy is not the college's first written word on the subject. The college has set three October events for faculty, staff, students and community members. A town hall at the Rio Grande Campus on October 12th will explore the policies in progress. A faculty convocation on academic integrity follows on October 16th at Eastview. A community seminar meets on October 22nd at Highland. The college says more than 79,000 students rely on it each year. The announcement names no date for adopting the policy and does not say who will vote on it.
