# The University of Texas at Austin requires every course syllabus to declare whether AI is permitted, prohibited or partially permitted

The Office of the Executive Vice President and Provost requires a Course Artificial Intelligence Policy in every syllabus every semester. The policy has to name one of three designations. AI is permitted or prohibited or partially permitted. An instructor who allows it has to write down what is permissible and how a student must disclose use. The university names the requirement one of two new syllabus statements for Fall 2026. That term's classes began August 24th, 2026. The rule on generative AI is therefore set course by course rather than university wide. A student can hold two syllabi with two different answers in the same week.

### Every course has to carry an artificial intelligence policy in its syllabus every semester.

> All courses must include a Course Artificial Intelligence Policy in the course syllabi at the time each syllabus is published each semester.

Source (primary_official): https://provost.utexas.edu/the-office/academic-affairs/your-syllabus-at-ut-austin/

### The policy has to pick one of three designations.

> The Artificial Intelligence Policy must include the University's Course AI Policy Syllabus Statement below and designate whether AI is 1) permitted, 2) prohibited, or 3) partially permitted in each course.

Source (primary_official): https://provost.utexas.edu/the-office/academic-affairs/your-syllabus-at-ut-austin/

### An instructor allowing AI has to write the conditions down and say how a student discloses use.

> If an instructor permits or partially permits use of AI in their courses, they must also provide written instructions on what is permissible and what is not and how they expect students to disclose such use.

Source (primary_official): https://provost.utexas.edu/the-office/academic-affairs/your-syllabus-at-ut-austin/
