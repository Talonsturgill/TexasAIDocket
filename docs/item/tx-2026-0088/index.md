# Brazos County Commissioners Court assigns the RELLIS data center tax abatement to ThisWay Global after the original developer's bankruptcy

Brazos County posted a Tax Code Section 312.207 notice that its Commissioners Court would consider assigning the tax abatement agreement for the RELLIS Campus data and research center. The agreement was originally approved on April 29th, 2025 with RELLIS Campus Data and Research Center, LLC. The pending assignee named in the notice is ThisWay, Global, Inc. The county's own notice puts the estimated cost of improvements and tangible personal property at a minimum of approximately 700 million dollars. The land sits in a reinvestment zone on the RELLIS campus. That campus belongs to The Texas A and M University System, so the abatement agreement is what decides whether the county collects anything from the build.
