# Comptroller bars state grant money from buying or renewing Flock cameras and other license plate readers

Texas Comptroller Don Huffines announced a ban on October 13th, 2026. State grant money may no longer buy or contract for Flock cameras, automated license plate readers and other vehicle identification systems. Those systems turn plate images into computer readable data. The restriction applies to all future grants the Comptroller's office administers. The agency says it will write it into grant solicitations and agreements. A grantee may not spend grant funds on the equipment or services, and may not enter into, extend or renew a contract for them. The release names Flock Safety, Motorola Solutions and Axon. It speaks only to grants the office administers and prints no count of the grants or dollars affected.
