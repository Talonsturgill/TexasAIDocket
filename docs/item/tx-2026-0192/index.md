# Asked for AI camera fire detections, Xcel counted cameras

The Public Utility Commission of Texas approved the system resiliency plan of Southwestern Public Service Company, which serves the Panhandle as Xcel Energy, in Docket 57463. The order lists the metrics the utility must carry into its annual reports. One of them is the number of artificial intelligence camera fire detections. The utility filed its first annual report in Project 57941 on May 1st, 2026. Against that metric it reports 33 cameras installed and projects 34 for 2026, and it gives no count of detections. The same report counts 24 wildfire ignitions associated with its overhead power lines and puts the 2026 capital cost of the AI camera project 64.04 percent above the plan's estimate.
