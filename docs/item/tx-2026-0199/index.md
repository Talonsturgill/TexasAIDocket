# National Science Foundation funds Sagebrush, an AI inference platform for researchers nationwide, hosted at UT Austin's supercomputing center

The National Science Foundation made a cooperative agreement with the University of Texas at Austin under its National AI Research Resource program. It pays for Sagebrush, a platform that runs large AI models for researchers on SambaNova processors rather than on GPUs. It is hosted at the Texas Advanced Computing Center and is meant for researchers across the country, reached through Jupyter notebooks and chatbot interfaces. The award record dates the award to July 20th, 2026 and gives September 15th, 2026 as the start of the work. It runs to August 31st, 2028. The abstract names no Texas project it will serve and no results yet.
