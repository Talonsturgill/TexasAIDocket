# UT Southwestern finds AI triage models moved some emergency patients who later got worse down the line after one stigmatizing detail was added

UT Southwestern Medical Center reported on October 20th, 2026 a study published in npj Digital Medicine. Its researchers paired emergency patients of similar starting severity, using records from Stanford Medicine and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. Three open source large language models were asked which patient was more likely to deteriorate and should be seen first. Before any social or stigmatizing detail was added, all three picked the higher risk patient at rates well above chance. A line describing frequent emergency visits or past psychiatric emergencies most often moved a model away from the patient who later deteriorated. In some instances nearly 10% of patients were wrongly downgraded. The authors say bias varies by model and that each tool should be tested against real patient outcomes before use. No Texas hospital is reported using these models for triage.
