# TACC opens Horizon to early users, a GPU supercomputer in Round Rock that ranks No. 24 in the world

The Texas Advanced Computing Center at UT Austin has opened its Horizon supercomputer to early users, the university said on November 17th, 2026. Horizon is the centerpiece of the National Science Foundation's Leadership-Class Computing Facility. Its first phase has 4,000 GPUs. It delivered 132 petaflops on the High-Performance Linpack benchmark, and the university says that ranks it No. 24 on the November 2026 TOP500 list. The machine runs in a new 20 megawatt data center with liquid cooling in Round Rock. The university's release gives no opening date, no count of early users and no list of the first projects.
