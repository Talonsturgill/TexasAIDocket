# Waymo opens its San Antonio robotaxis to anyone with the app, the airport included, while its flood recall remedy is still listed as under development

Waymo opened its San Antonio robotaxi service to anyone with the app on October 14th, 2026, and the service now reaches San Antonio International Airport. Riders had come in by invitation since February, when the service opened across 60 square miles of the city. In April one of its empty cars washed into Salado Creek during heavy rain, and another was stranded in floodwater earlier that month. Waymo then filed a software recall with federal safety regulators. The recall says the system may let a car slow and then drive into standing water on higher speed roadways. The recall record lists the remedy as under development, with tighter weather limits on operations in the meantime.
