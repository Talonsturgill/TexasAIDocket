# A Texas Senate committee took testimony on how much water data center cooling uses and whether local water providers are told

The Senate Committee on Water, Agriculture, and Rural Affairs sat on September 1st, 2026 on the water demands of energy intensive cooling. The Senate's video archive carries the recording under the committee's name and that date. The committee's page lists the sitting among the interim work of the current Legislature, beside a second sitting the following day. Senator Charles Perry chairs the committee. What the committee heard is on the recording. No recommendation from either sitting has been published. A hearing is not a decision. It is the stage at which the committee that writes the state's water bills settles what it will recommend.
