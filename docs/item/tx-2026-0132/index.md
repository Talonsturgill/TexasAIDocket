# Texas A&M tells its campus to run AI through a university portal rather than a public chatbot

Texas A&M University Technology Services published guidance on August 31st, 2026 naming TAMU AI Chat as the university approved way for faculty, staff and students to use commercial AI models. The single platform carries models from several vendors behind a university login. The university states the service supports information classified up to and including University-Confidential, which is what decides whether a researcher may put non-public university data into a model at all. It also states that conversations in the tool are not used to train external models. The same channel that protects the data is a channel the university controls, and the guidance does not say what the university does with the conversations itself.
