# Round Rock says its zoning does not cover drone delivery hubs as Zipline starts flying from one outside city limits

The City of Round Rock tells residents that drone delivery hubs are not specifically addressed in its current zoning ordinances. Zipline is beginning commercial deliveries from a facility in the city's extraterritorial jurisdiction, near North Kenney Fort and Joe DiMaggio Boulevards, where the city does not zone. The city says the FAA controls flight paths and that drones may fly over homes on approved routes. It is working with Zipline toward a second hub inside city limits behind Renaissance Square, and says that site will help it write rules for future hubs. Zipline says its aircraft carry onboard detect and avoid and are watched from a remote operations center. No hub rule has been adopted yet.
