# The grid operator sends every conditionally classified large load a questionnaire, with a notarized attestation and ten business days to answer

ERCOT published the user guide for the Verification RFI on September 11th, 2026, the questionnaire it issues as part of the Batch Zero eligibility verification. The guide states the questionnaire goes to every large load that was conditionally classified on the list distributed to transmission service providers on September 3rd. Each of the three parties on a project, the load entity, the distribution service provider and the transmission service provider, answers the sections assigned to its own role. Which sections a project sees depends on the conditional classification ERCOT gave it. Every participating party has to upload a notarized attestation before the response can be submitted. Responses are due within ten business days of issuance and a five day extension may be requested through the transmission service provider. A load entity that fails to supply what its provider needs may be taken off the Batch Zero process. Only the transmission service provider can submit the finished response, and once submitted it is locked.

## Evidence

### The grid operator has issued a Verification RFI as part of the Batch Zero eligibility verification.

> ERCOT has issued a Request for Information and Production (RFI, referred to as the Verification RFI) as part of the Batch Zero Eligibility Verification process.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf

### It goes to every large load on the conditional classification list distributed on September 3rd.

> This is being issued to all Large Loads that were conditionally classified on the list distributed to TSPs on Thursday, September 3, 2026

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf

### Responses are due within ten business days and a five day extension may be requested.

> Responses are due within 10 business days of issuance. A 5-day extension may be requested from ERCOT through your TSP.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf

### A load entity that does not supply what its provider needs may be taken off the Batch Zero process.

> If the Load Entity fails to provide information and/or supporting documentation necessary for the TSP to timely submit a completed response to this Verification RFI, the Large Load may be removed from the Batch Zero Process.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf

### Every participating party has to complete a notarized attestation.

> Each participating party (ILLE, DSP, and TSP) is required to complete a notarized attestation. Upload your company's attestation file in the designated Section before submitting.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf

### Which sections a project answers depends on the conditional classification it was given.

> The sections and questions you see depend on your role and the Large Load Project's conditional classification in Batch Zero.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf

### Only the transmission service provider may submit the finished response, and it locks on submission.

> It is the TSP's responsibility to submit the fully completed Verification RFI to ERCOT. Only the TSP has the Submit to ERCOT button available. Once submitted, the Verification RFI is locked and cannot be edited.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf

### Answers are treated as confidential to the extent the law permits.

> All information submitted will be treated as confidential to the extent permitted by law.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/11/Verification-RFI-User-Guide-v1.1.pdf
