# A foundation grant funds a Texas A&M campus lab where AI and robots are to develop new metal alloys

Texas A&M says a $2 million grant to its Department of Materials Science and Engineering will create the Aggie AI Foundry. The grant comes through the WoodNext Foundation, which manages the philanthropy of Roku founder Anthony Wood and his wife Susan. The university describes a campus facility with a fully automated system that takes a new metal alloy from idea through testing, with AI and robotic workers doing the work. It says the process will cut alloy development from months to a week or less. It also says the foundry will be the first lab of its kind in academia. The speed figure is the university's projection for a facility that is not yet built, and the release gives no opening date.
