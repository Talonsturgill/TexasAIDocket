# The utility commission sues the Attorney General to keep the county and water planning area of each surveyed data center from release

The Public Utility Commission of Texas filed an original petition in Travis County district court on October 5th, 2026 against Attorney General Ken Paxton in his official capacity. It challenges two open records letter rulings, OR2026-038988 and OR2026-040171. They would require it to release two fields from the facility level responses to its Energy and Water Use Survey. The fields are the facility county and the facility state regional water planning area. The commission asks the court to declare both fields confidential under the Public Information Act and the Texas Homeland Security Act. The rulings answered six requests from five requestors, among them reporters at the Texas Tribune, the Texas Observer and the Fort Worth Star-Telegram. The petition says the requestors may intervene.
