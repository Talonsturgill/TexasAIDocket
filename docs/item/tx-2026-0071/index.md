# SpaceX will build the Terafab semiconductor plant in Grimes County on a state grant and two school district agreements

Governor Greg Abbott announced on August 6th, 2026 that SpaceX will construct a vertically integrated semiconductor fabrication plant in Grimes County. A Texas Enterprise Fund grant of $30 million has been extended to the company. The project is also qualified under the state's Jobs Energy Technology and Innovation program. The release states a first phase of more than $16.8 billion in capital investment and 3,000 new jobs. The company's own statement in the release says the plant will produce AI chips at scale. Two school districts have agreements of their own. The Iola board voted to enter one and the Anderson-Shiro superintendent describes another.
