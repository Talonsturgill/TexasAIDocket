# The National Science Foundation puts the national AI research resource into standing operations, co-run from Austin

The National Science Foundation established the NSF National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource Operations Center on September 1st, 2026. That moved the resource out of a pilot begun in 2024 and into standing operations. The San Diego Supercomputer Center at the University of California San Diego leads it, in collaboration with the Texas Advanced Computing Center at The University of Texas at Austin. The center coordinates the resource providers and integrates computing and data and models and tools. It operates the national portal and provides support and training to users. The foundation says the pilot has supported over 800 research projects and thousands of innovators and students nationwide.
