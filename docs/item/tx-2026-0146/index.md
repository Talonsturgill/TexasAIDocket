# Amazon and Wiwynn say they will expand the El Paso County plant that builds the racks going into AI data centers

Amazon and Wiwynn announced an expansion of Wiwynn's manufacturing operations in Socorro, the Taiwanese contract manufacturer's first plant in the United States. Wiwynn builds and integrates the server systems and racks that go into Amazon's data centers. Wiwynn's chief executive puts the investment at over 1.6 billion dollars and Amazon's vice president of Annapurna Labs states the number of advanced manufacturing roles it supports. Socorro is in El Paso County, which is the one corner of Texas that is not on the ERCOT grid.
