# The state technology agency publishes its account of carrying out the four AI laws the 89th Legislature passed

The Texas Department of Information Resources published an account of its work implementing Senate Bill 1964, House Bill 2818, House Bill 3512 and House Bill 149. It states that Senate Bill 1964 established the state's AI governance framework. It states that the same bill directed the agency to build a public sector AI sandbox and write rules for government use of heightened scrutiny AI. It states that House Bill 3512 requires annual AI awareness training for certain state and local government employees and officials. It states that House Bill 149 requires governmental entities to disclose their use of AI and creates the Texas Artificial Intelligence Council. The agency reports adopting Texas Administrative Code Chapter 219, which carries the AI Systems Code of Ethics.

### The agency states what the governance bill directed it to build.

> Senate Bill 1964 established Texas' AI governance framework and directed DIR to develop the Public Sector AI Sandbox and adopt rules for government use of heightened-scrutiny AI.

Source (primary_official): https://dir.texas.gov/news/ai-texas-dir-implementation-laws-89th-legislature

### A training requirement now reaches local government as well as state.

> House Bill 3512 requires annual AI Awareness Training for certain state and local government employees and officials.

Source (primary_official): https://dir.texas.gov/news/ai-texas-dir-implementation-laws-89th-legislature

### A disclosure requirement and a council come from the fourth bill.

> House Bill 149 requires disclosures of use of AI by governmental entities, creating consumer protections, the Texas Artificial Intelligence Council, and a regulatory sandbox while expanding the Information Resources Deployment Review to include agency AI system inventories.

Source (primary_official): https://dir.texas.gov/news/ai-texas-dir-implementation-laws-89th-legislature

### The agency names the rule chapter it adopted and what it carries.

> Adopting Texas Administrative Code Chapter 219, which includes the AI Systems Code of Ethics, and publishing AI governance resources, including, notices and a model AI Acceptable Use Policy.

Source (primary_official): https://dir.texas.gov/news/ai-texas-dir-implementation-laws-89th-legislature

### A second bill created the division doing the work.

> House Bill 2818 established DIR's AI Division to support AI initiatives in the public and private sectors, as well as modernization efforts for state and local government.
