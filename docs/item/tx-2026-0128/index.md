# Tesla registers a semiconductor plant at its Austin campus with the state and the filing says so in its own words

Tesla registered a project with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation on August 25th, 2026 under the project name Austin Semiconductor Fab. The address is 1 Harold Green Road in Austin and the filing's own county field reads Travis. The scope of work reads as ground up construction and complete interior finish out of the semiconductor facility at 489,600 square feet. The stated completion date is December 31st, 2029. The filing states the project is privately funded on private land for private use. A registration with the licensing department is an accessibility review filing. It is not a building permit or an air permit or a grid interconnection.
