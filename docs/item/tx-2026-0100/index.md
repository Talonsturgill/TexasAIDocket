# UT Austin puts a computer science and AI course in the core every undergraduate takes

The University of Texas at Austin published a new Core Curriculum on August 13th, 2026 that names Computer Science and AI among the foundational courses. The others alongside it are Great Questions of Human Life, Western Civilization and Great Ideas in Science. The university says the core will be introduced in phases starting with new courses in the autumn of 2027, so no student sits the requirement yet. A history major on that campus will take an AI course to graduate, which is a different kind of AI decision from buying a machine.
