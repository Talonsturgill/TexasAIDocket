# San Antonio counts loose dogs in 32 study areas with phone cameras and object detection software

San Antonio Animal Care Services is working with Southwest Research Institute on an automated survey of unrestrained dogs. A cell phone mounted on a vehicle window and paired with object detection and data analysis software will record the street. Automated and human review of the clips will verify whether each detected dog was unrestrained. The same review will determine any human supervision, verify how many dogs were present and assess whether the dog was owned or a stray. The release describes strays as appearing malnourished and skittish. It says healthier and more approachable dogs are likely owned and are classified as roaming. The survey will repeat the city's 2019 count in the same 32 study areas, and field research is expected to run through December 2026. The city says the project is not intended to count every unrestrained dog in San Antonio. SwRI will forward the department a final report with its findings and data.
