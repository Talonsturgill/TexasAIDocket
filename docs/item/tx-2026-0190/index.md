# College Station City Council votes 4 to 2 to keep its Flock plate reader cameras on state and city roads

The College Station City Council voted 4 to 2 on September 24th, 2026 to approve two agreements with Flock Group. One covers automatic license plate readers on state highway roads and the other covers cameras on other roads. Council members Mark Smith and Bob Yancy voted against. The cameras photograph the rear of passing vehicles day and night and sort each one by make, model and color. The city says no search can be run without reasonable suspicion. It says its police department decides which agencies can search the network and keeps every search indefinitely. An automated audit tool that went live in April is the city's named check on misuse. The city reports lower property crime since the cameras went up and says the drop correlates with them. It does not claim they caused it.
