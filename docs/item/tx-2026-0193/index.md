# Judges back El Paso Electric's gas plant for Meta only if other customers don't pay

El Paso Electric asked the Public Utility Commission of Texas to amend its certificate so it can build and own the McCloud facility. It is a 366 MW natural gas plant in northeast El Paso County made of 813 modular gas generators. It would primarily serve a data center being developed by Wurldwide LLC, which the case record says is owned by Meta Platforms. Meta calls the El Paso site an AI data center that can scale to one gigawatt. On September 23rd, 2026 two administrative law judges issued their proposal for decision. They found a need for capacity to serve the data center. They also found the utility did not show the plant is a cost-effective and reliable resource, and did not show it adequately considered alternatives. They recommend approval only if the utility's other retail customers are held harmless from the plant's capital and operating costs, and denial without that condition. The commission decides at an open meeting. El Paso City Council has since authorized a counteroffer seeking a full hold harmless for ratepayers. The utility serves outside the ERCOT region, so no ERCOT large load rule decides who pays here.
