# Richardson ISD drafts a legislative ask that puts classroom AI guidance first

The Richardson Independent School District board reviewed a draft of the district's 2027 legislative priorities at its September 18th, 2026 meeting. The draft is built on three areas and the first of them is the responsible use of technology and artificial intelligence. That priority asks the Legislature for resources and guidance for the thoughtful use of artificial intelligence in education, alongside age appropriate approaches to screen time. The district says it gathered the input behind the draft through community roundtables with parent teacher associations, local chambers of commerce, higher education partners and other community organizations. The board is expected to vote on the final priorities on October 15th. The draft asks for resources and guidance and does not say what the state already provides, so nothing here establishes whether any exists. That is what makes this a record of where classroom AI rules are actually being written.
