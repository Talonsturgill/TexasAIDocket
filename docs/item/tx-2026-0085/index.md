# National Science Foundation awards Rice University a cooperative agreement for a self driving laboratory that designs its own semiconductor experiments

The National Science Foundation obligated a cooperative agreement to William Marsh Rice University for a test bed called READINESS. The award runs from August 1st, 2026 to July 31st, 2030 and its estimated total is carried in the award record quoted below. The agency's own abstract describes the platform as a self driving laboratory that designs, tests and improves material synthesis processes with minimal human intervention. The performance site is Houston and the principal investigator of record is Jun Lou.
