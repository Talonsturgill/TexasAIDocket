# Denton City Council takes up a data center moratorium resolution and a schedule of the public hearings it would require

An agenda item asked the Denton City Council to approve a resolution on a moratorium. It covers the acceptance and processing and approval of applications for data center development inside the city limits. The same item asked the council to approve a schedule for the notice and public hearings the moratorium would require. The city's legislative record shows no final action and the meeting minutes for August 18th, 2026 are not finalized. Denton runs its own municipal electric utility, so a moratorium here is a city deciding whether to keep taking applications for loads it would have to serve itself.
