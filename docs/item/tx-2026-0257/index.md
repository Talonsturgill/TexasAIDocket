# Dell Med and UT's IC² Institute put 80 clinicians through simulated patient visits, half of them with Claude, to see how AI changes clinical reasoning

The IC² Institute and Dell Medical School at UT Austin launched an AI Learning Lab in October. Eighty Dell Med clinicians meet actors playing patients and reach a diagnosis. They are a mix of faculty, residents and students. Half have Claude to help. The other half use UpToDate with its AI turned off. Researchers watch how each group reasons and how fast clinicians reach for the AI. They also ask whether clinicians question its output. No results are published yet.
