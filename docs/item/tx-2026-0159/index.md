# ERCOT tells its board that AI training loads can fatigue grid generators and drafts a limit on how fast their power draw may swing

The Electric Reliability Council of Texas put a list of emerging Large Load risks in front of its own Board of Directors at the September 14th and 15th, 2026 meeting. The presentation names three risks and a mitigation for each, and the three sit at three different stages. Ride through requirements are already in force, a model quality rule went to the board this month, and the third rule has not been written yet. The grid operator states that Large Loads tied to data center AI training processes can swing their power draw fast enough to perturb synchronous generator shafts. It says that swing can cause equipment fatigue or damage. Its draft rule would hold a Large Load's power variation to 10 MW per 5 seconds. The same page states that the dynamic models the grid operator uses to study these loads have proven inaccurate, and that Texas A&M University is building better ones. The presentation was for information only and the board was asked to take no action on it.

## Evidence

Every fact above rests on one of these. The words are the source's own.

### The grid operator states that data center AI training loads can swing power draw fast enough to damage generators.

> Large Loads, particularly those associated with data center AI training processes, are capable of producing rapid fluctuations in active power consumption. These variations can induce perturbations on synchronous generator shafts and potentially result in equipment fatigue or damage.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The draft rule names the limit it would set on a Large Load's power variation.

> Consequently, ERCOT's draft NOGRR, Large Load MW Variation Requirements, will recommend a limit of 10 MW per 5 seconds for power variation in Large Loads.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The grid operator says how long it has been investigating the risk.

> Since July 2025, ERCOT collaborated with industry experts and engaged stakeholders to investigate these risks.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The same page says the models the grid operator uses to study these loads do not work.

> Dynamic models for Large Loads have proven to be inaccurate and thus are inadequate for studying their grid impacts.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The grid operator names who is building the replacement models.

> ERCOT is working with Texas A&M University on developing more accurate models.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The university has finished a model for one kind of load and not for the other.

> Texas A&M has released a new model for crypto mining facilities and is working on a data center model.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The first of the three risks already has a rule in force.

> The PUCT approved NOGRR 282/ NPRR 1308 in July, effective on August 1.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The second of the three risks reached the board this month.

> TAC endorsed PGRR 144 in August and is up for Board consideration in September.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The third rule has not been filed and the grid operator gives a window rather than a date.

> ERCOT plans to submit NOGRR in September or October.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf

### The presentation states what the board was asked to do about it.

Source (primary_official): https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update.pdf
