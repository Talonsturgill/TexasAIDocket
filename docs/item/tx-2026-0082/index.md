# UTMB moved its Epic health record into Microsoft's cloud and named advanced technologies as the reason

The University of Texas Medical Branch migrated its electronic health record from servers it runs itself onto Microsoft's Azure platform, and announced the move on August 13th, 2026. The institution states the cutover took nineteen minutes and that the migration was first announced as part of a five year agreement with Microsoft in May 2025. Its own account names a stronger foundation for advanced technologies that support patient care, research, education and operations. Where a public hospital's patient records physically sit is the decision that has to be made before any clinical AI tool reaches a bedside.
