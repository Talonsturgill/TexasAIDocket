# Texas A&M's CLARKE turns drone imagery into building and road damage maps in minutes, and Florida's state drone team has made it standard procedure

CLARKE is an AI tool built at Texas A&M. It reads drone imagery taken after a hurricane, tornado or wildfire and grades the damage to each building and road. Its developers say it does in minutes what takes 18 to 24 hours otherwise. The models learned from drone imagery of more than 21,000 damaged homes in 10 disasters, Hurricane Harvey among them. 125 responders from 63 agencies in 14 states and Puerto Rico have trained on it. Texas A&M says Florida's state drone remote sensing team has written it into its standard operating procedures. The November 24th release names no Texas agency among its users.
