# El Paso City Council adopted a letter asking Governor Abbott to clarify his data center directives

The El Paso City Council approved and adopted a letter to Governor Greg Abbott seeking clarification on his directives on data center development throughout Texas. The council took the item at its regular meeting on August 18th, 2026 as agenda item 34, and the motion carried. The item had been introduced on August 11th, 2026 by a member of the City Council. The question underneath it is jurisdictional, because El Paso is served by El Paso Electric in the Western Interconnection and statewide directives written around ERCOT do not automatically reach it.
