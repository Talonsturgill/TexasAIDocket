# Federal survey office asks whether the household internet survey should start measuring AI use, closing November 9th

The National Telecommunications and Information Administration submitted its Internet Use Survey to the Office of Management and Budget for renewal on September 9th, 2026. It asked the public directly whether more data is needed on artificial intelligence. The notice names AI use at work or home and attitudes toward AI as areas it is asking about. Comments must be received on or before November 9th, 2026.
