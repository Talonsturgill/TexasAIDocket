# National Science Foundation makes Texas Southern University one of five regional nodes in an HBCU network for AI and quantum science

The National Science Foundation made a cooperative agreement with Texas Southern University in Houston under its Accelerating Research Translation program. It is Texas Southern's part of the HBCU AI - Quantum Innovation Network, which runs through five regional nodes with Clark Atlanta University as the lead institution. Texas Southern leads one of the five. The abstract says the network will build education, workforce preparation and research ties with industry, government, small businesses and community groups. It names regional meetings and workshops, shared training resources and pathways for students and faculty. The award record gives the start of the work as October 1st, 2026.
