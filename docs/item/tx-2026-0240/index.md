# Permian Resources tells investors it now runs an AI system that watches its artificial lift

Permian Resources filed its third quarter results with the SEC on November 4th, 2026. The company says its production operations team has deployed an AI-powered artificial lift monitoring system to raise runtime and well performance. It says it is also feeding its own subsurface data into large language models to guide where wellbores go and how wells are drilled. The release gives no measured result for either tool. It reports 457,528 barrels of oil equivalent a day for the quarter. Its annual report puts about 67% of its leasehold acreage in Texas, with drilling work tied to Reeves County. The same release lists AI and machine learning among the factors in its cautionary note. In May the Railroad Commission told the Texas Tribune it was not aware of any use of AI by oil and gas companies. ExxonMobil separately says it plans to automate more than half of its unconventional drilling fleet by the end of 2028, a plan its post describes as automation rather than AI.
