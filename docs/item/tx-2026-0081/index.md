# NSF awarded UT Austin a grant to replace hand written robot safety rules with a model trained on demonstrated safe behavior

The National Science Foundation made an award to the University of Texas at Austin on August 13th, 2026 under its Foundational Research in Robotics program. The project trains a model on expert demonstrations and uses it as a filter that pulls a robot's action back toward behavior that was demonstrated as safe. The award record states the obligation as FY 2026 = $360,000.00 and runs the project from October 1st, 2026 through September 30th, 2029. Its own abstract is the argument for it, because it says engineers today must define every dangerous situation by hand.
