# SpaceX asks Starbase for a tax abatement on a $24 billion gas-fired power plant and super-compute facility beside Brownsville, with a hearing December 9th

The City of Starbase gave notice that its City Commission will meet on December 9th, 2026. It will consider a property tax abatement agreement and a related economic development agreement with Space Exploration Technologies Corp. SpaceX proposes to build, own and operate a gas-fired power plant and super-compute facility. The notice puts the capital investment for the initial phases at approximately $24 billion and says the project may grow in later phases. The site is an approximately 444-acre tract in Cameron County, just east of Brownsville between Boca Chica Boulevard and the Port of Brownsville, now inside Starbase's city limits. The Texas Tribune reports Brownsville commissioners voted unanimously in August to take the land out of the city at SpaceX's request. In exchange SpaceX agreed to give up to $220 million for water and wastewater projects, and Starbase later annexed the land. The Tribune reported on November 10th that Brownsville's 90-day moratorium on data center applications had taken effect the week before, and the site sits outside it. The Tribune reports the gas plant could power the data center without a connection to the state grid. SpaceX did not answer the Tribune's questions.
