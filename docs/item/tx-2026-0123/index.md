# UT Austin funds four fusion seed grants and says in its own release that the AI method offers limited guarantees

The Institute for Fusion Studies, the Oden Institute and the Cockrell School jointly funded four fusion seed grants announced on August 31st, 2026. Each pairs a principal investigator in computation with a second in fusion studies and supports graduate students for two years. Three of the four build surrogate machine learning models, covering real time plasma shape control, liquid metal wall alloy screening and alpha particle leakage. The university's own release states the limits of the reinforcement learning approach rather than only its promise.
