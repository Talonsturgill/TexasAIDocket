# A Sugar Land maker of AI data center optics says its United States expansion is waiting on electrical power

Applied Optoelectronics filed its third quarter results with the SEC on November 5th, 2026. The company makes optical transceivers for AI data centers, and its quarterly report says it makes its laser chips only in its Sugar Land facility. Its chief financial officer says total equipment capacity is approaching 330,000 units a month. The same officer says the 2027 expansion plan in the United States will take longer to finish, because obtaining the electrical power it needs is taking longer than expected. The company expects equipment for over 500,000 of its 800G and 1.6 Tb products a month by the end of 2026. It expects capacity for over 1 million of those transceivers a month by the end of 2027. Its quarterly report lists new space at Gillingham Lane in Sugar Land and a property it bought in Pearland. It also lists two buildings still to be built on Chimney Rock Road in Houston. Neither filing says which building is waiting on power or which utility serves it.
