# Kodiak and Charger USA start hauling freight between Dallas and Laredo on autonomous trucks with a safety driver behind the wheel

Kodiak AI and Charger USA announced on October 8th that trucks running the Kodiak Driver are hauling refrigerated and dry freight. The lane runs between Charger's terminals in Dallas and Laredo. The companies say their first delivery was on September 8th. A safety driver sits behind the wheel today, and the companies say they intend to go driverless once Kodiak completes its safety case. It is Kodiak's first route serving Laredo. No public body voted on it.
