# Railroad Commissioner Wayne Christian tells Texas oil and gas operators to harden their networks against AI driven cyberattacks

On November 18th Railroad Commissioner Wayne Christian urged Texas oil and gas producers, pipeline operators and other energy companies to strengthen their cybersecurity. He pointed to a November 13th FBI advisory on ARTEX, an AI driven tool attackers have used to automate parts of intrusions. It is one commissioner's call to action. No rule, order or commission vote is attached.

### On November 18th Railroad Commissioner Wayne Christian renewed his call for Texas oil and gas producers, pipeline operators and other energy companies to strengthen their cybersecurity.

> RRC Commissioner Wayne Christian today is renewing his call for Texas oil and natural gas producers, pipeline operators and other energy companies to act now to bolster their cybersecurity defenses and remain vigilant as major businesses, financial institutions and critical services across the nation report widespread outages and disruptions, underscoring the growing risks to the nation’s interconnected infrastructure.

Source (primary_official): https://www.rrc.texas.gov/news/rrc-commissioner-christian-sounds-alarm-on-ai-fueled-cyber-threats-to-texas-energy-infrastructure/

> The outages across the nation closely follow a Nov. 13 advisory from the FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center detailing the growing threat posed by ARTEX, an artificial intelligence-driven autonomous penetration-testing framework that malicious actors have used to automate multiple stages of cyber intrusions.
