PUCT open meeting calendar published as a live feed through mid 2027

The Public Utility Commission publishes its open meetings and public comment deadlines as a machine readable feed. Every entry is marked open to the public and names the hearing room. The schedule runs well into 2027, so a reader can see in advance when the commission will take up large load and transmission matters.

How to take part

Open meetings are held in Commissioners Hearing Room 7-100 at commission headquarters in Austin and the feed marks each one open to the public. Subscribing to the feed is the simplest way for a member of the public to see a comment deadline before it passes rather than after.
