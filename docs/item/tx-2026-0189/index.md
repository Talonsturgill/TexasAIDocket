# Justice Department takes comment on a judgment barring a Frisco apartment manager from rent software fed by rivals' data

The Justice Department filed a proposed final judgment on September 4th, 2026 against Pinnacle Property Management Services, an apartment manager headquartered in Frisco. Pinnacle licensed AI Revenue Management and YieldStar from RealPage, which is headquartered in Richardson. The judgment would bar Pinnacle from any outside revenue management product that uses other landlords' nonpublic data to recommend or set prices. It would also bar Pinnacle from sharing competitively sensitive information with competitors. The government may inspect Pinnacle's own pricing product down to its code and pseudocode. A monitor can be appointed if Pinnacle uses outside pricing software without a qualifying certification. The notice was published on September 18th, 2026 and invites public comment for 60 days. The judgment would expire five years after the court enters it. The case against RealPage and the other landlords goes on, and Pinnacle must cooperate in it.
