# National Science Foundation funds an open access robot run alloy laboratory at Texas A&M that outside researchers may book

The National Science Foundation made a cooperative agreement to the Texas A&M Engineering Experiment Station in College Station. It funds the Autonomous Robotic Metallurgist Materials Innovation Platform. The award record describes a national user facility that designs and makes and tests new alloys in one autonomous laboratory. It joins robotics and artificial intelligence with simulation and automated experimentation. The record states that scientists and engineers from universities and national laboratories and industry come to it as hands on users and run their own campaigns. Remote access modes are named as a way to reach researchers at institutions outside the top research tier. The agreement runs from September 1st, 2026 to August 31st, 2032.
