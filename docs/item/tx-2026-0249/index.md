# UT San Antonio and the VA test whether AI can read signs of mild brain injury in veterans' own stories

The San Antonio Medical Foundation gave a $200,000 grant to a one year pilot. Researchers at UT San Antonio and the South Texas Veterans Health Care System lead it. UT Health San Antonio announced it on November 5th, 2026. The study will use natural language processing, a form of AI, on recordings already made at the San Antonio VA. In them veterans describe how they were injured and an important life event. The recordings were made at several points during treatment. The team plans to measure the transcribed speech for coherence, emotional tone, organization and level of detail. Today that analysis takes slow transcription and coding, often by several reviewers. The team says the tool would support clinicians and not replace them. The first question is whether an automated method can find communication differences at all. The second is how those differences track symptoms such as anxiety, depression and poor sleep. The source reports no results yet.
