# Grid operator weighs a year's grace for data center cooling equipment on ride-through rules

A revision request pending at the Electric Reliability Council of Texas concerns the grid's ride-through requirements. It would give a large computational load up to 365 days after its in-service date to comply. It applies where the cooling load can't meet the standard on the day it starts. The posting names the reason as design and manufacturing limits, including vendors still adapting chiller and variable frequency drive equipment to the applicable standards. Ride-through is the requirement that keeps a large load on the system through a voltage dip rather than dropping off all at once. The request was posted on July 21st, 2026 and is still pending, with the reliability and operations subcommittee named as its next group.

### The request would allow ride-through compliance up to 365 days after the in-service date where cooling load can't meet it.

> This Nodal Operating Guide Revision Request (NOGRR) permits a Large Computational Load (LCL) to achieve compliance with the applicable ride-through requirements within 365 days after its in-service date where the LCL's cooling load is unable to satisfy such requirements as of the in-service date due to ongoing design and manufacturing limitations, including constraints associated with vendors' continued adaptation of chiller and Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) equipment to the applicable standards.
