# A statewide poll finds most Texas voters oppose a data center in their own community for a second survey running

The Texas Politics Project at the University of Texas at Austin polled 1200 registered Texas voters from August 5th to 13th, 2026. It reported that 57 percent oppose the construction of a data center in their community. Only 30 percent expressed support. The project calls the result statistically indistinguishable from its June reading. Republicans split closely in August at 45 percent supporting against 43 percent opposing. Democrats and independents stayed decisively opposed.
