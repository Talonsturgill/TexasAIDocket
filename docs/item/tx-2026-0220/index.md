# San Antonio takes two data center zoning proposals to a public hearing on October 20th and a council vote on October 29th

The City of San Antonio's Department of Development Services presented two proposals to change the Unified Development Code for data centers. One would require every data center to receive a Specific Use Authorization zoning designation, which would give the City Council a vote on each proposed data center. The city says the proposals come before the Zoning Commission at a public hearing on October 20th, 2026. They go to a final City Council vote on October 29th, 2026. Residents can sign up to comment at both meetings. Councilmember Ric Galvan, who asked for the review, says every new data center in city limits should require a council vote.
