# UT Arlington replaces its business analytics degrees with artificial intelligence degrees

The University of Texas at Arlington College of Business launched two artificial intelligence degrees on September 14th, 2026. They are a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence for Business. The new degrees take the place of the college's existing Business Analytics programs rather than sitting beside them. The college describes a redesigned curriculum aimed at using the technology for business strategy, productivity and decision making. Certificate offerings accompany the degrees. No start term or first cohort size is stated in the announcement.
