# A UT San Antonio lab builds a solar flood sensor that runs its own AI at the street, tested so far on validation data rather than a flood

UT San Antonio researchers led by Chen Pan built a prototype street level flood warning station. It combines solar power, weather sensing, four optical water level sensors mounted at different heights and a machine learning model that runs on the device itself. When it sees rising water it sends long range radio signals to a local hub, where a second model maps where flooding could spread. The university reports a validation accuracy from initial assessments after training and a parts cost for one station. It names no field deployment and reports no result from a real flood. The work was funded in part by a Texas Coastal Management Program grant through NOAA. The team aims to turn it into a weatherized product for small coastal towns, homeowners' associations, farms and industrial sites.
