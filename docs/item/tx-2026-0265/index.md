# Zipline starts delivering Uber Eats orders by drone in the Houston area, first in Spring

Zipline says its drone delivery for Uber Eats customers in Houston launched on November 17th and that Dallas is next. DroneXL reports the first service area is Spring, a suburb north of Houston in unincorporated Harris County. Zipline says its deliveries happen from 330 feet above the ground and that its aircraft carry up to 8 pounds for 10 miles. DroneXL reports that a 2017 state law bars local governments from regulating drone operations. It also reports the FAA's review of Zipline's Houston operations was still a draft on November 18th.
