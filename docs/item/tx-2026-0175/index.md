# The National Science Foundation puts Texas Southern University in charge of one of five regional nodes in a new HBCU network for AI and quantum science

The National Science Foundation made a cooperative agreement to Texas Southern University in Houston for the HBCU AI and Quantum Innovation Network. The award record carries an award date of September 17th, 2026 and a start date of October 1st, 2026. Clark Atlanta University leads the network. The work runs through five regional nodes and Texas Southern leads one of them. Daniel Vrinceanu is the principal investigator. The stated work is identifying institutional strengths and needs. It also covers regional meetings and workshops. It covers shared training resources and pathways for students and faculty into these fields. The award sits in the foundation's Directorate for Technology, Innovation, and Partnerships.
