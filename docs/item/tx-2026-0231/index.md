# A UT Austin dance and engineering team wins an award to build an AI model that learns from recorded human movement

The University of Texas at Austin says two of its researchers received a $643,719 research award to develop an AI driven movement language model. They are Sinclair Emoghene of the Department of Theatre and Dance and Lillian Chin of the Chandra Family Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. Sensors built in Chin's lab capture movement, touch, force and pressure from recordings of dancers. The team says it has processed 155 dance recordings from 33 countries and plans to reach 500-plus recordings from more than 40 countries. The university's item does not name the funder, and no result from the model has been reported.
