# The Army funds a Rice research center on antenna arrays that carries AI as one of six disciplines

The United States Army Research Office established a five year research center at Rice University for secure sensing, imaging and communications. Edward Knightly of the electrical and computer engineering department leads it. The center studies extremely large antenna arrays. It lists artificial intelligence alongside wireless networking, antennas, radar, circuits and physics. The release describes no specific AI method. What the models will actually do inside the work is not yet on the public record.
