# Rice University sets out to hire 40 more AI faculty, for 75 this decade, and to take lab space at The Arc in Houston's Ion District

Rice University announced on October 13th, 2026 a universitywide AI initiative. It has hired 35 AI focused faculty in recent years and plans 40 more for a total of 75 this decade. Rice will be a lead tenant in The Arc, a research hub in Houston's Ion District. The Arc is scheduled to begin construction this fall and will hold a planned AI Foundry. A Master of Artificial Intelligence program launches in fall 2027. The release names no budget.
