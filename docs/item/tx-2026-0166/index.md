# A three campus Texas trial tests whether a phone microphone held to the abdomen can count fetal movement by machine

A feasibility trial registered on the federal clinical trials registry is recruiting pregnant patients at three Texas academic campuses. The lead sponsor is a professor of women's health at the University of Texas at Austin. Participants place a smartphone microphone on the abdominal wall and the recorded audio is assessed by a machine rather than by the patient's own sense of movement. The registered design is a prospective observational feasibility study of 60 patients drawn from both low risk and high risk pregnancies. What it measures is whether the application is usable rather than whether it prevents any loss. The registration reports an earlier pilot of 205 patients in which the machine assessment outperformed maternal perception against simultaneous ultrasound. The record gives a study start of August 6th, 2026 and was last updated on September 14th, 2026.
