# Aurora tells shareholders its driverless trucks will run straight to customer sites in November, with its truck build about six weeks behind plan

Aurora Innovation filed its third quarter letter with the SEC on October 28th, 2026. It says the Aurora Driver will run directly to sites for several driverless customers in November. The letter says that includes automated navigation through security checkpoints. It says the system can now determine on its own whether a weigh station is open and navigate to an available scale lane. The same letter says its production ramp is about six weeks behind its prior plan. Material availability, component quality and problems found in end-of-line testing held it back. Aurora says Charger Logistics plans to start its trucks between Dallas and Laredo under a non-binding MOU, with delivery expected in 2027 and 2028. Its hardware partner AUMOVIO is preparing a production line in New Braunfels.
