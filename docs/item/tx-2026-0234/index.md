# Plano's City Council takes up to $390,000 for ChatGPT Enterprise and the Darwin AI platform

Plano's City Council carried a citywide AI purchase on the consent agenda of its October 12th meeting, and the meeting's draft minutes mark it approved. It buys ChatGPT Enterprise from Carahsoft for $190,000 and the Darwin AI platform for $150,000. It adds $10,000 of Microsoft licenses and holds $40,000 for growth, inside a ceiling of $390,000. Darwin AI says its platform tracks AI use across users, tools and departments and keeps an audit trail. Every line was bought through a state DIR contract.
