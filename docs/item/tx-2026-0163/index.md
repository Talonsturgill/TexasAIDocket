# The National Science Foundation funds a UT Austin center to put assistive robots into occupied homes, workplaces and care facilities

The National Science Foundation has made a Science and Technology Center award to the University of Texas at Austin for the Center for Human and Robot Co-Adaptation. Joydeep Biswas is the principal investigator. The award record runs from September 1st, 2026 to August 31st, 2031. The center's stated purpose is to work out how robots and the people around them adapt to each other over long periods. The aim is that assistive and service robots stay safe and aligned with what people actually want. The award abstract commits the work to a network of always on living laboratories in residential, commercial and healthcare buildings rather than to laboratory testing alone. The abstract says those deployments will grow more complex over the life of the center. The abstract describes the facility network as a thing to be built and the award clock started this month.
