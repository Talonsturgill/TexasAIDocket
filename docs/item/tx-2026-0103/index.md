# Four Texas campuses took roles in the Energy Department's AI for science program

The Department of Energy announced the recipients of its Genesis Mission in Washington on July 22nd, 2026. UT Austin leads two projects and partners on three more. UT Arlington leads a project on keeping AI driven experiment workflows trustworthy at large scientific facilities. Texas A&M announced its own participation five days later. The program routes federal science money through an explicitly AI first frame, so Texas campuses are competing inside that frame rather than around it. The size of the round is the universities' own restatement of the announcement rather than a figure the department published where it could be read.
