# The state's checklist for a private school taking Education Freedom Account money has four requirements and none of them asks what teaches the child

The Texas Education Freedom Accounts program publishes exactly four requirements a private school must meet to take state money on a student's behalf. It must be accredited. It must have run a campus for at least two years. It must administer a nationally norm referenced assessment. It must be located in Texas or keep a Texas office if it is virtual. None of the four concerns curriculum, instructional method, or who or what delivers the teaching. The private school application period opened December 9th, 2025 and applications are taken on a rolling basis. Alpha School says on its own front page that an AI tutor gives students their coursework and that the adults in the room become Guides. It names campuses in ten Texas cities.

> Be a proven operator that has successfully run a campus for at least two years.
