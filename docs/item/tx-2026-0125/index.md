# Frontera's queues close permanently on October 1st, 2026

The Texas Advanced Computing Center's Frontera user guide carries a warning. The queues close permanently on October 1st, 2026 after seven years of running. Users will still reach Frontera's file systems to transfer data off. Frontera is funded by the National Science Foundation through award 1818253 and the guide calls it the largest cluster dedicated to open science in the United States. The successor is Horizon. It is funded through the Leadership Class Computing Facility award. Horizon's own guide carries a notice dated in July saying it is still limited only to internal users. That guide also says it is in progress. Horizon lists three ways in. They are the facility's own allocations and the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource Pilot and the state's allocation system.
