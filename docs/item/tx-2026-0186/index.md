# FAA takes comment until October 11th on letting Zipline fly drone deliveries around the clock over five Texas metros

The Federal Aviation Administration has published a draft environmental assessment of Zipline International's request to begin commercial drone package delivery in Amarillo, Austin, El Paso, Houston and San Antonio. Approving the change to the company's air carrier operating approvals is the federal action under review. The draft assesses up to 220 chargers, 4,400 pickup dropboxes and 220,000 deliveries a day across the five areas, flown around the clock at about 330 feet above the ground. Routes are generated by software. At each stop a pod lowers on a line and evaluates the drop spot automatically. When the pod can't identify the target and judge the spot itself, an image is sent to an operator. The draft finds no significant noise impact. Comments are open until October 11th, 2026.
