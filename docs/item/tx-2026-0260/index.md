# UTMB Health uses AI to read radiology reports and flag lung nodules for follow up, and most of its program's nodules came from scans taken for other reasons

UTMB Health says AI now helps run the Comprehensive Lung Cancer Screening Program it set up in 2016. The current system was built with the company Bunkerhill and reviews radiology reports for patients getting lung cancer screening exams. Any nodule found on a scan that includes the lungs is flagged for a patient navigator, and a dashboard ranks patients by a validated lung cancer risk score. The program's medical director says the old manual process sometimes took weeks or months. About two thirds of the nodules the program has identified were in patients who were not being screened for lung cancer. The account gives no accuracy figures and no count of patients. The medical director calls AI an assistant that makes no decisions.
