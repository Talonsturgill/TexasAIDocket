# A Houston oil producer buys into an AI vendor and commits its Ward County operations to the vendor's system

Battalion Oil Corporation announced on September 8th, 2026 an equity investment in Collide Industrial Technologies and a Strategic Partner designation. Under an executed subscription agreement the producer will deploy the vendor's operations system across its upstream operations. The company says the deployment begins by unifying more than 100 terabytes of well files, land records, contracts and production history into one queryable model. The company says the work environment is expected to reach its production engineers at approximately day 90. The company names its Monument Draw cube development program in Ward County as the first fit. Every figure and every result in the announcement is the company's own and none of it has been through a regulator. The release names no client and no measured figure behind its claim of proven results.
