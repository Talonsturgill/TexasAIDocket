# Hays County posts the sheriff's promise not to switch on AI report writing without coming back to the court

The Hays County Commissioners Court agenda for September 1st, 2026 carried an item acknowledging that the Hays County Sheriff's Office had committed not to activate or implement Axon Draft One. The agenda describes that product as a report writing feature inside a vendor platform. The commitment reaches any new AI based feature the same platform may later offer under the related contract. What makes the item unusual is the condition rather than the refusal. Operational use requires returning to this court for approval, so the decision stays with the elected body rather than with the office holding the contract. The vendor's own product page describes the feature as drafting a report narrative from body worn camera audio. That page says an officer has to review and approve one before it can be submitted. Local reporting says the court voted three to two on September 15th to move the money it had set aside for a possible Axon contract. The money went to two more corrections officer positions. The county's own minutes of that vote are not yet published.
