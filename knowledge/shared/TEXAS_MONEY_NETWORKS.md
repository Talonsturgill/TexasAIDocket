## 1. The transparency asymmetry, which is the real structural finding

> **The money people want attached to their name goes to PACs. The rest goes through Austin and
> Fort Worth nonprofits.**

- **Texas state PACs disclose everything**, which is why Dunn and Wilks are traceable to the
  dollar. **The Texas state PAC layer is the most transparent layer in this entire map**, and the
  TEC bulk files make it queryable.
- **The (c)(4) and (c)(3) layer above it discloses nothing**, lawfully, and that is where
  **96.5 percent of TPPF's $28.5 million lives.**
- **The federal 527 system is nearly blind to these men.** Across every itemized Form 8872 ever
  filed: **Tim Dunn's total federally-disclosed 527 giving is about $296,000. Farris Wilks:
  $5,800. Jo Ann Wilks: $6,300. Dan Wilks: none found.** `[DOC]`

**Set that against $69,054,038 in TEC-disclosed Texas giving. The federal database captures roughly
0.4 percent of what these two families actually spend on politics.** Anyone researching them
through federal sources alone will miss essentially all of it. **That is the single most useful
methodological fact in this document.**

## 7. The darkness report, quantified

**7.1 TPPF: 96.5 percent of its money has no public source.** FY2024 contributions **$28,489,353**.
Grants documentable by name from other organizations' Schedule I filings that year total
**$995,500**. **That leaves $27,493,853, or 96.5 percent, with no publicly attributable donor**
`[DOC]`. *Legal basis: a 501(c)(3)'s Schedule B contributor list is redacted from the public
inspection copy under 26 CFR 301.6104(d)-1. **The gap is lawful and structural, not an oversight.***

**7.2 Empower Texans: 100 percent of about $12.19 million, never disclosed.** Revenue across FY2012
to FY2023 sums to roughly $12,191,793, essentially all contributions. **A 501(c)(4) discloses no
donors at all. The only reason we know Tim Dunn is behind it is that the IRS Business Master File
carries his name as the care-of contact and Part VII lists him as Chairman** `[DOC]`.

**7.3 The 85 Fund: $143,050,000 in one year, zero donors, Texas address** `[DOC]`.

**7.4 Where the PAC layer goes dark, and where it does not.** All four Dunn-network PACs filed IRS
Form 8871 electing **qualified state political organization** status, and **all four have exactly
ZERO Form 8872 contribution records** `[DOC]`. Their federal disclosure is empty by design under
IRC 527(e)(5) and 527(j)(5). **The good news for reporting: that disclosure moves to the Texas
Ethics Commission, and TEC works.** That is why every dollar above could be named.
