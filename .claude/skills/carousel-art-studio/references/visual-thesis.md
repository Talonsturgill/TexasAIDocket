# Visual thesis — from record to image

## Start with a claim card

Write this before metaphor or style:

```text
CLAIM
One sentence:
Source / locator:
Place and time:
Known quantities:
Unknown or withheld:
What the image may assert:
What the image must not imply:
```

The last two lines are art direction. A line between two nodes asserts relation. A larger form
asserts magnitude or importance. A map anchor asserts place. A broken object asserts damage.
An empty chair asserts absence. If the record does not support the sentence, the geometry may
not say it.

## Find the story's native material

List the actual nouns and verbs in the record before reaching for abstract graphics:

- nouns: site, machine, filing, material, landscape, institution, network, measurement;
- verbs: accumulate, split, queue, harden, migrate, expose, withhold, overlap, accelerate;
- forces: pressure, delay, heat, friction, scarcity, distance, load, uncertainty;
- scales: microscopic, human, architectural, regional, systemic, temporal.

The strongest world usually joins one literal anchor with one explanatory transformation. A
literal transformer may contain a field of queued records. A filing may become architectural
space. The literal anchor keeps the metaphor accountable; the transformation makes it art.

## Thesis test

Use this sentence:

> The record becomes **[world/object/system]**, seen from **[vantage]**, where **[verified
> tension]** appears as **[behavior]**, illuminated by **[motivated source]**.

It passes only if each blank changes the pixels. “A cinematic data landscape showing change”
fails: no object, camera, behavior or source has been chosen.

Then write:

- **memory sentence:** what someone can concretely describe tomorrow;
- **literal anchor:** what prevents the image floating free of the story;
- **evidence behavior:** which geometry carries verified information;
- **metaphor boundary:** what is illustrative and cannot be read quantitatively;
- **anti-direction:** the obvious treatment this will refuse.

## Transformation ladder

If the first idea is generic, move one rung at a time:

1. **depict** — show the real noun;
2. **reframe** — choose a consequential vantage or crop;
3. **expose** — reveal internal structure, time or hidden relation;
4. **materialize** — give an abstract process physical behavior;
5. **systematize** — let verified data drive form;
6. **stage** — use light, scale and sequence to create the visual event.

Do not skip directly to spectacle. Every rung must preserve the truth of the previous one.

## Three concept families worth testing

These are prompts for divergence, not reusable styles:

- **Evidence object:** one story-native object becomes monumental; record fragments, measured
  geometry or institutional marks are physically integrated into it.
- **Impossible evidence camera:** a truthful vantage no physical camera can occupy — cutaway,
  multi-scale section, time stack — reveals the relation the article explains.
- **Behavioral field:** a deterministic system enacts the verified process; a literal anchor,
  legend and metaphor boundary keep it honest.

If all three yield the same centered composition, divergence has not happened.
