# Review gates — ambition before polish

Run these on actual renders, not the treatment.

## Gate 1 — truth

- Is each data-bearing position, size, direction and relation sourced?
- Are modeled and illustrative layers identified?
- Does metaphor imply damage, causation, geography or magnitude the record does not support?
- Is source matter represented faithfully rather than fabricated for texture?

Any failure is a veto.

## Gate 2 — feed stop

At 432 pixels, with type hidden for one pass:

- Can the dominant silhouette be named instantly?
- Is there a real light/dark event?
- Does the crop create energy or consequence?
- Is the focal event still larger than furniture and detail?

If the frame only becomes interesting after reading, return to composition.

## Gate 3 — specificity and memory

- Could another Texas technology or policy article wear this image unchanged?
- Can a reviewer describe it tomorrow with a concrete noun and verb?
- Does the story's own material world determine palette, marks and light?
- Is the direction visibly distinct from the recent-deck ledger?

Generic competence is a failure at this gate.

## Gate 4 — space, light and material

- Can the camera, horizon and depth order be named?
- Do overlaps, occlusion and scale agree with that camera?
- Does a motivated key reveal form, with fill preserving evidence?
- Does every grounded object have a lit ground and readable contact?
- Do adjacent materials differ by response rather than color alone?

## Gate 5 — type and evidence

- Does type have a spatial role or a genuinely quiet caption role?
- Does art still work when type is hidden, and does the slide still communicate when art is
  temporarily hidden?
- Are labels near the thing they explain without turning the frame into a dashboard?
- Is body text optional to the visual turn and readable at feed size?

## Gate 6 — sequence

- Do scale, density, value, vantage and tempo change intentionally?
- Does every slide have a different visual sentence inside the same world?
- Is there pressure and release rather than nine simultaneous peaks?
- Do at least two interior slides equal the cover's craft, rather than merely carry exposition?

## Gate 7 — finish

- Does microdetail reward full-size viewing without muddying the thumbnail?
- Do atmosphere and grade unify rather than conceal?
- Is the image optically coherent — one world, one camera, one light logic?
- Are there any “AI luxury” tells: gratuitous glow, generic gold, translucent cards, fake
  precision, ornamental data, default serif seriousness?

Only after these pass should machine QA and panel review begin. Machine green means safe;
panel green means accepted; neither replaces the studio's responsibility to make exceptional
work.
