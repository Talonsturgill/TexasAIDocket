# LinkedIn-native carousel direction

## The real canvas is 432 pixels wide

The source is 1080×1350, but the first judgment happens around 432 pixels wide in a moving
phone feed. Inspect both. At feed size:

- one image event must dominate;
- headline shape should read before every word does;
- body copy must be optional to understanding the visual turn;
- thin rules, fine particles and subtle value differences need intentional survival tests;
- the outer UI-safe band cannot contain the only evidence or focal detail.

Hide type during one review. If nothing remains, the artwork is a background. Hide art during
another. If the complete slide remains, the art is decoration.

## One visual sentence per slide

Write each as a verb:

- the queue **compresses**;
- the boundary **moves**;
- the filing **opens** to reveal the omitted layer;
- the network **narrows** at one verified point;
- the material **changes state** across a documented threshold.

“Shows three facts” is not a visual sentence. Choose the relation that advances the swipe.

## Swipe choreography

The right edge is an invitation, not mandatory furniture. Create continuation with cropped
motion, interrupted geometry, a question in the image or a changed state the reader wants to
resolve. Do not add arrows to every slide.

Use the deck as time:

- reveal hidden layers across frames;
- carry one object through changes of scale or state;
- reverse camera to expose consequence;
- let a system accumulate, then interrupt it;
- repeat one anchor while changing everything it touches.

The frames should not all peak at once. A quiet frame earns attention through precision,
absence or scale, then makes the next dense frame feel larger.

## Publication furniture

Counters, source cues and the Docket mark create continuity. Keep their grid stable enough to
orient without turning the artwork into a template. Furniture should recede until needed. It
must never be the most intricate system on a slide.

## Cover contract

The cover has one job: cause a qualified reader to stop and open the document. It needs:

- a four-to-seven-word hook when the story allows;
- one ownable silhouette or spatial event;
- a literal connection to the record;
- a clear value hierarchy under platform compression;
- enough unexplained tension to invite the next frame, without clickbait or ambiguity about
  the subject.

Do not make the cover summarize the article. Make it open a visual question the deck can
answer.

## Learning after publication

Record the shipped construction, material world, camera grammar and cover silhouette along
with available document/post analytics. Compare families over multiple posts. Saves, shares,
qualified comments and document engagement are useful signals; none alone defines artistic
quality. A repeated construction that performs is a franchise decision, not automatic license
to use it daily.
